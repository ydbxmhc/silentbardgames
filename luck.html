Core Mechanics

Luck Tokens

Luck Tokens are a metacurrency to alter events in the story. Every session you begin with a number of Luck Tokens equal to your base Level. These can be spent for several things:

  • Use one as Plot Armor to have your armor take ALL the damage from a hit (see Gear). This "best possible result" is usually the value of your EL, but it doesn't change what success means — it just makes it less; just remember that it doesn't change what success means.
  • Use one to absorb and negate a point of Harm you are about to take — sometimes Luck can be better than armor.
  • Use one to power a Maneuver; most Maneuvers don't need it, but some are so strong they require Luck Tokens (more powerful spirits will want actual Karma; weak ones will be happy with booze, incense, etc.).

Characters get one Token at the start of every scene if they have room, but can't have more than their Level. The PC (whichever is more) gets one Token per scene. The GM also starts the session with Tokens equal to the number of players, or as many as the highest Level PC (whichever is more), and also gets one Token per scene. The GM may use them on behalf of the opposition on any test.
