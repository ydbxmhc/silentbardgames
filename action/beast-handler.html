A Beast Handler In Action

Old Henrik uses an unconventional recruitment method.

Level 3, Beast Handler 3, Naturalist 2, Warrior 2.

Standing at the edge of Thornwick village, Henrik studies the claws marks the baker's door has recorded. Not a mindless monster, but something intelligent, curious. On the Thornwick Village Elder's request, he had gone to the well. That evening he treats the beast and sets camp upwind of the creature's lair, building a small fire and settling into patient observation.

After three nights the barghest — larger than any natural wolf, with too-intelligent eyes — eventually approaches the fire. It creeps into the firelight, considering. Three nights of careful presence without threat have started to shift the creature's wariness.

At Level 3 + Beast Handler 3 + Exotic Beasts 1 = EL7, Henrik rolls 3d[0-2] getting (2,1,1) for a 4 against the barghest's innate Hook of Betrayed by Humans, Approaches Warily which the GM rates at a predictable 5. Henrik succeeds by 1.

The barghest brings its head close, and Henrik sits very still, hand slightly extended, not reaching. He holds the posture for a long time until finally the barghest leans forward and sniffs his hand.

Over the following weeks he introduces Shadow to the villagers, and clothing from virtually all of them. Over the following weeks he introduces Shadow to the villagers, and Shadow creeps up on first the children, then the adults. The old man likes the children; he's curious but not hungry, and not dangerous — yet. He started treating him with respect and in a short time Shadow was licking their faces. He pets Shadow with a scratch behind the ears, and Shadow parks his head and cocks his ears to the terrified man.

"Can't," the man says. "Can't. It will eat us." Henrik just shakes his head.

Six months later, Shadow hasn't touched a single predator, and no bandit dares approach those farms. His methods don't work on every dangerous creature — some are too corrupted or too feral — but when they succeed, they create solutions that last for years.

At Level 3 + Beast Handler 3 + Exotic Beasts 1 = EL9, Henrik rolls a 5 to establish a Barghest Territorial Bond Persistent Boost. Shadow will roll against the GM's difficulty to guard this territory for the rest of the campaign — as long as Henrik keeps visiting.
