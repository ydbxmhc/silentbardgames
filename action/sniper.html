A Sniper In Action

Vera sends a message to the Cartel.

Vera — Level 3, Sniper 3, Bows 3, Urban Hunter 1

Vera has positioned herself on a rooftop overlooking the market square. Her target is surrounded by bodyguards and innocent civilians — this shot requires professional distance.

The "merchant" (Level 2, Performer 3) is unaware of her. His bodyguards are scanning the crowd, not the rooftops. Vera has a clear line of sight between the stalls, there's a minor complication from swaying laundry, and wind conditions will impose penalties.

She takes her time — this separates professionals from amateurs. Between the crowds, there's a brief window. She watches for patterns in the movement; too much chaos will interfere with the shot.

Setting Up the Shot

She wagers 4 points on an Aim 5 Boost.

She is now Level 3 + Sniper 3 + Bows 3 + Urban Hunter 1 + Aim 5 = EL15.

The GM adjusts with EL15 – Range 3 – Wind 2 = EL10. She rolls a 7.

No matter — she's good enough for this. She nocks and draws an arrow, accounting for crosswind and the swaying laundry, slowly exhaling, lining up her shot.

The merchant has no warning, no armor, no defense. The arrow takes him cleanly through the heart. He drops instantly, and Vera is already moving to her extraction route before his bodyguards even realize what happened. His cartel will think twice before trying to sell their poison in her city again.

Resistance Roll

He gets a basic resistance roll using just his Level 2. He rolls a 0 and drops.

Professional. Clean. Precise.
