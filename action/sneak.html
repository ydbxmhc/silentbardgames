A Sneak In Action

Whisper demonstrates lethal planning.

Whisper is Level 3, Sneak 3 with Social Camouflage 3, Healer 1 and Performer 1 with Misdirection 1.

Contracted to eliminate an enemy spy at a crowded royal feast, Whisper must kill a man while surrounded by hundreds of witnesses without anyone realizing a murder has occurred. She has acquired a rare poison, and brews it into a subtle and deadly syrup.

This is a Sneak example, not Healer, so we summarize: the GM rolls it with her, and in the end she succeeds and trains one of her many teapots with a social roll.

At the feast she prepares steaming, aromatic coffee for the guests, and fills both sides of her assassin's teapot. She makes the rounds, filling several cups before and after her target with the excellent coffee in the safe side of the pot. When she fills his cup, she uses the poisoned side. All she has to do is draw no special attention to herself. She knows the target is not expecting trouble, and has allowed himself a few glasses of wine. He does not notice her shifting her grip on the pot as she pours his coffee from the poisoned reservoir.

All her skills apply, so she is Level 3 + Sneak 3 + Social Camouflage 3 + Performer 1 + Misdirection 1 = EL11.

The enemy spy is Level 4 + Performer 2 + Cat's Eye 2 = EL8; the GM gives him a Tipsy −2 Hindrance, for EL6. Without expecting trouble, his Performer Maneuvers aren't applicable. The GM rolls his base predictable 5.

Whisper is the predator here, a little nervous with a 7 — she rolls 2d[0-2] Extra Effect dice for 1d[0-1]; rolling 7 against his predictable 5 for a win by 2.

He thanks her and sips as she flirts up the baroness across the room for several minutes. By the time he eventually begins coughing his chest, Whisper is already back in the kitchen, just another forgettable servant washing a teapot.
