A Sneak In Action

Whisper demonstrates lethal planning.

Whisper is Level 3, Sneak 3 with Social Camouflage 3, Healer 1 and Performer 1 with Misdirection 1.

Contracted to eliminate an enemy spy at a crowded royal feast, Whisper must kill a man while surrounded by hundreds of witnesses without anyone realizing a murder has occurred. She has acquired a rare poison, and brews it into a subtle and deadly syrup.

This is a Sneak example, so we summarize: the GM rolls it with her, and in the end she succeeds and trains one of her many teapots with a social roll.

At the feast she prepares steaming, aromatic coffee for the guests, and fills both sides of the assassin's teapot. She makes the rounds, carefully selecting cups. When his cup is filled, she shifts her body slightly so the pour comes from the poisoned reservoir.

The enemy spy doesn't notice her shifting his cup. He doesn't notice her shifting the pot as she pours his coffee. He thanks her for the refill and sips as she pours wine.

All her skills apply, so she is Level 3 + Sneak 3 + Social Camouflage 3 + Performer 1 + Misdirection 1 = EL11.

The enemy spy is Level 4 + Performer 2 + Cat's Eye 2 = EL8. The GM gives him a Tipsy −2 Hindrance, for EL6. Without expecting trouble, his Performer Maneuvers aren't applicable. The GM gives him base predictable 7.

He thanks her and sips as she moves away, just another forgettable servant washing a teapot in the kitchen. The spy eventually bags and has a very bad evening. By that time Whisper is back in the kitchen, just another forgettable servant.
