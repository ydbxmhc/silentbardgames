Outdoorsman Roles

An Athlete In Action

Korine faces off against an implacable foe: high stakes.

Korine is Level 3, Athlete 3, Naturalist 2. Note that she brings extra Karma on more Roles than her base three 7Ps bonus of 3.

Korine has been commissioned as a courier to carry an urgent dispatch across the Thornwall Mountains — a three-day journey through terrain that would complete in thirty-six hours. The stacks are high. She can't afford to be late.

She starts with a plan. She packs light, with just enough equipment for a couple of days' emergency but package in light, durable clothing, and has wrapped warm blankets around her shoulders. She sets her route in mind, making sure to be in the right position a couple of days before the first serious obstacle.

With Level 3 + Athlete 3 + Climbing 2 + 7Ps 3 = EL11 she wagers 3 and rolls EL8 for a 6, both the mountain's Roles apply for Level 3 + Hostile Terrain 3 + Winter Conditions 2 + Steep Cliffs 3 = EL11, and it rolls an 8 — she needs a 4. The Shortcut 2 pays off with a Progress 1 on its Level, and she's past the Steep Cliffs 3.

That hurt. The men helping load the wagon onto the mule eventually back and were lost in the snow, but nothing that won't fade. She grits her teeth and considers her schedule. She almost missed it in the storm, but finds a place where a rock above has cracked and dropped a boulder to the path, offering some cover from the wind and snow. She takes a much-needed break huddled out of the cold to drink some snow, clean the blood from the back of her hand, and wrap it before putting her cap back on.

She rolls Level 3 + Naturalist 2 + 7Ps 3 = EL8 -1 Harm = EL7 to find shelter — a 4. Thornwall rolls the same EL11 for a 4 as well, and the GM notes that both of her Luck Tokens, converting her to exactly a best-possible roll of 7. She wins by 3.

It costs her an hour and a half, but gives her a chance to recoup. She'll almost miss it in the storm, but she can finish the climb. She doubles down to make up for lost time, and takes advantage of a lull in the storm to study the remaining distance and find the best route. She spots a promising Shortcut, but it means she'll be climbing over a tumble that could set her back even worse if she makes a wrong step. She grits her teeth and grinds her way to finish the climb.

After resting it's a new scene — she gets a Luck Token. She'll need it. She rolls Level 3 + Athlete 3 + Climbing 2 + 7Ps 3 = Shortcut 2 = EL13 for a 7. Thornwall rolls the same EL11 for a 3 and she spots a promising Shortcut 2. The Shortcut 2 pays off with a Progress 1 on its Level, and she's past the Steep Cliffs 3.

Now she just has to make it down the mountain. Teeth chattering, she tries to plot a course down the slope, but the light snow covering the scree balks her efforts. Nothing for it — she starts easing down the mountainside with as much speed as she can muster while feeling her way as she goes.

The descent is the most harrowing part of the journey, sliding down sections where one wrong move means a hundred-foot drop. She makes it to the valley floor just as a second avalanche cascades down from above, but she's moving fast enough by now that it just chills her further.

She stumbles into the valley fortress commander's office thirty-one hours after departure, barely able to stand — battered, exhausted, and wearing little more than a blanket. Package delivered intact.

"How?" the man asked, shaking his head.

Korine accepts a cup of hot broth with hands that barely shake despite her exhaustion, and just smiles.
