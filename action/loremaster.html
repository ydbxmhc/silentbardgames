A Loremaster In Action

Marta demonstrates the power of village harmony.

Level 2, Loremaster 2, Performer 1.

Marta Brightquill serves as Millhaven's unofficial matchmaker, historian, and keeper of village secrets. She maintains wellworn record books with carefully maintained records with flowing handwriting, tracking family connections, personality matches, and the complex web of relationships and romantic disasters that make a small community tick.

She decides to focus her expertise on a particularly thorny situation: three potential romantic mismatches that could destabilize the village's social harmony if they develop badly.

The Rosalie Gambit

Marta starts with careful intelligence gathering. She banks 2 points for Strategic Thinking throughout the day.

With Level 2 + Loremaster 2 + Genealogy 2 + Local Customs 2 = EL8 she maps out family connections, personality matches, and social obstacles. Rolling 3d4: (2,1,3) a 5. She's now EL8 for a nice, safe, obvious success.

She identifies the core problem: William and Rosalie's families are suffering the consequences of a fifteen-year-old property dispute too close to either side to be resolved without outside help. Rosalie dances, and Rosalie isn't interested.

Marta arranges a casual encounter at the market where Thomas is buying flowers — clearly for someone special. She approaches with apparent concern.

"Oh, my, Thomas, those are lovely! Are they for someone special?" When he confirms it, she nods thoughtfully. "How thoughtful of you. Though..." she hesitates delicately. "I couldn't help but notice when I was updating the family records — the Henriks and the smithy folk have had quite a few marriages over the years. Your great-grandmother was Perrine's great-aunt, if I recall correctly. Perhaps a bit too close for comfort?"

Marta uses Level 2 + Loremaster 2 + Performer 1 + Social Mediation 1 = EL8 to present this redirection convincingly. She rolls 3d4: (2,2,0) for a 4 against his stubborn but simple infatuation rated at EL2. He rolls 1d3 for a 2, and her gentle guidance succeeds by 2.

The Elena Solution

Elena presents a different challenge — she's sophisticated enough to see through obvious manipulation, but honest enough to appreciate genuine friendship. Marta invites her for tea, ostensibly to share some research about the village history.

"I imagine it's quite strange, coming home to find everyone treating you like an exotic bird," she says warmly. "I'm still the same person who used to help my aunt knead bread, but now everyone acts like I might vanish in a puff of smoke."

Elena laughs, tension melting away. "It's maddening! I'm still the same person who used to help my aunt knead bread, but now everyone acts like I might vanish in a puff of smoke."

Marta uses Level 2 + Loremaster 2 + Genealogy 2 + Strategic Overview 2 = EL8 to identify which local young man would be confident and intelligent enough to appreciate Elena's growth rather than be intimidated by it. The village's social patterns offer little resistance here — she knows these people too well, and the GM declares it DL0.

Weaving the Web

Over the following weeks, Marta orchestrates a series of apparently coincidental encounters. She mentions to Elena that Marcus has an interesting book from the capital that might interest her. She arranges for Thomas to help Anna with her research for her weaving patterns, and the match proves inspired — Anna finds his earnest, uncomplicated devotion to helping her research utterly charming, while Thomas is fascinated by her patient way of explaining the loom.

The property dispute between Willem and Rosalie's families requires more delicate handling. Marta organizes a community barn-raising where both families contribute labor, gradually thawing the old tension through their shared accomplishment.

At the harvest festival, William finally finds the courage to ask Rosalie to dance, both of them laughing together at the complicated dance figures, bonding over their shared experiences of coming home with broader perspectives.

Three weddings planned to come by spring, and she's already cataloguing who might benefit from her next round of gentle interventions.

Sometimes the most important magic is just knowing people well enough to help them find happiness.
