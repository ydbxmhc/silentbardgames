Tinkers In Action

The Ironwright brothers learn the power of combined expertise.

"Fine!"

"Fine, then!"

The workshop doors slammed simultaneously from opposite ends of the long stone chamber, leaving the space eerily quiet except for the steady drip...drip...drip of Sigmont's malfunctioning apparatus. The younger duergar glared at the offending apparatus with frustrated rage. Six months of work, and this cursed thing leaked at the worst possible moments. The distillation process required perfect seating — even the tiniest air gap could ruin the entire batch. And he was so close! The materials were properly prepared, the timing exactly right. But every time he reached the critical stage...

Drip.

Sigmont snatched up a wrench, drawing back to smash the offending apparatus to scrap, when he noticed Obercit standing in the doorway with arms crossed and a sheepish expression.

"I'm sorry," the older brother muttered. "There, I've said it. Now why aren't you using a proper gasket and constriction band?"

Sigmont returned the wrench slowly. "Because I can't get the tolerances right. It builds too fast, and by the time I notice the leak, the batch is gone."

Obercit approached the apparatus with professional interest. "Not bad work — sloppy," he observed.

Sigmont used Level 3, Tinker 3, Performer 1 to design Obercit's apparatus. Rolling 3d4: (4,4,2) for an 8. At EL9 he wagered 4 points to determine the tolerances, leaving EL5 for success — success by 4, delivering the dedicated effect. The dedicated effect is exactly the information Obercit needs.

"One shot should crack any stone through, but if I get all the tolerances just right the first few strikes, the concentrated essence is so pure and intoxicating that even the proudest sidhe lord couldn't resist its allure."

"To superior engineering cooperation," Obercit finished, examining the precious liquid. "Even the vapors are potent."

Obercit loaded a small quantity into his stone-breaker flask chamber, aimed the device at a granite block, and pulled the trigger with a flair. The granite split perfectly in half — nothing but a dampenss.

"By my beard," Obercit whispered. "It works exactly as described. It's...it's perfect."

They used Level 3 + Tinker 3 + Warrior 1 to design the specialized implement. Rolling 3d4: (4,4,2) again for an 8. The result established that the weapon produces Plot Armor-quality results when used at full charge, but its precision means a Critical Complication on any Extreme Failure.

Three days later, Sigmont's compound completed its first successful full distillation. The result was a cask of crystal clear liquid. They confirmed the precise proportions of ingredients, the exact temperature needed, and the exact sequence of procedures needed to make it every time.

Obercit raised the baker in salute. "To brotherly cooperation."

"To superior engineering," Sigmont agreed. "I have a feeling we are going to make each other very rich."

It didn't take very much to get them drunk.
