Knowledge and Craft Roles

A Healer In Action

Tamoen demonstrates medical expertise under impossible conditions.

Level 1, Healer 1, Adept 1. Naturalist Maneuver: Infectious Medicine 1. Adept Maneuver: Fire Shaper 1.

Tam settled his cloak rack as he fetched the bottle and rummaged in the infirmary tent for another wounded guard stumbled through the tent flap. He sat up rolling tired eyes and set his boots on the floor as another soldier eased the sergeant into the one vacant cot. The man flopped back, arching his back in obvious agony, keeping one swollen boot clear of the floor.

Tam interrupted and silenced the babbling of the soldier who brought him in. His other hurts had healed, but this one person. He examined it quickly. A puncture wound from a few days ago — infected. Not good.

He's Level 1 + Healer 1 = EL2, rolling a 2 — not bad, but the man's Festering Wound 2 Limited him to a single die for a 0 and he seemed to pass out. He assessed 3d2 (0,1,0) for a 1. The man's Battlefield Wound 2 Limited him to a single die for 0 and he appeared to pass out.

"Are you going to torture me?" Tam met his eyes, his expression neutral but not unkind.

"Yes," he said gently. "Know that the price of the blessings of Rikitak is high, but not one moment of your agony will be wasted. All your pain and fear will pay for the best care you can receive, and I will see you healed and back to your family." The man's lip trembled, but he clenched his jaw and nodded, and sat still.

"You are fortunate to have bled so freely, friend — the wound is clean. Keep it that way. Change the dressing daily, and eat well. Don't use this arm unless your life depends on it. Do you understand this contract?" The man's eyes went wide and he tried to swallow but apparently his mouth had gone dry.

"Y-yes, ..." He appeared to be fumbling for how to address the young priest.

"You may call me Honored Flame, or 'Master Tamoen', but titles are not why I am here. Do as I have decreed without fail and you will heal. Now go, and use your good arm to help your comrades who need more care than you. Both of you." He waved the woman with the splinted arm out as well.

He looked at the two men lying on the other cots. The crow cawed at him.

"Be quiet," he scolded quietly. "There's nothing more I can do for them now. They need rest — and so do I." He frowned around the tent, and sat on the cot the soldier had just vacated. "I can't afford to make mistakes."

Critical Complications

He woke when the crow raised a fuss at more men coming through the tent flap. He sat up rolling tired eyes and set his boots on the floor as another soldier eased the sergeant into the one vacant cot. The man flopped back, arching his back in obvious agony, keeping one swollen boot clear of the floor.

Sergeant Doek came to Tamoen a few days ago for an injured foot. His other hurts had healed, but this one persisted. This morning his daily recovery rolled (0,0) — an Extreme failure.

Extreme Recovery Failure! Sergeant Doek came to Tamoen a few days ago for an injured foot. His other hurts had healed, but this one persisted. This morning his daily recovery rolled (0,0,0) — an Extreme failure. The wound has become infected and gone septic.

The sergeant panicked and hit his eye. "My man need me. I—" He grimaced and lost the rest of the sentence.

"Did you use the powder I gave you?" Tam interrupted with sets of tools laid out, needles and fine strong silk thread, strong coffee, a fresh bottle of brandy; he handed the terrified woman a brandy-laced coffee and told her to drink every drop.

Tam set aside just enough to dull the sergeant's edge with a gulp, and then poured the brandy over his hands and the bandage before making another pass.

He used the sedative as a Focus boost for the surgery. At Level 1 + Healer 1 + Infections 1 = EL3 he set aside just one point for the infection to give EL3. He rolled 3d2 (0,1,1) for a 1. The Festering Wound 2 also rolled (0,0) for a 0 to make a change. He succeeded and he slept soundly, giving Tam a Sedated Patient 1 persistent Boost.

He had his operating space Level 1 + Healer 1 + Infections 1 = (= Sedated Patient 1 + Poor Environment 1) = EL3 for the surgery. He rolled 3d2 (2,1,1) for a 4. The Festering Wound 2 rolled (0,0) for a 0 to make a change, and he succeeded in his emergency procedure.

He told his clearly everything he did, what he expected and what was expected of her, and then he did what he said with precision and commitment. She did not flinch. End. There was nothing more to do.

Doek's critical condition had been removed, and was now in the best possible hands for a level 3 or 4 character in the same world. He was now able to provide effective Long-Term Recovery bonuses to not only Doek, but to the entire tent.

A Changed Man

The sergeant now has an Amputee Hook on his character sheet that acts like any other.
