# Tesserae Sortis

Second pass: a *faded fresco* palette - dusty, sun-bleached, low-contrast, no stark color - and **irregular** tesserae (varied tile sizes, slight tilts, soft plaster gaps) rather than a tidy grid. The colour stays in the chrome at a whisper; the cards and real artwork carry the vivid notes.

## What changed

- Palette muted to aged-plaster earth tones; nothing pops.
- Tiles are uneven - different widths, small rotations, gaps.
- Two staggered rows in the band; one broken row in the rules.
- This is procedural CSS/SVG - placeholder for real mosaic art.
