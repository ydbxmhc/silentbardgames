# Three of Clubs

![Three of Clubs](cards/3c.png)

## Heckler's Victory

No, and — Conflict

**Keywords** — *Upright:* a room turned, public failure, the crowd lost · *Inverted:* the mob overreaches, the heckler goes too far

### Upright

The performance failed and the room turned. The heckler has the crowd now. Social no/and at its most pointed.

### Inverted

The room turns on you, but the turning goes too far to hold — the heckler overplays his hand, the crowd's cruelty curdles into second thoughts, and the very public shaming wins you a defender.

### In Play

*Upright.* The hall jeers Pell off the boards while the heckler grins at his handiwork.

*Inverted.* The heckler pushes the jeering until it sours, a man near the front stands up disgusted, and Pell has a champion she never had when they liked her.
