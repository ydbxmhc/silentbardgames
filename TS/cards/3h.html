Three of Hearts

Actor's Roses

Yes, and - Social

Keywords - Upright: applause, warmth, an audience won  ·  Inverted: adulation that smothers, a claim you can't shake

Upright

The crowd threw flowers. The performance landed and then some - warmth and generosity flowing back from an appreciative audience.

Inverted

The crowd loves you a shade too well, and the warmth becomes a claim - an admirer who follows you out, a part you're now expected to play forever, affection that wants paying back.

In Play

Upright. Pell's song brings the whole tavern to its feet and earns a free room for the night.

Inverted. Pell's song wins the room, and the lord at the back now considers her his private minstrel, asked or not.
