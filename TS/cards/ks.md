# King of Spades

![King of Spades](cards/ks.png)

## Artificer's Workaround

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a bound thing opposing, a seam in the making ·
*Inverted:* the flaw opening, the construct failing its maker

### Upright

*Something bound and purposeful is working against you, methodically and
without malice. But somewhere in the mechanism there's an improvised patch, a
jury-rigged solution, a crack in the construct. It won't last. It doesn't need
to.*

What stands against you is built, bound, and doing its job - no malice in it,
no fatigue, no bribe it can hear. Things made with purpose fail differently
than people do: not by weakening but at their seams. And this one has a seam -
a patch, a jury-rig, a place where its maker was in a hurry. You have not
beaten it. You have found where it was never finished.

### Inverted

The seam is giving. Pressed, the patched place spreads: the ward flickers on
its cycle, the binding slackens at the join, the construct hesitates where its
instructions were spliced together. What its maker postponed, you are
collecting. Whether the gap opens wide enough - and whether you are standing
in the right place when it does - is the next draw.

### In Play

*Upright.* The warded door ignores Tamsin's every key - but along the hinge
side she can feel where the binding was re-laid, coarser than the original
work.

*Inverted.* Each dusk the re-laid section dims a half-breath before the rest -
and tonight, in that half-breath, the hinge is only iron.
