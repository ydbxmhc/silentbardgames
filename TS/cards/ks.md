# King of Spades

![King of Spades](cards/ks.png)

## Artificer's Workaround

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a bound foe, a temporary crack, a patch that won't last · *Inverted:* the crack widens, a flaw that frees you

### Upright

Something bound and purposeful is working against you, methodically and without malice. But somewhere in the mechanism there's an improvised patch, a jury-rigged solution, a crack in the construct. It won't last. It doesn't need to.

### Inverted

The thing works against you, but the jury-rigged fault is more than a moment's grace - press it and it gives, the seam in the construct opening into the way through. The next move is prying it wider.

### In Play

*Upright.* The warded door resists Tamsin, but the binding's botched repair leaves a seam she can feel.

*Inverted.* The seam isn't a hairline but the whole flawed sigil, and one push stops the construct that should have held her.
