# Two of Spades

![Two of Spades](cards/2s.png)

## Catapult's Misfire

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a miss with information, range found the hard way ·
*Inverted:* the fluke shot, the wrong target proving right

### Upright

*The mechanism failed but something useful landed in the chaos anyway. Perhaps
not where intended. Perhaps better.*

The mechanism failed and the shot went wrong - no breach, no hit, the target
standing exactly as before. But a launched thing lands somewhere, and where it
lands teaches: the range is bracketed now, the flaw in the rig is found, the
ground it tore up shows something the survey missed. A miss that instructs is
still a miss. The instruction is the consolation, not the victory.

### Inverted

Once in a great while the error outperforms the aim. The skewed shot finds
what nobody was aiming at - and what nobody was aiming at turns out to matter.
Take the gift without trusting it; flukes are not a technique. Exploit what
the accident exposed before the defenders understand their own wall.

### In Play

*Upright.* Sera's ballista jams and the bolt skews into the gatehouse wall -
the gate holds, but the cracked facing maps exactly where the old repair is
weakest.

*Inverted.* The skewed bolt punches through a bricked-up arrow slit into the
munitions stored behind it - she missed the gate, and the wall came down
anyway.
