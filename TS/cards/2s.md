# Two of Spades

![Two of Spades](cards/2s.png)

## Catapult's Misfire

No, but — Labor

**Keywords** — *Upright:* a miss with a lesson, range found, a workable failure · *Inverted:* the wild shot finds a mark, failure with a windfall

### Upright

The mechanism failed but something useful landed in the chaos anyway. Perhaps not where intended. Perhaps better.

### Inverted

The shot goes wrong, but wrong toward something — the stray round meets a target nobody planned, or shows you exactly where the wall is thin. The shot that matters is the next one.

### In Play

*Upright.* Sera's ballista skews; the bolt cracks the gatehouse wall rather than the gate, and she marks the flaw.

*Inverted.* Sera's bolt skews into a powder store no one knew was there — she missed the gate and brought down the wall.
