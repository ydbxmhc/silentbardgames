# Queen of Hearts

![Queen of Hearts](cards/qh.png)

## Enchantress' Boon

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a favor freely granted, grace from another's hand ·
*Inverted:* generosity with a ledger, a benefactor's claim

### Upright

*A gift from someone who didn't have to give it. Charm, grace, and genuine
generosity flowing your direction. Something social blooms unexpectedly. Enjoy
it - these moments are rarer than they look.*

Someone with the power to refuse chooses instead to grant - a favor, an
introduction, a door opened by a hand that did not have to move. Freely given,
but not unremembered. Receive it well: gratitude, expressed plainly and once,
is the whole of the price today.

### Inverted

The gift was real, and so was the giver's arithmetic. What arrived as grace
begins to behave like credit: small reminders, warm requests, the gentle
suggestion that people who receive should be reachable. Nothing so crude as a
demand. You may pay it off early - with a favor of your own choosing, before
one is chosen for you.

### In Play

*Upright.* The marquess hears Vell's case and settles the debt with a word to
her steward - no terms, no paper, done.

*Inverted.* A season later the steward calls again: her ladyship is assembling
witnesses for a delicate matter, and remembers Vell fondly.
