# Three of Spades

![Three of Spades](cards/3s.png)

## Reviewer's Nostalgia

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a poor notice, a stirred memory, an accidental ally · *Inverted:* the memory moves, a critic turned patron

### Upright

The review wasn't favorable, but something in the performance triggered a memory in someone who matters. An unexpected benefit from an accidental angle.

### Inverted

The review stung, but it woke something in someone who counts, and that memory isn't idle now - it stirs, it speaks for you, it becomes the introduction the praise never would.

### In Play

*Upright.* The notice is unkind, yet the old patron in the third row hears a younger voice like Pell's.

*Inverted.* The unkind notice reaches a patron who hears his own beginnings in it, and his letter next morning opens a door no good review could.
