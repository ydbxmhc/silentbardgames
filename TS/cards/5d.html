Five of Diamonds

Wayfarer's Pack

Yes, but — Commercial

Keywords — Upright: provisioned but weighed down, resources at a cost  ·  Inverted: weight that breaks you, supplies that betray

Upright

You have what you need. It's heavy, it costs, and it slows you down. The pack is both resource and burden.

Inverted

You carry what you need, and the carrying is the trouble — too slow on the climb, too loud in the brush, the very stores that should save you making you the one who can't keep pace.

In Play

Upright. Doral shoulders a fortnight of provisions; the straps bite, but the camp will eat.

Inverted. Doral's load leaves him last across the scree when it starts to slide, and the pack that fed the camp may yet bury him.
