# Six of Spades

![Six of Spades](cards/6s.png)

## Hounds' Fault

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the trail lost, the losing-place found · *Inverted:* the fault as signpost, a doubled trail read true

### Upright

*The hunting term for losing the scent. The hounds failed the immediate task but their last heading is information worth following.*

The pack has gone quiet and circling: the scent is lost, and no urging will invent it. But a trail does not vanish arbitrarily - it ends where something changed. Water, stone, a cart, a trick. The fault is a fact about the quarry, and the ground where the hounds mill is the most informative ground on the hunt. The chase has failed; the investigation has just been handed its first clue.

### Inverted

Read rightly, the fault is an arrow. Quarry that breaks its own trail does it with intent, and intent has a direction: the doubling-back, the wading, the cart it hitched a ride on all point somewhere. The hounds cannot follow reasoning - but you can, and casting the pack where the reasoning leads is the next draw.

### In Play

*Upright.* The pack loses the scent at the mill-race and circles, baffled - which tells Edda the man knew the water was here, and came this way on purpose.

*Inverted.* Knew the water, used the water: Edda walks the far bank until the ground softens, and casts the hounds where a wet man would have climbed out.
