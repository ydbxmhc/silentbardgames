# Six of Spades

![Six of Spades](cards/6s.png)

## Hounds' Fault

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a lost scent, a last heading, a lead worth chasing · *Inverted:* the fault was the clue, the quarry found

### Upright

The hunting term for losing the scent. The hounds failed the immediate task but their last heading is information worth following.

### Inverted

The hounds lost it, but where they lost it tells the tale - the break in the trail is itself the sign. Read it and the next cast closes on the quarry; or the scent was never truly gone.

### In Play

*Upright.* The pack circles, baffled, but the spot where they lose the trail is the spot worth searching.

*Inverted.* The pack 'loses' the scent at the river because the quarry doubled back, and that shows Doral exactly where he's gone to ground.
