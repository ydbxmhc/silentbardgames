# Three of Diamonds

![Three of Diamonds](cards/3d.png)

## Mentor's Critique

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* success with notes, an earned lesson, conditional praise · *Inverted:* praise that never satisfies, strings on approval

### Upright

The performance worked. Now here are the notes, the expectations, the required improvements. Success with an invoice of obligations attached.

### Inverted

It worked, but the approval comes leashed to expectation - every win now weighed against a bar that keeps climbing, the critique outlasting the applause.

### In Play

*Upright.* The maestro nods at Pell's recital and hands her a list of all to fix by spring.

*Inverted.* The nod arrives with conditions that multiply, and Pell sees she is performing for one man's approval she will never quite earn.
