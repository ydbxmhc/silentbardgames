# Five of Spades

![Five of Spades](cards/5s.png)

## Herbalist's Antidote

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* harm met with a remedy, time bought, a small mercy · *Inverted:* the stopgap holds, recovery from the brink

### Upright

The situation is bad but here is something specific that addresses the harm. Quiet, practical, bought time if nothing else.

### Inverted

Things went wrong, but the countermeasure is better than a delay - it actually turns the harm, and the buying of time becomes the saving of it. What looked like a holding action was a rescue.

### In Play

*Upright.* The wound festers, but Liss's poultice draws the worst and keeps the ranger walking.

*Inverted.* Liss's poultice doesn't slow the poison, it breaks it, and the man given up for lost is on his feet by dawn.
