# Ten of Diamonds

![Ten of Diamonds](cards/10d.png)

## Razor's Nick

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a clean result, a small toll, precision priced · *Inverted:* a small cut that festers, the minor cost was the main one

### Upright

The tool is sharp, the work is done, and there's a small precise cost attached. Almost incidental. Almost.

### Inverted

The work is done well, but the little price wasn't little - the nick goes bad, the small expense compounds, the trivial thing you paid proves to have been the one that mattered.

### In Play

*Upright.* The blade does its work; Oskar barely notes the bead of blood on his thumb.

*Inverted.* Oskar barely notes the nick, until the thumb stiffens by dusk and the hand he works with won't close by morning.
