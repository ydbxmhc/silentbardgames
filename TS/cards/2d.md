# Two of Diamonds

![Two of Diamonds](cards/2d.png)

## Marksman's Reload

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a hit, then a pause, a cost in tempo · *Inverted:* the gap exploited, no time to reset

### Upright

The shot landed. Now there's a pause, a cost, a moment of vulnerability while the mechanism resets.

### Inverted

The shot lands, but the breath after is where it goes wrong - the half-second of reloading is exactly the opening the other side wanted, and now you answer for it.

### In Play

*Upright.* Sera's bolt drops the lead rider; she reaches for the next and feels the moment cost her.

*Inverted.* Her bolt drops the lead rider, and the two behind close the distance she spent reloading; the next pull decides whether she sees them off.
