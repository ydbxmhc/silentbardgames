# Four of Hearts

![Four of Hearts](cards/4h.png)

## Spy's Package

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a delivery and a bonus, a hidden gift · *Inverted:* a gift that implicates, freight you can't refuse

### Upright

The intelligence arrived and something extra was tucked inside that nobody expected. More than was asked for, delivered cleanly.

### Inverted

Something extra rides along that you never asked for and can't easily hand back - a confidence that makes you keeper of a secret, a kindness that is really a tether.

### In Play

*Upright.* The courier gives Cass the letters and, behind them, a map she never requested.

*Inverted.* Behind the letters is a thing Cass was never meant to hold, and those who lost it will come looking for whoever does.
