# Three of Hearts

![Three of Hearts](cards/3h.png)

## Actor's Roses

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* an audience won, warmth returned, standing gained ·
*Inverted:* adoration with a grip, a role you cannot put down

### Upright

*The crowd threw flowers. The performance landed and then some - warmth and
generosity flowing back from an appreciative audience.*

The performance did what performances are for, and then the room gave
something back. Flowers, coin, a name remembered, an invitation - the applause
has weight this time, and it lands as goodwill you can spend later. Enjoy the
warmth honestly; audiences can tell.

### Inverted

They loved it a little too well. The affection that follows a triumph can
arrive with hands attached: an admirer who does not hear refusals, a patron
who mistakes delight for ownership, a crowd that will want this exact
performance again, and again, and only this one. The love is real. So is the
grip.

### In Play

*Upright.* Pell's ballad brings the taproom to its feet, and the innkeep waves
away her bill - sing again tomorrow and the room is hers all week.

*Inverted.* Pell's ballad conquers the hall, and the baron's invitation
arrives before breakfast - the kind that comes with an escort, to a house
guests are not seen leaving early.
