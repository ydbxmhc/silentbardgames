# Ten of Spades

![Ten of Spades](cards/10s.png)

## Engineer's Salvage

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the build failed, the parts surviving · *Inverted:*
the failure diagnostic, ruin as blueprint

### Upright

*The mechanism failed but the parts are recoverable. Something useful remains
from the wreckage. Practical hope.*

It does not work. Not needs-adjustment - dead, and the hours in it are spent.
Salvage is the discipline that begins now: the gears survive, the frame is
true, the costly parts unseat cleanly. A failed build that yields its parts
has returned most of its price. The design was the loss. The inventory is the
consolation.

### Inverted

Taken apart, the failure explains itself. The cracked casting, the tolerance
that drifted, the joint that was always going to walk - the wreck is a better
teacher than the working model would have been, because it shows exactly where
the lie lived. The next build starts from knowledge the first one lacked.
Start it.

### In Play

*Upright.* The pump seizes past saving; Oskar strips it by lamplight and racks
eight good gears and a true shaft against the next attempt.

*Inverted.* Under the seized housing he finds the culprit - a green casting,
poured too fast at the foundry - and now he knows which supplier's stock to
test before trusting it.
