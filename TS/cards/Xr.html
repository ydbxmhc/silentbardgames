The Deck

Joker (Red)

Chaos with potential

Meaning

Chaos with potential - weird, wonderful, disruptive, and possibly a gift.

In Play

Mid-scene, the red joker lands, and the story's floor opens onto something nobody asked about. A stranger's outrageous offer, a wall where no wall stood yesterday, an inheritance from a name no one recognizes. The other cards in the draw say where the strangeness points - the joker only says that it is here, that it is large, and that it might be wonderful. Walk toward it.

Inverted

The Jokers do not reverse; the two are each other's mirror. Drawing both in one pull is rare enough to feel like the deck itself speaking.
