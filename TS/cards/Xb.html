The Deck

Joker (Black & White)

Chaos with teeth

Meaning

Chaos with teeth - extraordinary and probably dangerous.

In Play

The black joker falls and the temperature drops. Something extraordinary has entered the scene - and every instinct says it is not a gift, or not only a gift. The reversal, the too-generous stranger, the thing in the dark that moved against the wind. Read the other cards for its direction, keep your back to a wall, and remember that wariness is not refusal: you can walk in. Carefully.

Inverted

The Jokers do not reverse; the two are each other's mirror. Drawing both in one pull is rare enough to feel like the deck itself speaking.
