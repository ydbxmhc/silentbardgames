# Seven of Hearts

![Seven of Hearts](cards/7h.png)

## Victor's Laurels

Yes, and — Social

**Keywords** — *Upright:* public triumph, honor, the spoils of winning · *Inverted:* a crown that chafes, envy, a peak to defend

### Upright

Classical and generous. The crowd is cheering and the laurels are real. Everything that comes with public triumph arrives at once.

### Inverted

You wear the laurel and it sits heavy — the honor invites every challenger to test themselves on you, and the higher you stand the longer the fall has to watch.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick wins the games and the town carries him shoulder-high to the feast.

*Inverted.* Garrick wins the games, and by morning every young blade with something to prove knows just where to find him.
