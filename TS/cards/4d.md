# Four of Diamonds

![Four of Diamonds](cards/4d.png)

## Assassin's Compulsion

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the job done, a binding price, bought silence · *Inverted:* a handler who owns you, the next contract isn't optional

### Upright

The job is done. But now you're bound to something - a debt, a secret, a handler who knows what you did. The compulsion doesn't release.

### Inverted

The work is finished, and finishing it locked something round your wrist - a handler who now knows what you'll do, a secret that books your next job before you've agreed to take it.

### In Play

*Upright.* The contract's complete; the broker pays Yorin and pockets the proof of who did it.

*Inverted.* The broker pockets the proof, and it becomes the lever for the next job - Yorin draws to learn whether there's any refusing.
