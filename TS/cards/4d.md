# Four of Diamonds

![Four of Diamonds](cards/4d.png)

## Assassin's Compulsion

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the job closed, a hold retained · *Inverted:* the proof as leash, work that recruits you

### Upright

*The job is done. But now you're bound to something - a debt, a secret, a handler who knows what you did. The compulsion doesn't release.*

The work is done, quietly and completely, and payment clears. But work like this leaves residue by design: the broker keeps the proof, the client keeps the memory, and you keep the skills that made you worth hiring. Something of the job stays attached when you walk away. It always does. That was in the price, whether or not it was in the contract.

### Inverted

The residue has learned to pull. Whoever holds the proof of what you did has begun to spend it: a second job arrives with no fee discussed, only a reminder of the first. This is how the trade keeps its people - not with loyalty but with paperwork. What refusal costs, and whether the hold can be burned - draw, and find out who owns whom.

### In Play

*Upright.* The contract closes clean; the broker pays Cass in full and files the incriminating letter she recovered - insurance, he calls it, smiling.

*Inverted.* A month on, a sealed note names the next mark and no price - only a line quoted from the letter the broker kept.
