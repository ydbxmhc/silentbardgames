# Five of Hearts

![Five of Hearts](cards/5h.png)

## Forager's Bounty

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* abundance, a giving land, surplus · *Inverted:* more than you can keep, a haul that draws scavengers

### Upright

Nature provided more than asked. The land is generous today and something extra came with the harvest.

### Inverted

The land gives past what you can carry or store - the surplus spoils, slows you, or its scent brings something hungry out of the trees to claim its share.

### In Play

*Upright.* Doral's snares are full by dawn, enough to feed the camp for a week.

*Inverted.* Doral's snares are full past carrying, and the blood-smell walks a bear into camp before he can cache a thing.
