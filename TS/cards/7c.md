# Seven of Clubs

![Seven of Clubs](cards/7c.png)

## Dropout's Disgrace

No, and - Conflict

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a public quitting, failure witnessed, lost standing · *Inverted:* disgrace that frees, nothing left to lose

### Upright

Not just losing but the public consequence of failing to finish. The crowd that cheered in 7H is silent or worse here.

### Inverted

You fail in front of everyone and the standing is gone - but a man with nothing left to guard is a dangerous one. The shame burns off what held you back; below the bottom there's no further drop and no one left to impress.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick walks off the field mid-bout, the crowd's contempt trailing him out.

*Inverted.* Garrick walks off in disgrace and keeps walking, out of the contract and the debt and the name that owned him; they can't take what he's already thrown down.
