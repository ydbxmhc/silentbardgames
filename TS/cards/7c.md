# Seven of Clubs

![Seven of Clubs](cards/7c.png)

## Dropout's Disgrace

No, and - Conflict

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the public quit, standing forfeited · *Inverted:* worthlessness as release, discarded and free

### Upright

*Not just losing but the public consequence of failing to finish. The crowd that cheered in 7H is silent or worse here.*

Not losing - stopping, where everyone could see. The crowd's contempt for a beaten fighter is brief; its contempt for a quitter has a long shelf life, and it retails. Whatever reasons lived in your body at that moment, they will not be part of the story told about it. Standing is gone, backers are gone, and the name you built is now a caution recited to others.

### Inverted

Disgrace carries a strange mercy: the disgraced are worthless, and the worthless are released. The men who owned pieces of your fights want nothing owned by a quitter; the rivals stop studying you; the expectations that ran your life pack up and follow the crowd out. What is left is unwatched, unbacked, and - for the first time in years - unclaimed. What a free man does first is suddenly, for once, entirely his own affair.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick drops his hands mid-bout and walks - past the roaring gallery, past his trainer's face - and the odds-makers strike his name by nightfall.

*Inverted.* Struck from the lists, he is struck from the ledgers too: the fixers who scripted his losses have no use for a fighter no one will book, and no reason to watch where he goes.
