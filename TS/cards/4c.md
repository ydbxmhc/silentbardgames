# Four of Clubs

![Four of Clubs](cards/4c.png)

## Burglar's Ligature

No, and - Conflict

**Keywords** - *Upright:* caught and bound, the trap tightening · *Inverted:*
haste in the knots, captors bound to their catch

### Upright

*Caught and bound, and things are actively getting worse. The ligature is
doing real work.*

Caught - and caught by people who prepared for catching. The cords are
professional, the search was thorough, and every struggle so far has only
demonstrated their quality. Worse, being held is not the end state: you are
being held for something - questions, transfer, the arrival of whoever pays.
The situation is deteriorating on a schedule that is not yours.

### Inverted

Thoroughness has blind spots of its own. The trap sprang perfectly, and now
its owners must staff it: watches to keep, transport to arrange, a prisoner
whose feeding and moving is suddenly their problem. Somewhere in their haste
is a cord tied fast instead of well, a guard certain instead of careful.
Captors relax on a rhythm. The knot with give in it, and the moment they look
away - that is the next draw.

### In Play

*Upright.* The warehouse crew truss Cass to a roof-post and send a runner for
the man who pays them - the knots are a sailor's, and the runner is fast.

*Inverted.* Two stay to watch her, then one, then he steps out to relieve
himself - and the cord they tied in a hurry around the post, not her wrists,
has been working up the grain of the wood all evening.
