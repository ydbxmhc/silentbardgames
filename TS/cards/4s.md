# Four of Spades

![Four of Spades](cards/4s.png)

## Prisoner's Map

No, but — Labor

Keywords, inverted reading, and examples are working drafts — pending author review.

**Keywords** — *Upright:* failure with a lead, confinement, a thread, patience · *Inverted:* a lead worth a roll, the real exit, momentum from failure

### Upright

Caught, confined, the attempt failed — you’re not leaving the way you came. But the dark isn’t empty: a scratched mark, a loose stone, a guard’s habit noted. Not freedom — a thread to pull.

### Inverted

The *but* points firmly at the next move. You failed — but you saw something that might work, so follow it into another draw; the lead is real and actionable now. (Or, fully reversed: it’s no thread but a rope — the way out holds, and confinement was only ever temporary.)

### In Play

*Upright.* Thrn’s break fails and he lands in a deeper cell — but over three nights he charts the patrol’s gap.

*Inverted.* The old scratches map a real drain. Draw to see if tonight’s the night, because it might be.
