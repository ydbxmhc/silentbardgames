# Four of Spades

![Four of Spades](cards/4s.png)

## Prisoner's Map

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* confinement with a lead, a thread to pull, patience · *Inverted:* the lead is the exit, momentum out of the cell

### Upright

Failed, confined, seemingly stuck. And yet someone scratched a way out in the dark. It's there if you look.

### Inverted

Failure put you here, but the thing you marked in the dark is real and ready - follow it into the next move; or let it run all the way, and the scratched sign proves a true way out the cell was never going to keep.

### In Play

*Upright.* Thrn's break fails and the new cell is deeper, but three nights of watching give him the patrol's gap.

*Inverted.* The old scratches map a real drain, and whether tonight's the night is the next draw.
