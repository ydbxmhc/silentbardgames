# Six of Hearts

![Six of Hearts](cards/6h.png)

## Ox Team's Calf

Yes, and — Social

**Keywords** — *Upright:* a partnership that grows, a new asset, increase · *Inverted:* a dependent, a mouth to feed, increase you must tend

### Upright

The labor partnership produced something new and valuable beyond the work itself. A future asset arrived unexpectedly.

### Inverted

The partnership makes more than work — and now there's a thing to raise, guard, and answer for, an asset that can't pull its own weight yet and won't for a while.

### In Play

*Upright.* Over the season the plow-team leaves Edda a calf worth more than her wages.

*Inverted.* The calf comes early and sickly, and Edda has a new life to keep breathing in the thick of a hard haul.
