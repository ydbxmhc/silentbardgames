# Eight of Spades

![Eight of Spades](cards/8s.png)

## Hollow Leg

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* no change but no loss, a stubborn survival · *Inverted:* endurance turns the corner, held on long enough

### Upright

The medicine went in and nothing changed. The patient is still upright, still moving, still here. Failed healing that somehow left something intact.

### Inverted

The medicine did nothing, but the patient's refusal to fail did everything - held together by sheer obstinacy long enough for the real cure, or the worst of it, to pass. Lasting was the treatment.

### In Play

*Upright.* Nothing Liss tries moves the fever, yet the smith is still breathing, still arguing, come morning.

*Inverted.* Nothing moves the fever, and the smith outlasts it anyway, walking it off through pure stubbornness while better-tended men go under.
