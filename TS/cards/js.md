# Jack of Spades

![Jack of Spades](cards/js.png)

## Friar's Indulgence

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a reprieve unearned, the no postponed · *Inverted:* the pass holding, through on borrowed grace

### Upright

*You didn't earn this. The ledger says no, the merit says no, and yet here is a pass, slightly corrupt and entirely functional. Don't examine it too closely. Walk through.*

By every rule that applies, the answer is no - and yet here is a gap in the rules, found the way instinct finds things, a half-step ahead of thought. What you get is a reprieve, not a pardon: a season's delay, a look aside, a technicality with your name on it. The no still exists. You just do not face it today, and today was the emergency.

### Inverted

The gap holds further than gaps should. The paper nobody ought to honor keeps being honored; the look aside becomes a habit; the borrowed grace outlasts every checkpoint it was never meant to survive. You are through - genuinely through - on merit you do not have. Somewhere a debt of luck is accruing, and the next draw says nothing about when it bills.

### In Play

*Upright.* The friar cannot absolve Renn's temple-debt - but he can misfile the ledger until spring, and spring is a long way off.

*Inverted.* The misfiled ledger is never found; by summer a new prior has burned the archive's clutter, Renn's page among it, and no one living can name the sum.
