# Eight of Clubs

![Eight of Clubs](cards/8c.png)

## Apothecary's Grief

No, and — Conflict

**Keywords** — *Upright:* a treatment failed, worsening, a healer's anguish · *Inverted:* the loss that teaches, a death that warns

### Upright

The treatment failed and the condition worsened. The professional who understands exactly what went wrong has to live with that knowledge.

### Inverted

The treatment fails and the patient worsens — but the one who grasps exactly why carries that knowing on. The loss is real and so is the lesson bought with it, and the next case is the one this one will save.

### In Play

*Upright.* Nothing in Liss's kit holds the rot; she watches, helpless, knowing the name of what beat her.

*Inverted.* Liss loses the patient, and the manner of it shows her precisely what she missed; the next fevered traveler through her door lives because this one didn't.
