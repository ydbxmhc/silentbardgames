The Deck

Eight of Diamonds

Remedy's Addiction

Yes, but - Commercial

Keywords - Upright: a cure that costs, relief on credit, dependence  ·  Inverted: the cure becomes the ailment, a need that rules you

Upright

The remedy worked. Now you need it and that need has teeth. The success keeps costing in ways that weren't on the label.

Inverted

It worked, and now you can't do without it - the remedy that mended the first trouble is the second one, the relief parceled out by whoever holds the supply.

In Play

Upright. The draught ends Cass's tremors; she notices how soon she wants the next.

Inverted. The draught ends Cass's tremors, and the apothecary who brews it now names her price, certain she cannot say no.
