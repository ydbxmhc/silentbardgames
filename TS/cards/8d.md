# Eight of Diamonds

![Eight of Diamonds](cards/8d.png)

## Remedy's Addiction

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* relief that's real, a cost still sleeping,
tomorrow's tab · *Inverted:* the cure was the bait, a need that rules you, a
supplier who owns you

### Upright

The remedy works, and the relief is real - you got exactly what you needed,
and tonight that is the whole story. The price folded into it is real too, but
it is still sleeping: a seed, a hook set for later, not yet a hand at your
throat.

### Inverted

The relief was the bait. The cure holds, but the needing is the situation
now - the ailment you brought was never the real trouble; the supply is, and
a hand you did not choose rests on the tap. What you fixed stopped mattering
the moment the price did.

### In Play

*Upright.* The draught relieves Cass's agony; she sleeps deeply for the first
time in weeks. The encroaching aches of the morning are, for now, just a
warning for later...

*Inverted.* The draught relieves Cass's agony - the apothecary who gave the
first draught for free now names his price with a sinister smile, certain she
cannot say no.
