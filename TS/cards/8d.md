# Eight of Diamonds

![Eight of Diamonds](cards/8d.png)

## Remedy's Addiction

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* the cure that binds, relief on subscription · *Inverted:* supply as power, a need someone owns

### Upright

*The remedy worked. Now you need it and that need has teeth. The success keeps costing in ways that weren't on the label.*

The remedy works. That is not in question - the pain recedes, the body steadies, life resumes. What follows it home is the wanting: a cure that must be repeated is a cure with a hand on you, and the intervals will speak up when they are due. Effective and habit-forming are not opposites. Here they are the same fact.

### Inverted

The need has an owner now, and it is not you. Whoever compounds, carries, or prescribes the remedy has understood what it is worth to you, and prices are only the politest form that understanding takes. Finding another source, a true cure, or a way to do without - the next draw is which of those is real.

### In Play

*Upright.* Liss's draught ends the magistrate's tremors within the hour; he asks, too casually, how soon he may send for more.

*Inverted.* The apothecary who supplies the base resin doubles her price without explanation - and both women know the magistrate will pay it.
