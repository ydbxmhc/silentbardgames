# Seven of Spades

![Seven of Spades](cards/7s.png)

## Runner's Consolation

No, but — Labor

**Keywords** — *Upright:* not a win but a finish, quiet dignity, ground held · *Inverted:* the finish is the win, last becomes first

### Upright

Didn't win, didn't place, but finished. There's something in that. Quiet dignity in the effort when the result wasn't there.

### Inverted

You didn't place, but finishing was the whole of it this time — the ones who 'won' burned out, dropped, or were struck from the lists, and the steady figure still on its feet is the one who collects.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick comes in dead last, but he comes in, and the road took everyone who pushed harder.

*Inverted.* Garrick comes in last and is the only one to cross at all; the prize falls to the man who simply would not quit.
