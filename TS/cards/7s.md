# Seven of Spades

![Seven of Spades](cards/7s.png)

## Runner's Consolation

No, but - Labor

**Keywords** - *Upright:* finished not placed, the body kept honest ·
*Inverted:* attrition's arithmetic, still standing when it counts

### Upright

*Didn't win, didn't place, but finished. There's something in that. Quiet
dignity in the effort when the result wasn't there.*

No laurel, no purse, no place worth engraving - the race was lost by every
measure the judges keep. What remains is the measure they do not: the distance
was covered, the body answered, and the will held all the way to the line.
Finishing is a currency with no market and real value. It spends later, in
situations that do not resemble races.

### Inverted

The field is thinning in ways the odds never priced. The pace that beat you is
breaking the men who set it; the shortcuts are collecting their tolls; and the
steady finisher is still moving while the winners' bench fills with the
injured and the disqualified. Endurance is quietly becoming position. Whether
the race lasts long enough for position to become victory is the only question
still open.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick crosses dead last to a scattering of polite applause -
sound, upright, and the only entrant who never once walked.

*Inverted.* By the final stage the front-runners have burned out or bought
out - Garrick's steady pace is suddenly third, and two of the men ahead are
limping.
