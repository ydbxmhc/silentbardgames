# Joker (Black & White)

![Joker (Black & White)](https://pub-e0f96c01318c4755b491bf481c530eb1.r2.dev/cards/Xb.png)

## Chaos with teeth

### Meaning

Chaos with teeth - extraordinary and probably dangerous.

### In Play

The black joker falls and the temperature drops. Something extraordinary has
entered the scene - and every instinct says it is not a gift, or not only a
gift. The reversal, the too-generous stranger, the thing in the dark that
moved against the wind. Read the other cards for its direction, keep your back
to a wall, and remember that wariness is not refusal: you can walk in.
Carefully.

### Inverted

The Jokers do not reverse; the two are each other's mirror. Drawing both in
one pull is rare enough to feel like the deck itself speaking.

Notation Xb
