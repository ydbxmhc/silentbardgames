# Ten of Hearts

![Ten of Hearts](cards/10h.png)

## Alchemist's Discovery

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a breakthrough, an unlooked-for boon, invention · *Inverted:* more reaction than intended, a find that won't stay quiet

### Upright

The experiment worked and produced something nobody expected. Intellectual generosity flowing outward from a successful making.

### Inverted

The work succeeds past its bounds - it makes more than you can govern, draws eyes you can't wave off, or proves a thing certain people would rather had stayed unproven.

### In Play

*Upright.* Oskar's tincture doesn't just keep the meat; it clears the camp's lingering cough.

*Inverted.* Oskar's tincture works wonders, and the sick of three valleys are soon on the road to his door, the guild's licensors close behind.
