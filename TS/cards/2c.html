Two of Clubs

Arbalest's Volley

No, and - Conflict

Keywords - Upright: a relentless assault, a grinding advance, no quarter  ·  Inverted: the engine overreaches, the volley spent

Upright

Heavy, mechanical, and it's still coming. The assault didn't stop and it made things considerably worse.

Inverted

The barrage is overwhelming, and it overreaches - the engine throws everything and stands empty, the advance outrunning its own supply. In the lull it can't help but give you, the next move is yours.

In Play

Upright. Bolt after bolt comes over the wall; Cass can't lift her head, let alone answer.

Inverted. The volley comes so hard the crews burn through every quarrel they have, and the silence after is the gap Cass needs.
