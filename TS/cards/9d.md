# Nine of Diamonds

![Nine of Diamonds](cards/9d.png)

## Investigator's Attention

Yes, but - Commercial

**Keywords** - *Upright:* found, but noticed, a watched search · *Inverted:* the watcher acts, what you learned learned you

### Upright

You found what you were looking for. Someone noticed you looking. Professional interest is now aimed in your direction.

### Inverted

You got your answer, and seeking it lit a lamp over your head - and the thing that noticed is no longer content to watch. What you learned matters less than who learned of you.

### In Play

*Upright.* Mab finds the ledger's missing page; across the room, a clerk marks who was reading.

*Inverted.* Mab finds the page, and before she's outside the clerk's master has her description - now the speed of her next move is everything.
