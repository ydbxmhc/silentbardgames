# Nine of Hearts

![Nine of Hearts](cards/9h.png)

## Librarian's Recommendation

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* knowledge that opens onward, a scholar's welcome ·
*Inverted:* drawn in deeper, reading under someone's eye

### Upright

*The research succeeded and the librarian liked you enough to send you
somewhere better. Information that leads to more information.*

The research succeeded, and better: it earned you a guide. Someone who knows
the territory of this knowledge has decided you are worth pointing onward - a
rarer prize than the fact you came for. Doors open in sequence now; the trick
is only to keep asking good questions.

### Inverted

Each answer opens into a hall with another door, and you are further from
daylight than you meant to be. The knowledge is drawing you into its own
politics: whose collection, whose patronage, whose secrets brush against
yours. And somewhere behind you, someone has begun keeping a list of what you
have read.

### In Play

*Upright.* The archivist likes Wynn's questions and writes her an introduction
to the cathedral library, where the real records sleep.

*Inverted.* The introduction works - and the cathedral's librarian logs every
folio she touches, as he logs everyone's, for readers his masters find
interesting.
