# Nine of Hearts

![Nine of Hearts](cards/9h.png)

## Librarian's Recommendation

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* research that opens doors, a lead to more · *Inverted:* a rabbit hole, knowledge that recruits you

### Upright

The research succeeded and the librarian liked you enough to send you somewhere better. Information that leads to more information.

### Inverted

The trail of knowing keeps giving and draws you deeper than you meant to go - each answer a door into someone else's affairs, and those affairs now know your face.

### In Play

*Upright.* The archivist likes Wynn's questions and sends her to a finer collection across the city.

*Inverted.* The archivist sends Wynn somewhere finer - a private library whose owner does not forget who reads his books.
