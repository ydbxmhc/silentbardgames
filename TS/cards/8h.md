# Eight of Hearts

![Eight of Hearts](cards/8h.png)

## Nurse's Handfasting

Yes, and - Social

**Keywords** - *Upright:* a bond forged in care, loyalty earned, kinship chosen · *Inverted:* gratitude become need, a patient who won't be discharged

### Upright

*The healer and patient bound together through the act of care. A tie formed that wasn't there before, warm and lasting.*

Care given wholly has made something that outlasts the wound. Between healer and healed a tie has formed - gratitude, trust, something warmer - and it is the kind of tie people honor for years. Help was the work; this is the harvest. Few things in the deck are as simply good as this card.

### Inverted

The bond healed over the wound and kept growing. What began as gratitude has thickened into reliance: a patient who will not be well unless you are in the room, a rescued soul who has organized their life around their rescuer. Kindness built this, and only kindness can take it apart - slowly, and on purpose.

### In Play

*Upright.* Liss brings the ranger through the fever, and he leaves a standing pledge: her name is good at his lodge, and anywhere his people range.

*Inverted.* The ranger recovers in body and returns to her door with every small ailment after, certain that no other hands can be trusted.
