# Eight of Hearts

![Eight of Hearts](cards/8h.png)

## Nurse's Handfasting

Yes, and — Social

**Keywords** — *Upright:* a bond through care, loyalty, a lasting tie · *Inverted:* a tie that binds, dependence, attachment that complicates

### Upright

The healer and patient bound together through the act of care. A tie formed that wasn't there before, warm and lasting.

### Inverted

Care forges a bond that holds tighter than the moment earned — gratitude thickening into need, a patient who now leans on you for everything, a loyalty you can't fully repay.

### In Play

*Upright.* Liss nurses the wounded ranger through the fever; he wakes calling her family.

*Inverted.* Liss nurses the ranger through the fever, and he wakes unwilling to let her from his sight, sure he owes her every step he takes.
