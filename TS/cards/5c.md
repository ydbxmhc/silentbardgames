# Five of Clubs

![Five of Clubs](cards/5c.png)

## Mountaineer's Avalanche

No, and — Conflict

**Keywords** — *Upright:* an impersonal disaster, an unstoppable force, indifferent ruin · *Inverted:* the slide takes them too, ruin as cover

### Upright

Unstoppable, impersonal, and it absolutely made everything worse. The mountain didn't even notice you.

### Inverted

The mountain comes down on everyone, you among them — but it picks no sides, and what buries you buries your pursuers, erases your trail, seals the pass at your back. A disaster indifferent enough to be a door.

### In Play

*Upright.* The slope lets go above Doral; there's no outrunning it, only being small.

*Inverted.* The slope lets go and takes the whole party behind Doral with it, and when the snow stills, no one left alive knows where he went.
