Seven of Diamonds

Competitor's Fee

Yes, but - Commercial

Keywords - Upright: placed and paid, winnings minus the cut  ·  Inverted: the take eaten alive, backers who own the win

Upright

You placed, you earned. The entry fee, the trainer, the equipment all have their hands out. Success with an immediate practical invoice.

Inverted

You placed, but every open hand gets there before yours - trainer, house, the men who staked you - and the purse that looked like a prize is spent before it's yours.

In Play

Upright. Wynn finishes third, and the winnings just cover what the season cost her.

Inverted. Wynn finishes third, and the men who fronted her stake are waiting at the line to take more than she won.
