Two of Hearts

Scout's Report

Yes, and - Social

2H (Sniper) - clean intelligence, the fuller picture, a watcher's gift

Intelligence delivered cleanly, and more than was asked for. Someone was watching and what they saw benefits you.

Patience on the high ground has paid. The report that comes back is better than what was asked for: not just where, but when, and who, and how often. Somewhere in the extra detail is the lead you did not know to request. Read it twice; the second reading is where the gift is.

In play: Sera's lookout comes back with the patrol's route, its timing, and the name of the officer who sets it.

Crossed - Knowing too much, a source exposed.

The intelligence is good - all of it - and that is the trouble. It is too complete to act on quietly: move on everything you know and everyone will ask how you knew it. The knowledge itself now needs guarding, and the watcher who gathered it may need protecting from what they saw. Spend it slowly, or spend it all at once and accept what that reveals.

In play: The lookout saw everything - including the quartermaster passing a purse to the raiders. Act on the route now and the garrison will know exactly where its leak stands.

Mira in the Market

The bladesmith doesn't just sell her a blade. He's watched this market for years, and mentions, unasked, which steel holds an edge and who else has been buying swords lately. She leaves with more than she came for.

Crossed - The smith knows who else came asking this week, and says so too freely. She may not notice the hooded man who overhears with a frown, then pushes his way through the crowd to report who else heard it.
