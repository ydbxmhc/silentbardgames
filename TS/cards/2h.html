Two of Hearts

Scout's Report

Yes, and — Social

Keywords — Upright: a clean lead, generous intelligence, more than asked  ·  Inverted: too much to act on, knowledge that exposes

Upright

Intelligence delivered cleanly, and more than was asked for. Someone was watching and what they saw benefits you.

Inverted

The report comes in fuller than anyone wanted — so much that acting on it means admitting how closely you watched, or so much truth it can't be set down again.

In Play

Upright. Maret's lookout returns with the patrol's route and the name of the man who set it.

Inverted. The lookout learns everything, including the one fact that, spoken aloud, puts her on the wrong side of it.
