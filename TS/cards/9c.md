# Nine of Clubs

![Nine of Clubs](cards/9c.png)

## Censor's Torch

No, and - Conflict

**Keywords** - *Upright:* knowledge destroyed, exposure, marked for looking · *Inverted:* the burning reveals, ash that proves

### Upright

The knowledge existed, you found it, and now it's ash and you're known for having looked. Everything got worse the moment the torch fell.

### Inverted

What you found is ash and you're known for the seeking - but the burning is its own confession. Someone took great pains to unmake this, and that trouble is the proof the page never was: the fire shows you just what was worth hiding.

### In Play

*Upright.* Wynn reaches the record a day late; only scorch-marks and her name on the visitor's roll remain.

*Inverted.* The record's burned, but who burned it and how fast tells Wynn more than the pages would, and the charred gap is evidence in itself.
