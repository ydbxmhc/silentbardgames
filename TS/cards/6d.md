# Six of Diamonds

![Six of Diamonds](cards/6d.png)

## Courser's Re-shoeing

Yes, but — Commercial

**Keywords** — *Upright:* arrival at a cost, upkeep, a toll in time · *Inverted:* stranded by upkeep, the mount fails late

### Upright

You got where you needed to go. The horse needs attention before you go anywhere else. Time and coin and the farrier's schedule.

### Inverted

You reached the place, but the toll falls due at the worst hour — the horse goes lame before the next leg, the repair eats time you hadn't got, the road stalls where you can least afford it.

### In Play

*Upright.* Garrick makes the waystation; the farrier shakes his head at the mare's split shoe.

*Inverted.* Garrick makes the waystation, but the mare throws her shoe a day short of the pass with the storm already coming down.
