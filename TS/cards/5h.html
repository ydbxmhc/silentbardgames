Five of Hearts

Forager's Bounty

Yes, and - Social

Keywords - Upright: the land's surplus, provision, a good season  ·  Inverted: excess that spoils, a harvest that calls scavengers

Upright

Nature provided more than asked. The land is generous today and something extra came with the harvest.

The country is generous today - more than the pot needs, more than the packs were sized for. Abundance like this is information as well as food: the land is healthy here, the season is turning your way, and what you cannot eat you can dry, trade, or leave as goodwill for whoever follows.

Inverted

More than you can carry is not wealth; it is bait. Surplus spoils, slows the march, and announces itself downwind to everything with an appetite. Take what serves and walk away from the rest - the discipline of leaving plenty behind is the skill this moment is testing.

In Play

Upright. Doral's snares are full by dawn - enough to feed the camp and smoke a week's surplus besides.

Inverted. The snares are full past carrying, and by the time Doral has dressed the third hare, the blood-smell has brought a bear upwind of camp.
