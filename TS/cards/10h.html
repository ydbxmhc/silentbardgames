Ten of Hearts

Alchemist's Discovery

Yes, and - Social

Keywords - Upright: a breakthrough, the craft exceeding its brief  ·  Inverted: a result too big for the bench, unwanted renown

Upright

The experiment worked and produced something nobody expected. Intellectual generosity flowing outward from a successful making.

The work did what it was designed to do, and then did something better. This is craft rewarded with discovery: a side effect worth more than the intended one, a technique that generalizes, a door in the workshop wall you did not know was there. Write it down exactly. Breakthroughs that cannot be repeated are only stories.

Inverted

It works too well to keep. A result like this slips its bench and gets ambitious: word spreads, demand arrives, and with demand come the people who license, tax, copy, and confiscate. The discovery is sound - it is the world's response that now needs managing. Decide who learns of it before they decide for you.

In Play

Upright. Oskar's preserving tincture keeps the meat - and, tried on a whim, clears the kennel's mange in three days.

Inverted. The tincture cures the mange, then the miller's cough, and by month's end the guild has sent a man to ask after Oskar's license.
