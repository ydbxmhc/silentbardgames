Four of Clubs

Burglar's Ligature

No, and - Conflict

Keywords - Upright: caught and bound, worsening, a tightening trap  ·  Inverted: the binding overcommits, slack in the knot

Upright

Caught and bound, and things are actively getting worse. The ligature is doing real work.

Inverted

Caught and trussed and it's getting worse - but they bound you in haste and bound themselves to watching you. The thoroughness of the trap is where it fails: one knot overlooked, one guard too certain.

In Play

Upright. The cords bite Thrn's wrists; every twist he tries only draws them tighter.

Inverted. They tie Thrn so fast and so smug that no one checks the far cord, and that's the one with give.
