Ten of Clubs

Miller's Fire

No, and - Conflict

Keywords - Upright: total loss, fast ruin, everything taken  ·  Inverted: the fire clears, ash that fertilizes

Upright

The mill burned. Flour dust and millstones and the particular horror of grain fires that spread fast and take everything.

Inverted

The mill burns and takes it all - but a clean burn leaves clean ground. What the fire destroys it also ends: the debt that burned with the ledger, the old workings swept off, the forced fresh start no one would choose and the place perhaps needed.

In Play

Upright. Grain-dust catches and the mill goes up in minutes; a year's living gone with it.

Inverted. The mill burns to the stones, and so do the liens against it; Pell rebuilds on land that for once owes no one.
