Six of Clubs

Grizzly's Pursuit

No, and - Conflict

Keywords - Upright: the hunted, a relentless pursuit, prey  ·  Inverted: the hunter overcommits, the chase that traps the chaser

Upright

You are the prey. The pursuit is relentless and it made everything worse. Distance is your only friend right now.

Inverted

You're the prey and it won't quit - but a thing that single-minded can be led. The pursuit driving you is also a leash on it: run it onto thin ice, into a rival's ground, across the trap that was laid for you.

In Play

Upright. The bear has Doral's scent and the gap is closing; there's only running left.

Inverted. Doral runs the bear straight over the trappers' deadfall, and the thing hunting him springs the snare meant for him.
