Nine of Spades

Sage's Rescheduling

No, but - Labor

Keywords - Upright: knowledge delayed, an appointment kept, progress without result  ·  Inverted: the delay spares you, late proves better

Upright

The Sage couldn't see you today. The knowledge is delayed, not denied. The appointment exists. Progress without result.

Inverted

The answer's put off, but the delay does you a kindness you didn't ask for - the door you skipped today was the one that shut on someone else. The appointment that counts is the next.

In Play

Upright. The sage can't see Wynn until the new moon; the knowledge waits, not denied.

Inverted. The sage turns Wynn away, and the man who took her slot is the one the watch arrests on the steps.
