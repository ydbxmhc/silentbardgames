# Card Lexicon — Synoptic Table (stash)

*Cards listed H (Yes/and), D (Yes/but), S (No/but), C (No/and)*

## Face Cards

### Jacks - Adepts
*Intuitive power, internal fire, flexible and unpredictable.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| JH | **Bard's Ode** | A gift freely given, a story that opens doors. Charm and talent working entirely in your favor, rippling outward. |
| JD | **Prophet's Levy** | The vision was accurate, the path clear - but the Prophet collects his due and you didn't get a vote. |
| JS | **Friar's Indulgence** | You didn't earn this. The ledger says no, and yet here is a pass, slightly corrupt and entirely functional. |
| JC | **Soothsayer's Hex** | Truth was spoken and it cut deep. Someone saw something they shouldn't have, and the knowing has teeth. |

### Queens - Conjurors
*Negotiation, transformation, deals with powers seen and unseen.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| QH | **Enchantress' Boon** | A gift from someone who didn't have to give it. Something social blooms unexpectedly. |
| QD | **Sibyl's Price** | You got what you came for. The price was higher than quoted and the Sibyl is already counting it out. |
| QS | **Harpy's Bargain** | Unpleasant terms, uncomfortable company. And yet the Harpy held up her end. You got something out of this. |
| QC | **Witch's Curse** | Not merely opposition - deliberate, targeted, personal. Someone with real power decided you are the problem. |

### Kings - Spellweavers
*Institutional authority, bound power, academic mastery.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| KH | **Guildmaster's Favor** | Institutional approval, publicly given. Someone with organizational authority has decided you are worth backing. |
| KD | **Magister's Fee** | The knowledge was accurate, the work correct. The Magister's invoice is already waiting. Non-negotiable. |
| KS | **Artificer's Workaround** | Something bound and purposeful works against you. But somewhere in the mechanism there's a jury-rigged patch. |
| KC | **Inquisitor's Writ** | The full weight of organized authority, properly documented. Not personal. Which makes it worse. |

## Pip Cards

### Aces - Warriors
*Direct, physical, immediate. As opposition: a single guard, a common brawler, a drunk with a knife.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| AH | **Champion's Glory** | The fighter's triumph spills into celebration. The crowd is cheering and something beyond victory arrives. |
| AD | **Mercenary's Wage** | The sword arm won the day. The invoice is already on the table. |
| AS | **Duellist's Evasion** | The blade arm failed but the footwork saved you. A skilled retreat. You know more about your opponent now. |
| AC | **Berserker's Rampage** | Something let loose and made everything worse. Violence escalated past any useful point and kept going. |

### Twos - Snipers
*Precision, patience, distance. As opposition: a watchman on a wall, a careful observer.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 2H | **Scout's Report** | Intelligence delivered cleanly, and more than was asked for. |
| 2D | **Marksman's Reload** | The shot landed. Now there's a pause, a cost, a moment of vulnerability while the mechanism resets. |
| 2S | **Catapult's Misfire** | The mechanism failed but something useful landed in the chaos anyway. Perhaps better than intended. |
| 2C | **Arbalest's Volley** | Heavy, mechanical, and still coming. Didn't stop and made things considerably worse. |

### Threes - Performers
*Warm, slippery, deeply human.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 3H | **Actor's Roses** | The crowd threw flowers. The performance landed and then some. |
| 3D | **Mentor's Critique** | The performance worked. Now here are the notes, the expectations, the required improvements. |
| 3S | **Reviewer's Nostalgia** | The review wasn't favorable, but something triggered an unexpected benefit from an accidental angle. |
| 3C | **Heckler's Victory** | The performance failed and the room turned. The heckler has the crowd now. |

### Fours - Sneaks
*Quiet, shadowy, consequential.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 4H | **Spy's Package** | The intelligence arrived and something extra was tucked inside that nobody expected. |
| 4D | **Assassin's Compulsion** | The job is done. But now you're bound to something that doesn't release. |
| 4S | **Prisoner's Map** | Failed, confined, seemingly stuck. Someone scratched a way out in the dark. |
| 4C | **Burglar's Ligature** | Caught and bound, and things are actively getting worse. |

### Fives - Naturalists
*Grounded, physical, honest about what nature provides and takes.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 5H | **Forager's Bounty** | Nature provided more than asked. The land is generous today. |
| 5D | **Wayfarer's Pack** | You have what you need. It's heavy, it costs, and it slows you down. |
| 5S | **Herbalist's Antidote** | The situation is bad but here is something specific that addresses the harm. Bought time if nothing else. |
| 5C | **Mountaineer's Avalanche** | Unstoppable, impersonal, made everything worse. The mountain didn't even notice you. |

### Sixes - Beast Handlers
*Read from the perspective of whoever draws - the animal is sometimes ally, sometimes predator.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 6H | **Ox Team's Calf** | The labor partnership produced something new and valuable beyond the work itself. |
| 6D | **Courser's Re-shoeing** | You got where you needed to go. The horse needs attention before you go anywhere else. |
| 6S | **Hounds' Fault** | The hunting term for losing the scent. Failed the task but their last heading is worth following. |
| 6C | **Grizzly's Pursuit** | You are the prey. The pursuit is relentless and it made everything worse. |

### Sevens - Athletes
*Kinetic and human - the body's triumphs and failures under pressure.*

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 7H | **Victor's Laurels** | Classical and generous. Everything that comes with public triumph arrives at once. |
| 7D | **Competitor's Fee** | You placed, you earned. The entry fee, the trainer, the equipment all have their hands out. |
| 7S | **Runner's Consolation** | Didn't win, didn't place, but finished. Quiet dignity in the effort. |
| 7C | **Dropout's Disgrace** | Not just losing but the public consequence of failing to finish. |

| Card | Name | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 8H | **Nurse's Handfasting** | Healer and patient bound together through the act of care. A tie formed that wasn't there before. |
| 8D | **Remedy's Addiction** | The remedy worked. Now you need it and that need has teeth. The success keeps costing. |
| 8S | **Hollow Leg** | The medicine went in and nothing changed. The patient is still upright, still moving, still here. |
| 8C | **Apothecary's Grief** | The treatment failed and the condition worsened. The professional understands exactly what went wrong. |

| 9D | **Investigator's Attention** | You found what you were looking for. Someone noticed you looking. |

| 9C | **Censor's Torch** | The knowledge existed, you found it, and now it's ash and you're known for having looked. |
