# Dominion and Accord -- Playtest Pregens

*Three scenario options with ready characters. Built 2026-08-07.*
*Setting reference: `setting-notes.md` in this folder.*

**Karma standard: 10.** Calibrated against the Firefly conversions in
`firefly-reference.md`, which felt right for competent, experienced characters without
runaway numbers. A 5-Karma version of any of these is a viable "greener" variant.

**Expected table: 2 players**, possibly 3 or 4. Each scenario has a core pair; COOP has two
additional characters for a larger table.

## Which to run

| Scenario | Tests | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **HULK** | Creative Role application with no gear, authority, or combat | Purest system test. Most thematically loaded. |
| **CONTRABAND** | The core mechanic under time pressure | Clearest objective, easiest to run. |
| **COOP** | Hooks carrying the drama at low stakes | Best character work. Strongest two-player dynamic. |

Recommended order for a 2-player playtest: **HULK, CONTRABAND, COOP** -- though COOP has the
single best pairing of the three (see below).

## HULK -- the spares

> The crew wake when power to their cryopods starts failing. The ship is broken. They are the
> few **backup-crew synths** that survived, because their pods weren't in the rooms where the
> hull tore. Now they have to survive long enough to be collected.

Not the good crew. The *redundant* crew.

### Ledger -- purser / inventory synth

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Loremaster 2         Ship's Manifest 2
1K   Loremaster Maneuver  Procedures 1
1K   Tinker 1             Systems Diagnostics 1
1K   Performer 1          Reasonable Tone 1

Hook: Personhood Is a Local Matter 1 (free)

Tricks:
     I Know What We Had -- Loremaster(Ship's Manifest) as a
       free-action Boost to any search or salvage attempt.
       He doesn't look for things. He remembers them.

     By the Book -- Performer(Reasonable Tone) applied when
       invoking procedure or authority he doesn't actually
       have. Calm citation of regulation as a social lever.
```

### Ratch -- maintenance labor synth

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Tinker 2             Hull & Pressure 2
2K   Athlete 2            EVA 2
1K   Loremaster 1         Ship's Bones 1

Hook: Built for This, Not for Thinking 1 (free)

Tricks:
     Feel the Draft -- Tinker(Hull & Pressure) as a free-action
       Boost to locating a breach, leak, or failing seal.

     Long Arms -- Athlete(EVA) Wagered for reach and grip in
       vacuum or zero-g. He commits before he knows the hold
       will take.
```

### Running it

The pair works because **Ledger knows and Ratch does**, and neither can fight. Every problem
has to be solved rather than beaten.

**The lever: who is coming, and what are they collecting?** Salvage rights, their registered
owner, or an insurance assessor. If the assessor's job is to write off the hull *and* its
inventory, two surviving synths are line items that make the paperwork worse. **Rescue is the
threat.**

**Decide at the table, not in advance:** did they Epiphany before the accident, or did waking
up alone do it? One player treating it as obvious while the other is in denial is a session of
friction for free.

Ties to setting: Epiphany can't be guaranteed either way past a complexity threshold, and
personhood is a jurisdiction rather than a status. Nobody is aboard to rule on either.

## COOP -- the family ship

> Members of a small family ship that serves as a travelling home and just needs to earn
> enough to support everyone.

**Make them Hydran.** The two-player dynamic becomes two sisters for whom **being in the same
room always costs something** -- a mouthpiece, a helmet, a clock, a section reflooded.

Not a wall. Hydran workarounds are mature: extended breath-holding, casual bite-in aerators,
helmets that preserve speech, and **Flox, which both of them breathe normally.** The drama is
that togetherness is never free and never indefinite. See the Flexibilities section in
`setting-notes.md`.

### Sey -- elder sister, runs the ship

One of the 15% who cannot breathe water. A *bubbler*.

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Performer 2          Haggle 2
2K   Loremaster 2         Trade Routes 2
1K   Tinker 1             Navigation 1

Hook: Dry-Born 1 (free)

Tricks:
     The Long Sit -- Performer(Haggle) Wagered as a Persistent
       Boost across a whole negotiation. She outlasts people.

     Route Sense -- Loremaster(Trade Routes) as a free-action
       Boost when deciding where to sell, not how.
```

### Miri -- younger sister, keeps it flying

One of the 10% who can breathe *only* water. A *wheezer*.

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Tinker 2             Ship's Systems 2
1K   Tinker Maneuver      Flox Systems 1
1K   Loremaster 1         Salvage 1
1K   Athlete 1            Pressure-Born 1

Hook: Dry Is Borrowed Time 1 (free)

Tricks:
     Hands in the Wet -- Tinker(Flox Systems) free-action Boost
       to any repair in the flooded sections, which is most of
       the ship's guts.

     Read the Hull -- Tinker(Ship's Systems) as a Boost to
       diagnosis by vibration and pressure change. She feels
       the ship.
```

> Sey runs the dry half, Miri the wet. Day to day they talk through a pressure window and a
> comm because that's *easier*, not because it's required. Either can cross when it matters,
> geared and on a clock, and the Flox sections are common ground where both are simply
> comfortable.
>
> **Dry-Born and Dry Is Borrowed Time are the same Hook from opposite ends**, and both earn
> Karma constantly without anybody firing a shot -- not from being imprisoned, but from the
> steady accumulation of small costs, delays, and preparations that everyone else takes for
> granted.

**The lever:** something needs hands in both halves *at once*, for longer than a mouthpiece or
a helmet buys, and one of them has to trust a stranger.

### Third player -- Tavi, the middle sibling

Standard amphibious Hydran, one of the 75%. Both sisters *can* cross with gear and a clock.
Tavi is the only one for whom crossing is **free** -- no mouthpiece, no helmet, no countdown,
as many times as needed. Grew up knowing that made them the family's errand.

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Athlete 2            Both Media 2
1K   Tinker 1             Cargo Systems 1
1K   Loremaster 1         Dockside 1
1K   Performer 1          Family Translator 1

Hook: "I'll Go" 1 (free)

Tricks:
     The Threshold -- Athlete(Both Media) free-action Boost when
       transitioning between flooded and dry sections. Faster
       and cleaner than anyone, because it's their whole life.

     Two Hands, Two Rooms -- Tavi Assists whichever sister is
       working, using the standard Assist rule. Not exclusive
       access; the point is Tavi can do it repeatedly and
       indefinitely while the others are spending air.
```

*"I'll Go"* is reflexive volunteering. It earns Karma when Tavi is pulled two directions at
once, or takes on something that should have been someone else's, and pays out when being the
bridge is exactly what's needed. A person defined by an accident of physiology, who is
everyone's connective tissue and nobody's own.

### Fourth player -- Oro, married in

**Not Hydran.** Came aboard and *chose* modification to live on a ship built for people who
breathe water. Ship's medic, cook, and general hand.

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Healer 2             Field Medicine 2
1K   Loremaster 1         Hydran Physiology 1
1K   Tinker 1             Flox Chemistry 1
1K   Performer 1          Bedside 1

Hook: Chose This 1 (free)

Tricks:
     Three Body Plans -- Healer(Field Medicine) applied to a
       household where no two patients breathe the same thing.
       Oro knows all three, which almost nobody outside Hydra
       bothers to learn.

     Mix It Myself -- Tinker(Flox Chemistry) free-action Boost
       to any problem involving breathable medium, dosage, or
       contamination.
```

*Chose This* is the sharp one. Hydrans get lumped in with Corporists and answer *"I didn't
modify me."* Oro **did**. In a family that resents the label, Oro is the one person who
elected it, which makes them the family's outsider and its most deliberate member at once.

## CONTRABAND -- one crate, one blockade

> The players are the crew hired to smuggle one sizeable crate through a blockade to the next
> world on the route.

### Voss -- pilot and fixer, augmented

```
5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Athlete 2            Pilot 2
2K   Loremaster 2         Customs & Contraband 2
1K   Performer 1          Nothing to Declare 1

Hook: Wired on Credit 1 (free)

Tricks:
     Thread the Needle -- Athlete(Pilot) Wagered for a transit
       no inspector expects a hull that size to make.

     Paper Is Cheaper Than Guns -- Loremaster(Customs &
       Contraband) as a free-action Boost to producing,
       forging, or exploiting documentation.
```

*Wired on Credit* is augmentation debt. Someone else holds the service contract, which means
someone else can degrade or brick what's in her skull. Earns Karma when the lien is called;
pays out when the hardware is exactly what the moment needs.

### Torrig -- Pellan, cargo and muscle

```
     10K + 2K credit (Atavism rank 3) = 12K

5K   Level 3              3 Luck Tokens
2K   Warrior 2            Grapple 2
1K   Warrior Maneuver     Hold, Don't Hit 1
2K   Athlete 2            Heavy Labor 2
1K   Loremaster 1         Cargo & Rigging 1
1K   Performer 1          Very Still, Very Quiet 1

Hook: Pellan Atavism 3 (credited, +2K)

Tricks:
     Hold, Don't Hit -- Warrior(Hold, Don't Hit) applied to
       control and restrain rather than injure. The discipline
       of a man who knows what he does when he stops choosing.

     Dead Weight -- Athlete(Heavy Labor) free-action Boost to
       moving, bracing, or wedging cargo. Including himself.
```

*Very Still, Very Quiet* is Performer used entirely for **not being noticed** -- a Pellan's
most practiced social skill.

**Atavism reminder:** up to 3 dice whenever Pellan physiology is an advantage, once per scene.
Triggerable for Karma up to 3x/session (variance under test -- see `setting-notes.md`).

### Running it

**They're smuggling two things: the crate, and Torrig.** He isn't permitted on most worlds, so
he is contraband with opinions. Every inspection is two problems, and the one that can end the
session is the one that breathes.

**The lever:** the inspection goes long, someone gets rough, and Torrig's player has the
Atavism trigger sitting right there paying Karma. **Let them choose.** That's the whole system
in one moment.

**Crate contents worth considering** -- each changes the genre:
- An Epiphanied Synth who woke mid-contract, being moved to a jurisdiction that recognizes
  persons.
- Ptokeriat emigrants whose colony charter fell through.
- Something for the Property League that must reach the Collegium at L3.
- Flox-system parts for a Hydran world under embargo.
- Nothing. The crate is a decoy and the crew is the cargo.

## Notes for all three

- **Fold transit isolates the ship.** No comms, no news, no rescue call. Mass sensors only.
  Every scenario can use "you are alone and cannot ask anyone."
- **Under a kiloton means no bulk freight, ever.** A player crew carries high value per
  kilogram or carries nothing. That's the whole economic niche.
- **Personhood is jurisdictional.** Any synth, AI, uplift, or Pellan aboard is a different
  legal object in every port.
- **The T'kon are scenery.** Rumor, mystique, maybe a passing encounter. Not opposition, not
  PCs.
