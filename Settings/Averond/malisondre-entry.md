# Malisondre
*You will know her long before you meet her - by her servants, her debts, and the slow closing of a snare whose weaving you never saw.*

```
Level 6
Shade 6                  (primary Court: Dead)
  Display 6
  Manifestation 6
  ... and a thousand years of lesser seemings
Succubus 5               (secondary Court: Demon)
  Possession 5
  Aura Reading 5
  ... and the craft of binding a willing signature
Performer 6
  Acting, Persuasion, and every art of the turned word, at or near max
Loremaster 5
  names, debts, lineages, and the precise location of every buried thing
Sylph 2
  reach, and the whisper that arrives where she does not
Hook: Cannot Resist a Worthy Bargain
```

**Court:** Dead (primary) &middot; Demon (secondary)
**Presence:** Anchored - but the Anchor has changed.

**Anchor:** No longer a thing of the world. When she took Tahanos's bargain, a clause in that contract bound her continuance to her service, and over the centuries *that clause became her anchor.* She is nailed to Mundis not by bone or barrette but by a line of the Accords themselves. She cannot be ended while it holds. It cannot be reached with a blade.

---

She began as a Shade, a thousand years ago - one grieving, wanting soul like any other in this chapter, trapped by a passion that would not release her. Then the Godling of Necromancy came offering, as such powers do, a way into the world. She signed. What she received was demonic craft and a long leash; what she became was his **lieutenant**, and she has spent ten centuries earning the title.

She is no brute. Tahanos has brutes enough. Malisondre is his **broker** - the patient hand that turns a frightened village, a debt-ridden merchant, a wavering priest, by contract rather than force. The corrupted monastery and its grasping clergy, the priest who came to a coast hunting his lost ghola: these are not *her*. They are threads of a web she has been spinning since before the town had a name, and she is somewhere near its center, unhurried, watching it grow.

**Conceit - *Everything Is a Seeming*:** Malisondre does not brawl, and has not needed to in a thousand years. Manifest, she commands a pool of two dozen dice - but you will never meet it as a blow. Half she holds as a wall of pure refusal: the bowstring that snaps as you loose, the grenade she plucks from the air and tosses aside laughing, the brimstone smoke that floods the field as you blink. The rest she spends on *seeming.* Figures flicker around her in helm and khopesh, and you cannot tell what any of them is. Some are bound ghosts - your blade passes clean through, and theirs does not. Some are pure phantoms of Display, light with nothing behind, and yet they savage the mind: a Complication needing only her Display to turn aside. And some are *her* - though that is her shallowest trick, for form is only makeup to a spirit, worn for the result it buys. She may be several of the figures at once, or none of them: the bitter mist about your ankles, the spell that leaps from a guard's hand, the cloud of choking poison that fills the room while you hunt for a body to cut. Where she manifests, the thing is real - true Injury, answerable in kind only if you can reach the shape she chose. Against the formless air itself there is no shape to find: only a defense, or a flight, and the hope she permits it. Where she wears mere phantasm, she wounds the mind alone and no steel touches her - a spirit's weapon, or nothing. A Second Sight parts truth from show; her own reading parts you. This is her least art - her Performance, her lore, her contract-craft are each their own near-bottomless pool. Whatever ground you choose, she meets you there nearly as deep. She has no thin side.

## How She Works

You do not, at first, fight Malisondre. You are *managed* by her, and you may not know it for a season.

**By proxy.** She acts through what she has collected: lesser Dead bound by her own contracts, corrupted clergy, debtors, the seduced and the leveraged. The party feels her as a pattern - too many misfortunes aligned, too many doors that close at once - long before they find a hand to blame.

**By whisper.** Her Sylph-reach carries her where she is not. A suggestion in the right ear across a city; a presence felt in a sealed room; the sense, late at night, of being precisely *understood.*

**By leverage.** Her Loremaster knows your debts, your dead, your buried shame. She rarely threatens. She *offers* - the thing you want, the secret you need, the rescue you cannot refuse - and the price is always a signature you will tell yourself is small.

**By mask.** Expecting a demon of fire, the credulous receive one: a Display of brimstone and heat she wears like a borrowed coat, though her true element is air. A reputation that frightens is cheaper to keep than one that must be earned twice.

**And, at the last, in the flesh** - which almost no one survives to describe. By the time Malisondre troubles to manifest against you, she has decided you are worth the conspicuousness, and that decision is the most dangerous thing that can befall a mortal in this region.

---

*In play: Malisondre is not an encounter. She is a **situation** - a web of contracts, servants, and patience with a soul at its heart. Her stat block is a warning label, not a fight: reach the point of needing those two dozen dice and the table has already either won by another road or lost by this one. Run her, for nearly the whole of her presence, through what she has bound and bought.*

*When she does manifest, do not soften her. Let the party feel that the contracts and whispers and gentle ruin of the prior months were her being **economical** - that the real thing was always this, held in reserve beneath her notice, until they made themselves worth it.*

*Her undoing is not written here, and that is deliberate. She is anchored to the Accords - and what the Accords bind, they may also loose. The rest is a table's to find.*
