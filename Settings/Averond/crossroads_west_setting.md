# Crossroads West: Starting Region for Averond

## Geographic Overview

### Regional Layout
- **Ganth (The Free City)**: Northeast, at the base of the mountains, elevated position overlooking the region
- **The Bay**: Western coastline opening onto a greater sea
- **Southern Gulf**: Land dips south into an archipelago of islands
- **Terrain**: Mountainous region near sea, heavy forests dominating lowlands closer to shore, cleared farmland around settlements
- **Climate**: Maritime influence from bay and gulf, mountain weather patterns from the northeastern peaks

### Crossroads West - The Hub Town
**Population**: ~300 souls  
**Character**: Just large enough to warrant a proper tavern and inn due to its position at major crossroads  
**Function**: Trading post, rest stop, local administrative center for surrounding villages

**Key Features**:
- The Crossroads Inn & Tavern (primary gathering place)
- Market square (weekly market days)  
- Shrine cluster (multiple gods represented)
- Blacksmith/general craftsman
- Miller (serves surrounding farms)
- Small dock facilities for river/coastal trade

**Spiritual Significance**: Natural spiritual anchor where multiple travel routes have worn paths through the Borderlands over generations. The accumulated intentions and emotions of countless travelers, merchants, and pilgrims create a spiritually active location.

## Surrounding Villages

### Coastal Communities
**Saltwind Harbor** (Fishing Village)  
- Population: ~150
- Architecture: Terraced construction reminiscent of Mediterranean coastal settlements
- Primary Industry: Fishing, salt production, limited coastal trade
- Culture: Mix of practical maritime traditions with some Pacific island community structures
- Spiritual Notes: Strong connection to Susheil through daily interaction with sea and tides

### Agricultural Settlements
**Blumfield** (Primary Farming Community)  
- Population: ~200
- Agriculture: Grain production using irrigation techniques borrowed from ancient Mesopotamian models
- Structure: Communal field systems similar to medieval English arrangements
- Culture: Seasonal festivals tied to planting and harvest cycles
- Spiritual Notes: Strong Grolikarnigan influence through agricultural blessings

**Green Haven** (Mixed Agriculture)  
- Population: ~120
- Agriculture: Vegetables, orchards, some livestock
- Structure: Individual family holdings with shared resources
- Culture: Blend of European smallhold traditions with African agricultural practices
- Spiritual Notes: Shrine maintenance rotated among families

### Pastoral Communities  
**Windmere Pastures** (Herding Community)  
- Population: ~180  
- Lifestyle: Seasonal movement following grazing patterns, echoing Mongolian herding traditions mixed with Sahelian pastoral practices
- Structure: Extended family groups, council of elders
- Primary Stock: Sheep, goats, some cattle
- Spiritual Notes: Mobile shrine practices, strong connection to Grolikarnigan's wandering aspects

## Political Structure

### Governance
- **Regional Authority**: Nominally under Ganth's influence, but practically self-governing
- **Local Leadership**: Village councils, elected headmen, traditional elders depending on community structure
- **Trade Regulation**: Managed through Ganth's guild representatives who travel circuit routes
- **Justice**: Combination of local custom and Ganth's magistrates for serious crimes

### Economic Ties
- **Tax Structure**: Villages send surplus to Ganth in exchange for protection and trade access
- **Guild Connections**: Craftsmen maintain relationships with appropriate Ganth guilds
- **Trade Routes**: Multiple caravan routes converge at Crossroads West before continuing to Ganth or coastal/southern destinations

## Ancient History: The Fallen Saint

### Sarina, the Vanished Saint of the Wild
**Former Domain**: This region was once ruled by Sarina, an immortal Saint who governed the wilderness and forbade the establishment of large cities. She patronized the faerie courts and maintained a realm where wild magic flourished under strict protection of natural order.

**The Fey Empire**: Under Sarina's protection, both Seelie and Unseelie courts established estates throughout the region. Faerie lords commissioned troll laborers to build impossible structures - castles, towers, and courts that followed dream logic rather than architectural sense. These buildings often featured:
- Doors that open onto walls or empty air
- Stairs that ascend and descend simultaneously  
- Towers larger inside than outside
- Rooms that exist only on certain days
- Architecture that shifts based on observer's emotional state

**The Orchestrated Rebellion**: Centuries ago, Tahanos identified this region as strategically valuable and systematically worked to destabilize Sarina's rule. He:
- Fostered rebellion among the Winter Court's Unseelie faction, particularly targeting goblinkind (Halflings)
- Exploited natural tensions between Seelie and Unseelie courts  
- Provided goblinkind tribes with demonic patrons and Death magic
- Gradually weakened Sarina's support base through spiritual corruption

**The Goblinkind War**: The conflict that destroyed Sarina's realm was not a traditional fey civil war, but a rebellion of Unseelie Halflings against pure spirit faeries. Goblinkind - including goblins proper, ogres, and trolls - exist in both physical and spiritual realms simultaneously, allowing them to harm pure spirit faeries in ways other entities could not. The war had no clear battle lines since Unseelie communities were scattered throughout fey territories under Winter Court influence.

**Sarina's Erasure**: When the Saint became overwhelmed trying to maintain order during the escalating conflict, Tahanos struck directly. Unlike other forms of death that Saints can potentially recover from, Tahanos used his mastery of Death magic to completely erase her existence. She did not just die - she was removed from reality itself.

### Current State of Ancient Ruins

**Troll-Built Structures**: The abandoned fey estates now lie scattered throughout the forests, overgrown and partially collapsed but still architecturally impossible. These serve as perfect "dungeons" for adventuring parties:
- Interiors don't match exteriors in size or layout
- Rooms connect in non-Euclidean patterns  
- Some areas exist only during specific conditions
- Treasure hoards follow faerie logic rather than practical value

**Goblin Inhabitation**: Many ruins now house goblinkind communities, some of which are:
- **Descendants of original rebels** - still carrying spiritual corruption from ancient deals with Tahanos
- **Tribes with demonic patrons** - each typically dominated by a single demon pursuing its own agenda  
- **Independent communities** - freed from ancient obligations but harboring complex relationships with the past
- **Integrated individuals** - members of goblinkind who have left tribal life to live among humans (possible player characters)

**Surviving Fey**: Most powerful pure spirit faerie lords had their anchors systematically destroyed, making return to the material world difficult. Remaining fey are typically:
- **Small spirits** without significant power or territory
- **Nature spirits** - hybrid entities that are both elemental and fae (dryads, spriggans, etc.)
- **Hidden survivors** - pure spirit fae operating from deep concealment in remote locations
- **Corrupted remnants** - fae who have been twisted by centuries of exposure to Death magic

### Fey Court Structure
- **Winter Court of Mab**: Dominated by Unseelie faction, includes most Halfling goblinkind
- **Summer Court of Oberon and Titania**: Primarily Seelie, with fewer Halfling members
- **Goblinkind Classification**: Halfling members of the Unseelie faction, including goblins proper, ogres, and trolls
- **Nature Spirits**: Hybrid entities possessing both elemental and fae aspects simultaneously

### Spiritual Scarring and Veil Damage

**Sarina's Destroyed Legacy**:
- Most shrines and sacred sites were systematically destroyed by Tahanos's forces
- Remaining artifacts or holy sites are rare and therefore highly significant when discovered
- Her protective influence over the wilderness has been permanently severed

**Veil Manipulation**: Centuries of demonic influence have created:
- **Thin Spots** - areas where spirits can cross easily into the material world, typically near demon shrines or sites of ancient goblin rituals
- **Dead Zones** - locations where the spiritual realm has been stretched so thin it creates holes, resulting in "no magic zones" where spiritual power cannot function
- **Corrupted Currents** - spiritual pathways that have been twisted to serve Tahanos's purposes
- **Anchor Destruction** - systematic elimination of stable spiritual landmarks that once provided navigation for beneficial spirits

### Tahanos's Strategic Investment

**Why This Region Again**: Tahanos returns to this area because:
- **Previous Investment** - He has already expended considerable effort establishing influence here
- **Existing Infrastructure** - Corrupted goblin tribes, damaged veil, spiritual scarring all serve his purposes  
- **Strategic Resources** - The region's position between Ganth and the coastal/southern trade routes makes it valuable
- **Unfinished Business** - Complete control was interrupted by the legendary heroes who stopped his undead plague
- **Practical Power Base** - Any form of influence represents power he will not willingly abandon

**Current Assets**:
- Corrupted monastery serving as primary base of operations
- Network of goblin tribes with varying degrees of loyalty/corruption
- Damaged spiritual landscape that facilitates his operations
- Established spiritual anchors and thin spots for accessing the material world
- Accumulated knowledge of local geography, politics, and spiritual terrain

## Spiritual Interaction and Sacrifice Mechanics

### The Universal Nature of Spiritual Contact
Averond operates as an animistic world with spirits potentially present anywhere and everywhere. Any character can attempt to contact local spirits through sacrifice and negotiation, regardless of whether they possess the Conjuring Role. The more dramatic and meaningful the offering, the more likely a spirit will take notice and respond.

### The Sacrifice Principle
All meaningful spiritual interaction requires genuine sacrifice - something of actual value must be given to gain spiritual attention and obligation. The underlying principle is that spirits operate on reciprocal exchange governed by the Accords.

**Mechanical Cost**: Every spiritual bargain requires spending a Luck Token, representing the fatigue, risk, and spiritual cost of the interaction.

**Narrative Sacrifice Examples**:
- **Air Spirits**: Offer the breath of your body (temporary breathlessness, dizziness)
- **Earth Spirits**: Blood from your hands, a treasured possession, years of your life
- **Water Spirits**: Tears, memories of joy, promises of future service
- **Fire Spirits**: Pain, passion, secrets you've never told
- **Ghosts**: Days of your life, specific memories, access to sensory experiences

### Consequences of Spiritual Bargains

**Memories as Currency**: 
- Can be paid with Karma points (permanent mechanical loss)
- Specific memories traded away are gone forever
- Example: Trading memory of a loved one's face to save your village

**Days of Your Life**:
- **Option 1**: Pay with Karma points (representing life force/vitality)
- **Option 2**: Wake up later after the spirit has "lived" those days in your body
- The spirit experiences human life through your form while you're unconscious
- Consequences depend entirely on the spirit's nature and understanding

**Ghost Motivations**: 
- Ghosts remember being human and desperately miss physical sensations
- They want to smoke, drink, eat, have sex, feel warmth - all denied to them in death
- Ghost bargains often result in indulgent, hedonistic behavior that creates social problems
- You might wake up banned from taverns, in debt, with angry lovers, or wanted by authorities

**Other Spirit Consequences**:
- **Elementals**: May reorganize your life according to their alien logic
- **Fae**: Will fulfill bargains through creative interpretation that technically meets the terms
- **Demons**: Actively seek to corrupt and create long-term spiritual debt
- **Nature Spirits**: May expect ongoing respect for natural spaces and cycles

### Negotiation as Core Skill
This system makes negotiation skills incredibly valuable, transforming bards from "social support" into "supernatural brokers" who understand:
- How to structure fair deals under the Accords
- What constitutes appropriate payment for different spirit types
- How to avoid unfavorable contract terms
- When to refuse dangerous bargains

### The Accords in Practice
- **Binding Nature**: All agreements are magically enforceable
- **Fair Exchange**: Spirits must provide value equivalent to what they receive
- **Literal Interpretation**: Spirits often interpret terms differently than humans expect
- **Debt Transfer**: Anyone can assume another's spiritual debt voluntarily
- **Contract Breaking**: Violating the Accords releases all parties from obligation
