## 1. The Through-Line

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED*

The system was never the point. **The table does the heavy lifting; the system's job is to get out of the way and hand the GM the right small tools.** Everything below serves that.

- The same small toolset - Hooks, Harm, Karma, the Accords - points in *any* direction. One statline can run an arc from curse to companion with no new rules.
- **Never declare a system "can't do something" until the toolbox is empty.** We were wrong multiple times in one session - 5e's Wisdom-drain, Fate's Bronze Rule, 5e's formless predator, Boundless's Extras all worked. The bias is to match a system to its *stereotype* and miss its depth. Check the deep cuts first.
- Every system has a grain. Knowing it is the craft.

## 2. Entry Design (Grimoire Format)

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED - contains one confirmed fabrication (the "validated mold")*

- **Seeds, not specimens.** An entry is the smallest set of facts from which a GM can rebuild the creature. The horror, pity, and trap are theirs to find. Leave room.
- **Teach by springing the trap.** Build entries that *do it to the reader* rather than lecturing the mindset. Internalized, not instructed.
- **Format follows function.** Complex agents earn the full mold. Simple hazards stay lean - their austerity *is* the content. Do not bloat the simple example to match the complex one.
- @@ **UNVERIFIED - the author does not recognize this.** ~~The validated mold (for complex agents): color line, stat block, Court/Presence, Anchor, Conceit, *How It Works*, *The Roads*, three-tier GM notes.~~ "Validated" by whom is unclear; *How It Works* and *The Roads* appear nowhere else in any document or in the rulebook, and Lee has never heard of them. Most likely coined by a prior model - possibly as section headers in the lost Agnes/Vane/Malisondre artifacts - and recorded here as though established. **Do not build to this template until the author confirms or replaces it.**
- **Conceits vs Traits.** Traits for shared supernatural properties (recognized keywords). Conceits for unique narrative puzzles. A Conceit invites creative solving; a bare Trait invites rules-lawyering.
- **No hardcoded Levels/ranks in base templates.** Let GMs calibrate. Examples illustrate a *range*, they do not prescribe.
- **Avoid taxonomy.** Give exemplars, let tables extrapolate.
- **Ground in myth, then twist.** Familiar archetype bent to fit Averond reads as fresh *and* legible.
- **Order a section to teach an arc.** The Dead walk an ascending relationship to *form*: *trapped* (Hanged Man) → *bound* (Agnes) → *forced* (Vane) → *free* (Malisondre). Bracket with *living* characters (Pell: Touched; Reincarnate: Born).

## 3. The Two-Axis Model

*Status: ✅ **VERIFIED (with amendments) 2026-07-21** - confirmed by Lee as his own work, except as noted below.*

> **Amendments from the author, 2026-07-21:**
> - The **model is his**; only the *term* "Presence" came from elsewhere. It's serviceable, so it stays.
> - **Court and Presence as labels on every entry was never his idea. They come off.** Everything in The Dead is Dead; the tag is noise. Worse, it's wrong on Pell and Liora, who are not Dead and not members of the Court.
> - **No "Deadborn."** An irresponsible trigger term for anyone who has suffered a miscarriage. "Deathborn" is also wrong for a reincarnate. *Trollborn* and *Faeborn* do fine work; this construction does not.
> - **"Pure Matter" is a clumsy way to say *mundane*.** Retired.
> - **The spectrum is an authoring tool, not player-facing content.** Its job is to help *us* find logical holes and gaps. It should never be presented to players except possibly in-character, in something written for Conjurors and Loremasters.
> - **Target feel: Grimm's and the Nibelungenlied, not a dichotomous key.** Medieval list, not Monster Manual. The hordes of Chaos should not sort as neatly as Vrock and Glabrezu.

- **Court** = *kind* (Dead/Ghost, Demon, Fae, Elemental). The broad category.
- **Presence** = *physicality*, a spectrum from pure spirit to pure matter, split at a midline:
  *Pure Spirit · Anchored · Halfling | Born · Touched · Pure Matter*
- **Role** = the mechanical subtype (Spectre, Shade, Revenant, Succubus). Distinct from Court.
- **Split Court.** A creature may hold more than one, with a primary.
- **The two mirrored pairs:**
  - *Acquired* (toward the poles): **Anchored** - a spirit that took on a tether; **Touched** - matter later infused with spirit.
  - *Native* (flanking the midline): **Halfling** - born deeply dual, spirit at the core; **Born** - born with the spirit-kind as a blood-deep predilection, matter at the core.
- **"Born" is flesh with an inborn affinity, never spirit wearing flesh.** Duer are stone-*born*. The Dead's Born is the **reincarnate** - a living person carrying a prior death as a thread.
- **A spirit born of an aspect that never lived is a Demon - not the Born rung.**
- **The Dead resist Born, because death is acquired.** Their one native rung is the reincarnate, who is not dead at all.
- **The native rungs are the player-character zone.** Halfling, Born = persons, playable. Acquired/forced rungs trend monstrous.

## 4. Mechanical Truths *(L1 rules clarifications)*

*Status: 🟡 PARTIALLY VERIFIED 2026-07-20 - see per-bullet marks*

- **"The attacker chooses the Harm"** is the unifying engine. One mechanic runs the whole escalation - Hindrance, Complication, installing a Hook, taking them Down.
- **Hooks are the template engine** *and* a handle anyone can grab against you when relevant. Imposed Hooks tapped once per scene, relevance-gated, deplete and reset.
- ⚠ **Hooks degrade when resisted** ~~(about one rank per session if unfed)~~ and may be *renamed* as they fade. `@@ MECHANISM WRONG, 2026-07-21.` It is not decay from resistance. Per `hooks-and-growth.md`: **declining** a proposed Hook event costs a Luck Token **or** one rank, once per session. The renaming half is correct - *"Curiosity Killed the Cat" may become "Once Bitten, Twice Shy."*
- **Hindrance is situational** - invisible until relevant. Drags relevant rolls *and* resistance to the next attack. Corruption self-accelerates.
- **Unspecified Harm** sorts on first recovery: Complication wipes, remainder was Injury.
- **Injury is a general EL drag** - softens the victim against every threat, not just its source.
- ✅ **Down is the takeover trigger.** No special meter. Possession = winning the fight.
- ✅ **Level measures force, never threat.** A "weak" creature compounds via Hook stacking into a terrifying climax.
- ✅ **A Role contributes only as much of Level as it can use.** Force a creature onto a contest only a low Role can answer and its great Level barely matters.
- ✅ **Authored seams, not forced ones.** L1 leaves the brush in the designer's hand. Malisondre's seam is *Sylph 2* - drag her onto air and the 24-die pool collapses to six.
- **Form is fiction.** A spirit's Manifestation need not be a body - mist, gas, the air itself. Manifest = real Injury, targetable; phantasm = psychic only; formless = no shape to strike.
- ✅ **A manifest spirit may run Manifestation and Display together** - one pool Split between unbreakable defense and offense across many foes.
- **All damage defaults to Complication for the Zombie** unless explicitly Wagered as Injury or Hindrance. Called shots are the only meaningful attacks.

## 5. Boundless Mechanics *(key grain)*

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED - no Boundless reference material on hand to check against*

- **Only players roll.** Position affects PC dice. The GM rolls damage and Fortune Rolls only.
- **Position:** Impossible (can't try), Bad (6 only = Hit), Standard (5-6), Good (4+)
- **Conditions** fill inventory Slots; cleared only in fiction. Some worsen over time.
- **Pools** (Companion, Morale, Strain, Event) erode by 1 per Miss on each roll (max 2). Recovered by fiction or rest. Everything counts down by default.
- **Fortune Rolls** for uncertain GM-side outcomes: more dice = more likely (0D: only 6 counts; 1D: unlikely; 2D: even; 3D: likely; 4D: very likely).
- **Create an Advantage** - make a Check; each Hit creates an Advantage (+1D to future relevant rolls for self or allies).
- **1HP** signals "this is not a combat encounter" without a special rule.
- **3 Armor** solves "immune to undirected damage" natively - only called shots penetrate.
- **Morale Pools** for NPC groups; **Companion Pool** for allied NPCs; **Strain Pool** for session-spanning pressure.
- **Shadowdark is OSR/B/X DNA** - simple monster blocks (AC, HP, attacks, 1-2 special abilities), torch management, deadly and fast.
- **Daggerheart uses 2d12 duality dice** (Hope die and Fear die) - beat the Difficulty, but the GM gains Fear on your roll; spend Hope for positive effects. Adversary format is streamlined.

## 6. GM Craft **[TIPS]**

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED - craft advice, low risk, but still Lee's call*

- **Win slowly.** Spend a grip post-roll, only enough for the barest success. The dread is in gradual loss.
- **Never let them feel *stopped* - let them feel *persuaded*.** Narrate thoughts arriving in the PC's own voice, built from true things, toward a conclusion that flatters the choice.
- **Present an imposed bargain as the contract that grants the benefit.** The buried clause is what it costs to leave.
- **Some things are weather, not monsters.** For a mindless hazard, do not roll initiative or treat it as a foe.
- **The power to erode a PC from the inside is a trust.** Make the struggle *meaningful*, never *hopeless*.
- **Let resistance win sometimes.** A corruption with no exit is a railroad, not horror.
- **Make the right choice cost the most.** That is the honest shape of temptation.
- **A foe only some can perceive removes the perceiver first.**
- **In Boundless, describe the Position before the roll.** The GM frames the situation; the player chooses their approach; *then* dice. Position is the GM's primary tool, not creature stats.

## 7. Cross-System Lessons

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED*

- **Be fair, and concede openly.** Credibility requires honesty. Conceded wins: 5e's Ethereal/Truesight; 5e's Wisdom-drain; 5e's 3-Armor zombie; Fate's invoke-loop; Fate's concession mechanic; Boundless's Extras solving session-spanning threats.
- **Compare like for like.** NPC tier to NPC tier. No strawmen.
- **The three "souls":** 5e adds machinery. Fate spends its economy. L1 names what the thing is. Cortex assembles reusable parts (closest kin to L1). Boundless trusts the GM with minimal scaffolding. Shadowdark strips to the bone (OSR). Daggerheart narrates the dice (Hope/Fear).
- **The dual-natured descriptor family:** L1 Hook = Fate Aspect = Cortex Distinction = (approximately) Shadowdark special ability tag. 5e alone lacks a native tappable-liability mechanic.
- **Unification is L1's deepest edge.** One grammar from a 1-line Hanged Man to a 24-die Malisondre. 5e fragments by chapter. Fate and Cortex hold creatures on one sheet; Boundless strips furthest.
- **Bounded vs. open scaling.** 5e's d20 is the only true ceiling. L1 scales by stacking ranks into one deep EL. Cortex scales by widening the pool (more dice = better total + fatter effect die + fewer glitches). Boundless scales by Position and pool size.
- **The authored seam beats "no thin side."** L1 doesn't grant invulnerability - it hands you the brush. Malisondre HAS a seam (Sylph 2). That's better design than a genuinely seamless foe.
- **Scary vs. playable is a real tradeoff.** L1 can foreclose. Fate and Cortex give players levers. Boundless trusts fiction. Neither is wrong; they serve different tables.
- **5e's kludge is cross-category, not difficulty.** A simple single-category creature (zombie) rolls out cleanly in 5e. Fragmentation bites Agnes (item + monster) and Malisondre (five subsystems).
- **Boundless runs closest to the fiction.** The minimal block forces the GM's imagination to do what rules text does elsewhere. This is a feature and a risk simultaneously.

## 9. Worked Examples On File

*Status: ⬜ UNVERIFIED*

*Seven creatures, complete in L1/5e/Fate/Cortex. Boundless drafted for three (Pell, Hanged Man, Zombie). Shadowdark and Daggerheart pending. See `work_remaining.md` for current state.*

- **Old Pell, the Drowned** - Touched (living). On-ramp. Desperate Plea = 4-die ghost horde defense. *The tutorial.*
- **The Hanged Man of Gallows Hill** - Anchored, mindless hazard. 4 lines in L1. Weather not monster. 1HP in Boundless. *Trapped in form.*
- **Zombie** - Forced Halfling. Heedless of Harm. 3 Armor in Boundless. Mercy in a stat block. *Forced into (broken) form.*
- **Agnes Under a Bushel** - Anchored, Level 2. Compounds to terrifying. Curse-to-companion arc. "Win slowly." *Bound to form.*
- **Sgt. Aldous Vane** - Forced, Level 4. Relentless. Authored seam: Social Camouflage 2. Party may be the quarry. *Forced into borrowed form.*
- **Malisondre, Lieutenant of Tahanos** - Anchored apex. 24-die pool. Sylph 2 as authored seam. Situation not encounter. *Free of form.*
- **The Reincarnate** - Born (living). Gentle close. Previous life as thread not leash. *The bracket close.*

**Section arc:** Pell (Touched/living) → Hanged Man → Agnes → Vane → Malisondre → Reincarnate (Born/living). Two living souls bracketing the dead; ascending relationship to form between them.
