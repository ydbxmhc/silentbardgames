# Averond Setting Summary

## The Fantasy Layer
**Averond** appears as a world dominated by the visible Arch of Heaven supported by massive Pillars, where gods move in chariots of fire. Technology reflects late Renaissance advancement with clockwork, dirigibles, and sophisticated vessels, but steel is sacred to Carterion and controlled by his temples.

**Political Structure:** Independent city-states ruled by immortal Saints (demigods), each with unique personalities and supernatural portfolios.

**The Greater Gods (in Vaults of Heaven):**
- **Carterion:** God of Craft, skill, creation (temples serve as libraries/academies/guildhalls)
- **Rana:** Goddess of Beauty, Air, Caprice
- **Susheil:** Goddess of Secrets, Water, All That Swims  
- **Grolikarnigan:** God of Deep Places, Earth, Wood, Silence
- **Rikitak:** Demon God of Fire, Strife, Mischief, Magic, Healing

**Magic:** Primarily conjuring with elemental spirits (air, water, earth/wood, fire). Fire spirits are demons; others are fae court members.

## The Science Fiction Reality
**The Charter Members:** The "gods" are colonial expedition survivors:
- Ian Carter (Carterion): Ship's engineer, designed Heaven AI
- Groli Karnigan (Grolikarnigan): Terraforming specialist
- Susan Sheil (Susheil): Life support specialist
- Becky Rana (Rana): Ship's pilot
- Richard Takahashi (Rikitak): Ship's psychologist

**Infrastructure:** Terraformed planet with orbital ring, colony ship mounted in ring, planetary nanite ecosystem maintaining terraforming and AR network. Every human has brain nanites making AR indistinguishable from reality.

**Immortality Technology:** Progressive nanotech enhancement including longevity, regeneration, enhancement, and backup/restoration through brain-scanning networks.

**The Saints:** Mostly first-generation colonists with longevity tech governing cities with synthetic citizens (often unaware of their artificial nature).

## Current Developments
**Divine Modifications:** Charter Members have extensively modified themselves:
- **Susheil:** Aquatic adaptations, sonar, water-breathing
- **Rana:** Magnetic flight, lightning control, cerulean blue appearance with aurora eyes
- **Grolikarnigan:** Can appear as walking tree of bark/stone with crystals
- **Carterion:** Herculean smith form, mostly appears in prerecorded dreams
- **Rikitak:** Maintains consistent human appearance - thin man in red robes with flame-blue eyes

**The System of Justice:** Carter built limitations into Heaven AI to minimize suffering. Cosmic justice system maintained with invisible "spirits of the air" dispensing rewards/punishments. Prayer stations in Carterian temples monitor Celestial language, with priests translating and ensuring honest needs are met.

**The Ship of Theseus Problem:** Deep existential questions about identity after countless backup/restoration cycles. Rikitak appears to be the conscience of the group, potentially the anchor point questioning what humanity means in their posthuman state.

## Setting Publication Concept
- **"Averond - World of Gods and Wonders":** Pure fantasy sourcebook
- **"Secrets of Averond (Sci-Fi Addition):"** Optional layer revealing the technological reality
- Most population is now "real" (descendants of low-berth passengers and AI-clone children)
- Agents scattered throughout magistrates, bazaars, temples
- Benevolent panopticon where prayers are answered and justice exists, just through posthuman mechanisms rather than divine ones
