## The Project

### What We're Building
A multi-system bestiary/grimoire for Averond, released as **separate Court booklets** (The Dead, The Demon Court, The Fae Courts, The Elemental Courts, etc.) each usable standalone, each containing:
- System-agnostic creature descriptions (the creature IS the description; stat blocks are translations)
- Stat blocks in **seven systems**: Level One · D&D 5e · Fate Core · Cortex Prime · Boundless · Shadowdark · Daggerheart
- GM craft notes and design philosophy woven throughout

### The Larger Product Architecture
These booklets slot into a **boxed set product line** (sequential, each building on the last):
- **Core rules booklet** (simplified L1 dice and Roles overview)
- **World overview booklet** (Averond)
- **Regional booklet** (Crossroads West)
- **Location booklet** (Saltwind Harbor - small coastal fishing/shipping village)
- **Role booklet** (starting with Warrior - full rules, Maneuvers, annotated story, sample characters at various starting Karma)
- **Sample characters booklet** (multi-system, same characters in all seven systems - also a conversion guide)
- **Adventure booklet** (tuned to party capability, increases with box number)
- **Physical components** (maps, tokens, clear plastic overlays for grids/hexmaps, dry-erase pen, possibly dice)

### Scope Expansion (New)
The project now explicitly includes **character examples across systems**. Possibly a **game jam style open call** for experienced players of various systems - not decided yet, but keep in mind when building templates.

### Boundless Notes (Key Mechanics)
- **Only players roll** - Position affects PC rolls, never creature rolls
- **Conditions** fill inventory Slots; must be resolved in fiction
- **Pools** (Companion, Morale, Strain, Event) erode on Misses
- **Fortune Rolls** for uncertain GM-side outcomes
- **1HP** for pure hazards (Hanged Man) signals "not a combat"
- **3 Armor** solves "Heedless of Harm" without custom rules (zombie)
- **Boundless Pell** has a 4-die Desperate Plea attack where the Dead defend him

## Key Design Principles (Summary)

*Full version in Lessons Learned doc.*

- **The description is the creature**; stat blocks are translations of the same truth
- **Seeds, not specimens** - entries give the minimum facts to rebuild the creature at the table
- **Format follows function** - simple hazards stay lean; complex agents earn the full mold
- **Level measures force, never threat** in L1 - Agnes at Level 2 compounds to terrifying
- **Authored seams** - every creature has a deliberate weakness (Malisondre's Sylph 2)
- **Tier calibration** - compare tiers not numbers across systems; check shape not just tier
- **Only players roll in Boundless** - position/conditions affect PC dice, not creature dice
- **The attacker chooses Harm** in L1 - one mechanic runs the whole escalation ladder
