*Redundancy pass, first bite. The L1 / Fate / Cortex prose beats below are reduced

## Level One - factored prose  (replaces ~394-444; keep stat block 382-392)

Agnes usually only rolls Boosts - almost always unopposed DL0, creating situational
dice for other rolls. When she opposes an action directly and wins, she imposes a
Hindrance, or rarely a Hook - psychological and subtle enough the victim won't notice.

*She distracts.* Display to Boost the opposition against anyone searching (unless a
lone, suitable host). Free to work directly, she rolls Possession to impose the Hook
*Keep It Secret*; winning the opposed roll prevents that action for the exchange (a
"stray thought"). Thereafter she imposes *Keep It Safe* as a Hindrance that does
nothing but resist her further rolls on that victim. Both go on the sheet; the GM
offers Karma when the Imposed Hook triggers, letting the player adopt it.

*She erodes.* She picks the lowest EL (counting leverageable Hooks), following a
victim until they sleep and imposing the Hook - then, a later night, a Hindrance -
while they resist at base Level only. Each Hook rank marks a behavior change; each
Hindrance point earns more frequent hints to the player.

*She helps.* She rolls **Boosts**; the bearer "just gets" extra dice, with Shade/Sneak
Synergy where apt. Encourage self-Hooking (Karma and Tokens, and the flag it raises).

*Her grip compounds.* The Hindrance is **uncapped**. The deeper her hold, the weaker
the defense; left long enough, resistance *evaporates* and she does as she pleases.

*She takes hold.* Even one rank of *Keep It Secret* is a foothold. The player chooses
whether to rank it up - harder to resist, easier to detect (their flag to the party) -
and may tap it when their own greed or secrecy serves. Be generous with self-Hooking.

*She drives.* Once *Keep It Safe* overcomes all their default EL, hand the player a
copy of her.

*She panics.* If the player pays a Token to reject the Hook and warn the party, or
anyone moves to counter and *remove* the Hindrance, she rages and attempts forced
Possession; if her Possession Attacks Take the victim Down, she takes control.

## Fate Core - factored prose  (replaces ~466-504; keep stat block 448-464)

*She distracts.* Deceive for the glamour; she usually keeps a situational Aspect with
a free invoke already banked.

*She erodes.* On a sleeping victim, a Provoke Create an Advantage places *Keep It
Secret*, which the GM Compels to stop them telling anyone.

*She helps.* Mostly a *Teamwork* bonus, plus CaA rolls whose free invokes she passes
to the bearer - a renewable source of Fate points. Encourage self-Compels.

*Her grip compounds.* *Keep It Secret* doesn't expire while they hold the barrette,
even hidden and away. At least once a night she rolls Provoke to bank another free
invoke, hoarding them; as they exceed the victim's Will, behavior changes grow
stronger and more frequent.

*She takes hold.* The player chooses whether to accept her Teamwork and passed invokes
- using them makes her easier to detect (their flag). They may self-Compel. Be
generous with self-Compels.

*She drives.* Once the invokes stack up, Compel the bearer to slip away and fondle it,
more and more often.

*She panics.* If the player pays a fate point to reject a Compel and warn the party,
or anyone tries to Overcome and *remove* the *Keep It Secret* Aspect, she rages and
attempts forced Possession (Provoke to Take Out). A player who Concedes must
accommodate her victory - still possession, but on the PC's terms, and the surest way
to deny her their skills and memories.

## Cortex Prime - factored prose  (replaces ~535-565; keep stat block 510-534)

Agnes usually only creates Assets to pass to the bearer.

*She distracts.* She rolls to oppose any search.
`>> almost the entire original beat was shared story - consider dropping this header
and folding the line into the pool/Asset rules.`

*She erodes.* On a sleeping victim she offers the *Keep It Secret* Complication: refuse
it and they wake in bed; take it and they keep the dream - a forlorn child bringing
feathers and ribbons.

*She helps.* She creates **Assets** the bearer "just gets."

*Her grip compounds.* She spends PP (the bearer's, or the GM's pool) for Influence
dice; the fuller the pool, the deeper her hold and the more the bearer's behavior
shows it. She hoards it, spending only to Sleepwalk them once it is large enough
(GM's discretion).

*She takes hold.* Against exposure she opposes every attempt, stepping up *Keep It
Secret* and adding pool dice to win. Shrinking the pool halts the Sleepwalking until
it regrows.

*She drives.* Step the Complication past d12 and she seizes control; hand the player a
copy.

*She panics.* If the player insists on warning others, or anyone tries to remove or
reduce *Keep It Secret*, she rages and attempts forced Possession - stepping the
Complication up to Take the victim Out.

## Candidates to promote to the agnostic "How She Works"

*Facts repeated identically in all three blocks - they're system-agnostic and belong
once, up top:*

- **She has no Manifestation.** She works only through a host, touching Mundis solely
  through the bearer (glamours aside). Currently restated in L1, Fate, and Cortex -
  state it once in the agnostic section. *(The 5e Agnes DOES manifest/possess via the
  item at near-death; keep that distinction there.)*

- **"If someone who can detect her is present, she stays hidden unless the barrette is
  threatened, then misleads/draws them away and escapes."** Near-verbatim in all three;
  it is already the agnostic *She distracts*. Drop it from every block.

- **The self-Hook / self-Compel "raise a flag, be generous" beat** is the same idea in
  each; the agnostic already carries the clue-mandate, so each block needs only its
  own verb (self-Hook / self-Compel), not the reasoning.

Fate and Cortex lose the most (their beats were mostly shared story with thin unique
mechanics); L1 shrinks least (it carries the real machinery - imposed Hooks/Hindrances,
Karma, the uncapped compounding). Nothing mechanical was cut - only the story that now
lives once, above.
