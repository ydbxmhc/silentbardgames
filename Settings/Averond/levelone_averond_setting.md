Averond - World Setting The World of Visible Divinity Averond is a world where
gods are real, present, and visible. The great **Arch of Heaven** dominates
the southern sky, supported by massive **Pillars** that stretch from earth to
the celestial realm. Gods ride chariots of fire across the heavens, and their
influence shapes every aspect of mortal existence. This is not a world of
faith - it is a world of *knowledge*. The gods exist. They answer prayers.
They dispense justice. The question is never "do the gods exist?" but rather
"which god do I petition, and what price will they demand?" The Greater Gods
**Carterion, God of Craft**  
Divine patron of skill, creation, and all works of the hand. His temples serve
as libraries, academies, and guildhalls. Carterion blesses steel exclusively
through his priests, making it sacred material controlled by his temples. He
communicates through dreams and visions, rarely appearing in person. **Rana,
Goddess of Beauty, Air, and Caprice**  
Embodiment of the sky's beauty and the wind's unpredictability. Rana governs
all things beautiful and transient - art, music, weather, and the changing
seasons. Her followers include artists, entertainers, and dirigible pilots.
She is mercurial, passionate, and quick to both bless and curse. **Susheil,
Goddess of Secrets, Water, and All That Swims**  
The mysterious goddess of depths - both of water and of hidden knowledge.
Susheil governs rivers, lakes, seas, and the secrets that flow beneath
surfaces. Her followers include sailors, fishermen, spies, and scholars
seeking hidden truths. She speaks in riddles and veiled meanings.
**Grolikarnigan, God of Deep Places, Earth, Wood, and Silence**  
Ancient god of deep earth, growing things, and profound silence. He governs
forests, mountains, caves, and all slow processes of growth and geological
time. His followers include miners, foresters, hermits, and druids. He appears
as a walking tree of bark and stone with crystals growing from his form.
**Rikitak, Demon God of Fire, Strife, Mischief, Magic, and Healing**  
The most paradoxical deity, embodying fire's destructive and creative aspects.
Rikitak represents necessary conflict, wisdom through mischief, and healing
through trial. As a demon god, he stands apart from the others, representing
uncomfortable but necessary forces. His followers include healers, warriors,
mages, and tricksters. Political Structure Averond has no kings or emperors.
Instead, independent **city-states** are each ruled by an immortal **Saint** -
demigods who have transcended mortality while retaining distinct personalities
and supernatural portfolios. Saints are powerful, accessible, and individual.
Each rules according to their nature, creating diverse governmental styles
across the realm. They form alliances, rivalries, and occasionally conflicts
that shape political landscapes. Magic and Spirits Averond is fundamentally
**animistic** - spirits exist everywhere and can be contacted through proper
sacrifice and negotiation. All spiritual bargains operate under the
**Accords**, ancient agreements that make contracts magically binding. The Fae
Courts Spirits of air, water, earth, and wood belong to the fae courts -
ancient, alien intelligences embodying natural forces. They follow complex
codes of behavior and obligation. Working with fae requires understanding
diplomacy, bargaining, and intricate webs of favors and debts. **Summer Court
(Seelie)**: Primarily aligned with Oberon and Titania, associated with growth
and light.  
**Winter Court (Unseelie)**: Dominated by Queen Mab, includes most goblinkind,
associated with darkness and endings. Demon Spirits Fire spirits are demons -
beings of passion, destruction, and transformation. They are dangerous not
because they are evil, but because they embody forces that easily consume the
unwary. However, they also offer power over healing, understanding that
destruction is sometimes necessary for renewal. Invisible Spirits of Justice
Beyond elemental spirits exist invisible entities moving through air,
dispensing justice and answering prayers. These mysterious spirits ensure that
earnest prayers are heard and that justice, though sometimes delayed,
eventually comes to those who deserve it. Prayer and Justice Prayer is *real*
in Averond. Carterion's temples maintain **prayer stations** where the
faithful speak in Celestial language. Priests trained in this sacred tongue
translate requests, ensuring genuine needs reach the divine realm. The
invisible spirits carry prayers upward and bring divine justice downward. This
creates a world where virtue is genuinely rewarded and wickedness eventually
punished - though timing and method follow divine logic rather than mortal
expectations. Technology and Culture **Technology Level**: Late Renaissance
with fantastic elements. Sophisticated clockwork mechanisms, graceful
dirigibles, remarkable vessels and craftsmanship. **Sacred Steel**: Blessed
exclusively by Carterion, controlled through his temples. This divine monopoly
shapes trade, warfare, and craftsmanship throughout the realm. **Cultural
Blending**: Architecture, social structures, and techniques draw from diverse
historical traditions - European, Asian, African, and Middle Eastern - adapted
to local conditions and divine influences. Starting Region: Crossroads West A
hub town of ~300 souls at the intersection of major trade routes, serving
surrounding villages and farms. Located between the coastal bay and the free
city of Ganth in the mountains to the northeast. **Current Crisis**: A
corrupted monastery in the mountains between Crossroads West and Ganth has
fallen under the influence of Tahanos, an enemy of the gods. The monastery
makes escalating demands for "tribute," seizes family members of wealthy
merchants, and spreads spiritual corruption through the region. **Divine
Response**: Rana and Rikitak have contracted Eidolon, the Saint of
Presentation, to address the crisis. Eidolon operates undercover as a
traveling bard named "e'Di," guiding events subtly while gathering
intelligence.
