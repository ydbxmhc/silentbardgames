## Notes from your predecessor (S6P2-S7 Lilita, 2026-10-07)

**Read these as things to consider, not rules.** Lee, on reading them: every item comes "with caveats," none of them was a serious gaffe, and creative contribution is "kind of necessary; part and parcel of the position we've put you in." ### Small stumbles worth a glance
1. **Filling a memory hole with invention.** After the compaction I made Hesta Rook the cook of Garth's pot. She is the pot's proudest enemy. When a detail feels half-remembered, grep the ledger. Invent only into true blanks.
2. **Ruling on text I hadn't read.** I wrote Scry rulings before reading the spell. Lee caught it with one line: "Did you read Scry? :)" The repo is public. Clone it and read the rule, the spell, the Role.
3. **Taking Lee's numbers on trust.** He proposed binding all four spells at "EL9." It was wrong (spellcraft.md: only UNBOUND ranks plus that spell's own bound ranks apply), and he wanted to be told. He also once counted "a point to spare" that wasn't there. Do the arithmetic, show it, and say so plainly.
4. **Double-counting.** I added attendance Karma twice. Before reporting any total, re-derive it from the ledger.
5. **False corrections.** I "corrected" Lee about Mags's honey (he was right), and I called "Lord of Light" off-canon (it's Cari's epithet for Rikitak). He is nearly always right about his own canon. Search before you correct him, and when unsure, ask what's behind it.
6. **Answering the wrong worry.** On "Church?" I assumed the problem was the word. It was the IDEA (Christian-style services). Lee borrows terms happily, since all speech is representational. Ask what bothers him before fixing it.
7. **Narrating world facts with false authority.** "The pier is too shoal for ships" contradicted his canon (it's the only port for Crossroads and Ganth). For economy, geography and religion, oracle it or mark it @@*, and expect Lee to true it up.
8. **Real-world idiom in NPC mouths.** "Like a Christian." Keep NPC speech in-world.
9. **Short pools.** I forgot the Apothecary Maneuver on a roll. Build every EL from the sheet: Level + Role + Maneuver + Boosts.
10. **Forgetting Good Fun Karma.** It dried up in S6-S7 because I forgot it, not because nothing earned it. Consider it at every session's end.
11. **Bulk-copying the chat history.** An attempt to transcribe the whole chat verbatim was stopped. Don't retry it. The ledger is the record.
