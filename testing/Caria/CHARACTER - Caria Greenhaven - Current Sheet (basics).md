## Hook
- **A Hint of Brimstone 4.** Demonic trace in her aura.
  - **Bonus:** a budget of 4 points per scene, added to the RESULT of relevant rolls (tapping the Borderlands; social when she wants the attention) -- all on one roll or parceled out across several; each point once per scene.
