Saltwind Harbor, on the coast south of Crossroads. Built 2026-10-07 from the cumulative S1-S7 ledger, the S06 cast card, the S1-S7 gap log, and the Mira-era Saltwind background (Feb 2026). Every line traces to one of those.

**Updated 2026-10-09 from the S08 ledger:** Saltwind's last night (Mirna's kitchen, Cobb's bees, the north rocks, the dawn goodbyes) and the first day on the road north. The road is new section 6: Tanner's Fords and the camp at the edge of the Court woods.

**This version is player-safe.** GM-only secrets stay in the ledger. Things Caria has learned in play appear only where they touch the village.

Language: everyone in Averond speaks languages we don't know, so the words here are representational, borrowed to deliver a feeling (chapel, sexton, Cari's Latin) [Lee]. "Lord of Light" is Cari's own epithet for Rikitak: fire makes light.

## 1. Saltwind at a glance

- **What it is:** a small fishing village and the ONLY port serving Crossroads and Ganth [Lee]. Southern ships call rarely: small ones (cog, caravel, sloop) that can thread the reef archipelago. Anything bigger anchors out and sends longboats.
- **Size:** about forty souls came to Brother Aubric's burial ("far more than a stranger-Brother draws"). Cari: "a town smaller than will support a lawyer." @@ The total population is unset. Forty mourners suggests something like 80-150 people, children included.
- **Living:** boats work fish, shellfish, seaweed and kelp. The herring run in autumn, a salt boiling in autumn, gutting and net-mending, a little shipping. No market: barter happens on the beach behind the warehouse.
- **No:** harbormaster, resident priest, or healer beyond Mira's trauma care. Brother Aubric was the coast's circuit healer for thirty-one springs.
- **Has:** a cooper and a smith (unnamed @@*), a boatwright (boats, not ships) [Lee], a little tanning for boot and harness leather [Lee] (nobody tans anything finer), a sexton, and a two-man watch.
- **Mood when Cari came:** defiant, close-knit, proud of driving off the Monastery priest, and watching every stranger. By the time she left, the village was split between Hesta Rook's version of her and its own.
- **Growing things:** thin soil; the salt wind burns everything. Anything grows only behind walls @@*.
- **Faith:** Susheil (Karinna's well-house), Rana (her shrine on the east bluff [Lee], and a wayside niche at the dead tower's foot; Mira tends both), a small nondenominational chapel above the harbor where the dead are honored and prepared [Lee], and spirits in daily life: Shimmer in a rain-barrel, the Hanged Man on the hill.

## 3. Places

### The waterfront
- **The Pier.** Built for shipping [Lee]. During Cari's stay only fishing boats tied up. The square lies at its landward end, and the well-house at its root.
- **The Square.** The town common: the open ground between the dock and the lodge, where the North Road meets the pier and the Coast Road passes through [Lee].
- **Harbor street and the lanes.** Cobbled ("Get up off my cobbles. I'm too old to watch young folk kneel in fish-scale." - Karinna). The houses run up the harbor street from the well-house. "The lane" runs out toward Mira's, and from it the dark tower on Gallows Hill shows against the stars.
- **The Warehouse.** Shipping storage, east of the pier [Lee]. Inside @@*:
  - tarred nets and dry rope
  - Korrin's six kegs of iron nails, waiting for a southern ship [Lee]
  - two dozen Corvane salt crocks, stacked at the back since late summer and never collected ("bad luck," says Bett)
  - the net-menders' common box of waxed thread and beeswax cakes

  Harl keeps the key: "back on his nail by dark" @@*. Cobb mends nets in its lee in the afternoons.
- **The barter beach.** Behind the warehouse. A plain path runs east and west along the shore.
- **Korrin's yard and the packing sheds** @@*. At the harbor's east end: a yard office with a stove, a well, and sheds where caravan cargo keeps. The view from the yard takes in Rana's shrine on the east bluff, the well-house, the chapel, and the Teeth far off to the west. (RETCON: S04 put the shrine on the "west bluff.")
- **The try-pots** @@*. Below the warehouse. Old Pask renders fish oil for lamps there: it stinks, but it makes good rubbing oil.
- **The salt boiling-sheds** @@*. This year's boiling sits packed and half-paid. Corvane's agent paid half and never came back, and the boilers are still cursing him.
- **The gutting tables.** Where the fishwives work, Annet Fallow among them. Day's end means livers, heads and scale for anyone who asks nicely. Jory's liver-oil pot goes "downwind."
- **The racks.** Fish-drying racks stand east, behind the warehouse [Lee]; net racks stand by the lodge. Old Corra lives "past the racks" (which ones? @@).
- **The pens and the tanning** [Lee]. East, behind the warehouse: animal pens, and a little tanning for boot and harness leather. Nothing grand. RETCON: Karinna's "no tanner" now means nobody tans anything finer.
- **The harbor wall, watch post and north rocks.** Harl and Davey keep the watch. Harl was on this wall at sixteen, the night the Kittiwake went down. The best laver grows on the north rocks, west of the watch post, at low water. The watch "will look the other way" for a little lad picking weed at night. On the S08 midnight run Jory slipped on wet weed reaching one-handed and wrenched the bad shoulder; Min got him up, and the knit held.
- **The harbor shingle.** Below the chapel yard, where boats are drawn up. Cari sat in the surf here after the burial, and Jem saw the sea for the first time.

### Susheil's well-house (Karinna's shrine)
- A low stone well-house by the pier's root, with a blue-painted lintel @@*. Inside, steps go down to a basin with a blue-painted rim, shells and small offerings.
- Outside: Karinna's bench by the well-curb, a rope and bucket (Nell draws the water and Karinna lets folk think it's training), and the sea-wall beside it. Karinna has a loft, and a brass mortar "older than the well-house."
- It is a deep, old, wet anchor in the Borderlands. Asher couldn't get near it. "Water doesn't lie to me."
- @@ The water's source: the basin was "fed by the sea through a channel," but later Karinna draws "Susheil's spring, water that has never failed." (Both? A spring that rises at the sea's edge?)

### The Lodge
- **The hall.** On the north side of the square, by the North Road [Lee]. A long timber-and-stone hall with a central fire-pit, a smoke-hole, trestles and benches, a fire bench, seaward shutters, and three high lamps on the crossbeam that Mirna can't reach without the stool @@*.
  - Mirna tends the cookfire, and Asher may tend it "when the widow asks."
  - It held the burial meal for Aubric.
  - **Garth's winter pot** has been served here nightly since Day 1. On Cari's last night Mirna let her "show off a little": Stone Shape turned a yard flagstone into a scourer, and the pot came out bright iron, clean to the pits. Mirna re-oiled it herself, and left an oily handprint on Cari's cheek.
  - The grain under the ambergris agreement will be stored here under Mirna's key.
- **Mirna's back kitchen.** Off the kitchen passage: a banked hearth, a crane holding Garth's huge iron pot, a drying rack, a scrubbed table, a pantry with scales, and the high shelf by the salt box ("Nobody touches my shelf. Not even the cat."). The yard door takes sea spray.
  - It was Cari's medicine workshop, with the door propped open on Mirna's orders: "answer to Hesta in plain sight."
  - The kitchen council (S6) and the ambergris signing (S7) both happened here.
- **The kitchen step.** Anything left on it at dusk goes in the pot. Jory skims liver oil here over a brazier.
- **The yard.** The yard wall, Mirna's bench in the sun, the woodpile, and the midden (Tam has landed in it twice). The yard step's loose flagstone used to rock underfoot; since S08 it sits flat and clean @@*. The goats live here, including the billy, who still has one untrimmed hoof.
- **Garth's old kitchen patch** @@*. Behind the lodge, gone to nettles since he drowned. Nettles make spring greens.

### Around the lodge
- **The lodge huts.** Thatched, clustered around and behind the lodge. A winter cough was starting there when Cari arrived.
- **The Fallow hut** @@*. Annet's home, by the lodge huts. It was drafty with a dead hearth until Cari and Asher gave it a true fire. Cari left most of her belongings on Annet's shelf.
- **The brook and the willow stand** @@*. Behind the lodge. Hal cuts the willow for creels. The caravan's eleven wagons camp in a ring by the brook [Lee].

### Up the hill
- **The chapel** [Lee]. "Squat, above the harbor." A small NONDENOMINATIONAL house of the dead: here the village honors its dead, washes and prepares them for burial, and holds the funeral rites, calling on whichever god the dead or the mourners keep. It has no regular services and no homilies; this is not that kind of culture, least of all in a remote village. Kade washed and wrapped Aubric here.
- **The chapel yard** [Lee: "chapel yard," not churchyard, for the subtle difference]. Old Tobin's. It sits on the hill, up the chapel yard lane, inside a wall with a gate. Brother Aubric lies just inside the gate, facing the sea. Harl's Jenna is "eleven years on the hill," presumably here.

### Inland
- **The North Road** [Lee]. Comes down through the woods from Ganth and Crossroads, straight through the village to the pier. Ganth is three weeks each way by wagon @@*.
- **The Coast Road** [Lee]. Runs east-west through the village: west toward Mira's and Gallows Hill, east toward the works and the bluffs.
- **The Gate** @@*. Where the North Road enters the village. The caravan was met here by the watch. @@ Is there a wall or palisade, or just a gate-post and a habit?
- **Woods north of the gate.** Chicken-of-the-woods, rose hips at the woodland edge, late elderberries, juniper, rowan and yew. The forest itself runs to spruce, fir, pine and cedar, with salt-stunted oak and maple. Good mushrooms and deadly ones both: don't forage without Naturalist.
- **Cobb's hollow** @@*. Past the gate, where the old oak came down: its root-plate stands up like a wall with a lee behind it. Four skeps of coiled straw sit on a plank shelf on stones, each with Maud's little burned bee and a thatched straw cap. Everyone knows, and nobody says.
  - **Since S08 a brownie keeps them.** THIMBLE (a use-name, not its True Name) was called here by Cari and struck an Agreement with Cobb under the Accords, with Cari standing surety. It keeps the skeps healthy, hidden and safe, may frighten thieves while they trespass, and minds the children. Its wage is a thimble of honey from each crock. Its glamour hides the skeps from strangers, but it can't make the village forget what it already knows.

### West of the village
- **The cliff path.** It runs west from the village, past the west cove, out to Mira's. Steep in parts. Bett walked it by lantern past midnight with a choking child.
- **Mira's cottage** [Lee]: west, between the Teeth and the village, a short walk outside the village proper, and not easily seen.
  - GM detail @@*:

    - a poor house: one black iron pot, a quern-mortar, a few crocks, no milk, no salve.
  - Scurvy-grass roots are planted along the south wall. A stream runs below the cottage.
- **The west cove.** Below the cottage, down a steep path. Tide-pools, wet sand, and the Teeth black offshore.
  - Mira leaves flowers in a rock cleft here for Mirna's two drowned husbands [Lee]. Cari doesn't know whose they are.
  - The dawn turn of the tide is "when the sea breathes out" @@*.
  - Old Bellows' coal was quenched here. "The sea is not Shimmer's, but the edge of it is."
- **Gallows Hill** (the west bluff):
  - **The path** zigzags up through late gorse.
  - **The gibbet stump** sits halfway up: a weathered post-butt in a ring of stones. Hollis stopped dead there by daylight. In the Borderlands there's a black root under it, and above it a hollow shaped like a hanging man, "empty by day, like a coat on a peg."
  - **The Hanged Man** is a specter pleading for mercy, for mother, for moments. He never shows by daylight [Lee].
  - **Rana's wayside niche** sits at the foot of the old tower. It is NOT her shrine proper, which is on the east bluff [Lee]. This one is the lighthouse keepers' own little altar, and the sailors'. It's built of fallen tower-stones, not a proper cairn: a niche of fallen tower-stones holding a worn figure with face and arms lifted, swept by Mira, and left shells, gull feathers and a faded blue sailor's knot @@*. Harl has climbed up here for forty-one years with spirits and tobacco. On Day 2 the wind carried Cari's gorse flowers INLAND, against the sea wind, and Harl had never seen that before.
  - **The dead Light** [Lee: an old abandoned lighthouse on this coast]. It has been dark forty-one years, since the winter the Kittiwake sank @@*. It was the reef light for southern shipping @@*.
    - The oak door is swollen half-open, wide enough to squeeze through but not walk in.
    - Inside: a round ground room, a stone stair spiraling up toward the open lantern room, gull feathers and old bracken and sheep droppings, a cold hearth, and an iron ring in a floor flag.

    - Old Tobin's ewes always sheltered here in a blow. Since about the turn of the moon they won't go in.
    - Mirna says don't go up alone.
  - **The Keeper's walled garden** @@*: the best walls on the coast, under the dead Light. Nobody has set foot inside in forty years.
  - **The ledge over the Teeth** sits on the far side of the hill, where the Kittiwake went down. Below it, a shingle where Cari washed her face in Susheil's water and Harl came down to her.
- **The Gallows Teeth.** A shattered boulder field running out into the surf: crevasses, sharp rock, fast tides. It lies offshore west, beyond the cove.
  - The Warden washed up here. The Kittiwake was lost here, and Annet's man two winters ago.
  - There are caves under the Teeth that Calder wanted searched.
  - @@ Is it one of the reef archipelago's "problem reefs"?

## 4. People

Households first, then the rest. Ages are given only where stated or inferable.

### Mira's cottage
- **Mira Thousand-Voices** [Lee]. Wandering hedge shrine-keeper, unordained. "Thousand-Voices is what they call me when they're being polite. Mira will do."
  - Sighted, barefoot, sea-weathered, late twenties @@*, with quick net-mender's hands.
  - Lends her voice to spirits and the dead. Carries the village's prayers. Tends both of Rana's sites. Trusted, though some whisper "warlock."
  - Faced down Calder. Burned the first ghola on the beach with e'Di and Shimmer.
  - Aubric set her arm when she was nine.
  - Hosted Cari; told her "the tide bring you home."
  - **End of S07:** at home, with the Fallows asleep in her back room. She'll wake Min for the midnight laver run.
  - **S08:** at the farewell she slipped her HAG-STONE into Cari's coat pocket, wrapped in net-twine. Cari found it on the road [Lee: it was a gift].
- **Min** (Min Caratosh'qi-te, "Little Target"). A young goblin under a formal Accords agreement as Mira's assistant: he keeps the fire, mends the cottage, helps cook and keeps watch, in return for food, shelter, teaching and protection.
  - One ear, one cataracted eye, a club foot, and a good eye of sapphire shot with gold and silver.
  - Nocturnal, prissy about doors, sleeps clutching a jar of ghee. Talks in pidgin, until he doesn't.
  - The village tolerates him; it hasn't accepted him. "Min no go big-house. Many eyes."
  - He coined "Everybody give pot. Pot give everybody." @@*
  - **End of S07:** full of fish-head soup, asleep at the table, due at the north rocks at midnight with Jory.
  - Goblins stand about three feet tall, maybe four; Min is an adolescent [Lee].
  - **S08:** got Jory up off the rocks and toward Mira's ("Min say SHUT MOUTH"), saved two-thirds of the laver from the tide, and saw Jory to the blue door. Struck an Agreement with Cari: he eats soup for her every night while she's gone, and Asher visits to remind him. "Min not on list. Min here. Min get love direct."

- **Shimmer** [Lee]. An undine. Lives in the rain-barrel and rides at Mira's wrist. Paid in secrets and gossip. "The sea is not hers, but the edge of it is." Has a soft spot for Mirna's house.

### Susheil's well-house
- **Karinna** [Lee]. Blind elder, keeper of Susheil's shrine, speaks Celestial, the spiritual anchor of the council. Knows people by their step and their smell. A dry, fair, unafraid old coast woman @@*.
  - Her hands are arthritic from sixty years of salt and rope @@*. Cari set her a regimen: a warm brine soak every night (Nell makes her tell a different story each time), willowbark tea and rosemary oil.
  - Her verdict on Cari: "You're no spy of that mountain. You're something it made and couldn't keep."
  - Gave Cari a flask of Susheil's spring water for the road.
  - **End of S07:** climbed to the lodge for the last supper, which took near an hour and three rests. Her goodbye with Cari was cut short.
  - **S08:** too spent to go home, she slept on the lodge fire bench with her head in Nell's lap and Asher keeping her warm. At first grey she was off to draw her own first bucket, as every morning. She found Mirna's handprint on Cari's cheek ("Then you've been blessed") and gave her a Keeper's word for the road: "When you're not sure what you are, wash your face in it. She'll tell you." Her hands were softer and less stiff; she'd used the salve.
- **Nell** @@*. About ten, Karinna's guide. Quietly draws the water. Stares at Cari's white lock. Reports that EVERYONE is talking. Gave Cari an egg.

### The Rook house

## 5. Village life

### Trade and goods
- **The port** [Lee]. Korrin's iron nails were commissioned from Ganth ore and wait in the warehouse for a southern ship. Iron is rare and precious; steel is sacred to Carterion.
- **Boats** are the boatwright's work, with no nails and little or no silk.
- **Salt:** the autumn boiling ("salt is cheap in a town called Saltwind"). The Corvane payment is half unpaid @@*.
- **Lamp oil** is southern and comes from traders, about 3 copper a flask @@*. Fish oil comes from Old Pask's try-pots; liver oil is now Jory's trade.
- **Caravans bring** grain, cloth, pottery, iron, spirits, and freshwater salt fish for inland buyers and ships' stores.
- **Shortages:** no tanner, no poppy or hemp, no market. Ganth is three weeks each way by wagon @@*.

### Garth's winter pot
- **History.** Garth kept it. When he drowned, nobody would relight it under Mirna's nose, and pride called it "trough-eating."
  - Jory asked, and Mirna said yes. Tam hauled it out and washed it.
  - The first night: a clear fish-head broth, chicken-of-the-woods fried in goose fat, thyme, bay, juniper, livers at the end, and Mirna's onions.
- **Mirna's rule:** "Bowls at full dark. Anybody can come. Nobody asks who put in what. That was always the rule." Food left on the kitchen step at dusk goes in.
- **Supply:** heads and livers from the gutting tables; the woods; Min and Jory's greens; whatever folk drop in.
- **The pot keeps the count.** Min's Accords reading of it, which Mira will be carrying up to Mirna.
- **Lee's design note:** the pot is "pride and warmth and a bowl of evidence that someone actually cares."

### The ambergris paper (QUIET: the village does not know)
- Harl's 1 1/4 lb of grey ambergris goes with Korrin to a perfumer in Ganth. Korrin takes a carrier's tenth, openly.
- Korrin advances winter grain from the first market on the road and sends it back by a returning wagon. Mirna holds it under her key, and it goes out through the pot "without question of who or whence."
- The remainder is Harl's to direct.
- Marks: Harl's fouled anchor, Korrin's name, Mirna's crossed pot, Cari as witness, Fennick as clerk. The original stays with Mirna.

### Customs and sayings
- **Aubric's price** lives on: feed the hungry at your back door.
- **Rikitak etiquette** [Lee]: never ASK a priest of the Order for help. Say someone is hurt.
- **"Met as paid"** [Lee]: two fingers laid on an open palm. It's a greeting between parties under the Accords meaning "we meet square, nothing owed either way; I see you; terms will be kept." Min uses it.
- **Folk charms** [Lee, as Cari taught them]: salt for the dead, silver for demons, iron for fey. They need intent and commitment and are usually enough to thwart a small spirit. A threshold works against any spirit if you have a right to it and believe it. Garlic is no better than roses, but neither is useless. (Saltwind's fishwives knew the salt ring long before Cari.)
- **Burials** @@*: a meal at the lodge, barley and fish from Mirna's kettles; the speaker throws the first earth.
- **The breath-count** ("one... two... three") went from Cari to Bett, Jory and Mira. Karinna wants every mother on the lane to learn it.
- **Sayings** @@*:
  - "a rule written in drowned men"
  - "They'll hold." (the highest praise for knots)
  - "A net is a lot of holes tied together with string." (the oldest joke on the coast)
  - "And the tide bring you home."
- **Cari's own farewell** [Lee]: "Till the embers rekindle."

### Gossip
- Davey carries the news, along with Nell and the neighbors' children.
- Bett's word carries at the lodge. Karinna and Bett talking is why forty came to the burial.
- **Hesta Rook** goes house to house. "Half the village listens to Hesta; the other half asks who's spoken to the girl themselves."
- Kade: "Gossip you fight, you feed."

### Food
- **Everyday:** fish and barley; fried gurnard; barley bread; sea-beet; small beer; goats' milk; mussels; drying fish "for the winter."
- **The problem:** a widow's winter diet of salt fish and barley brings winter-sickness.
- **Greens:** laver and dulse off the rocks at low water ("old folk ate them; young ones think it's poor-man's food"); scurvy grass, sea fennel and wrack on the cliffs; nettles in Garth's patch; rose hips past the cove; barley sprouts.
- **Honey:** only Cobb's.

### Health, and the medicines Cari left
- **Sickness this autumn:** croup (Daw, Lissa), the winter cough in the lodge huts, early winter-sickness (Annet), Karinna's hands, Jory's collarbone, hands torn by nets.
- **Left behind:**
  - elder-thyme-honey cough syrup
  - rose-hip honey, a course for Annet
  - juniper hand salve
  - willow-bark tincture (steeping about three weeks; not for small children or bleeding gums)
  - scurvy-grass vinegar at Mira's (ready in about a week)
  - horehound drying at Mira's

### Seasons and tides
- **Late autumn.** The grain is needed before snow. The herring run thins in three to four weeks @@*.
- **Tides:** Day 2's low water came a little past noon, so the next fell just after midnight.
- **The noon bell** marks the day. Dusk is bee-quiet time. Sunrise comes up behind the cliffs and lights the Teeth gold.

## 6. The road north (S08)

### The route
- **Northbound:** Saltwind, up the North Road through the woods, to Tanner's Fords (about half a day, midday). Then on uphill into older wood, to camp at the near edge of the Court woods (dusk). Beyond lie the Weeping Court, the fork, Crossroads and Ganth. Southbound in S3/S4 the order ran the other way. @@* The order is derived from S3/S4 timing; Lee to true up.
- **Ganth** is about three weeks from Saltwind by wagon @@*. The grain advance goes back from the first market.

### The caravan
- **Dasha Korrin's eleven wagons,** a mixed train. The heavy wagons are drawn by OXEN. Wagon one has had MULES since Saltwind: the off-ox went lame on the hill road down, and Korrin traded the pair [Lee approved: "mules since Saltwind, oxen before. Now we know why."].
- **Wagon one:** Korrin on the box, Cari, and Jem in the back on the grain sacks. **Wagon two:** Tam. Brother Kade, Hollis and Clerk Fennick ride with the train.
- **Korrin's rules:** "Light at night, eyes on the dark." No fire in a wagon full of grain and dry canvas. When she says "Jem," he jumps. Her brass dark-lantern (horn panes, sliding hood) goes with the watch [brass: Lee; iron isn't cheap].

### Tanner's Fords [Lee: plural; a thorpe off the road is welcome]
- **The wagon ford.** The river is wide and shallow over gravel, brown and quick. In autumn it runs knee-to-hub on the drovers' known line, firm going. On the bank is the old camp ground where the caravan stopped southbound [Lee]. Both times, Cari washed there behind a wagon with blankets strung up [Lee].
- **The tanners' foot-ford,** a little downstream, by the thorpe @@*.
- **The four black stumps** upstream are the tanners' bridge @@* (approved in principle). They raise one every summer and the thaw takes it every spring. Tibby's mam's rule: "Don't go out past the black posts. That's where it's deep."
- **The thorpe** @@*: a handful of turf roofs off the road between the two fords, with smoke, drying frames and the sour smell of the tan-pits. Leatherworkers. Cari thought of kitting Jem out here (jerkin, breeks, girdle, boots), then put it off to Crossroads.
- **Tibby** @@*, about six, a thorpe child with bare feet. She watched Cari talk to the river, bossed Jem through rigging the screen, and went home with a plait tied with half his sleeve-hem: "Thank you, Mes Cari!" (Cari wears the other half.)
- **The river.** Running water is alive; through the hag-stone it's simply busy being a river @@*. No named resident. Cari greeted it as a child of Susheil. It stilled a clear pocket for her bath and took the salt out of her yellow robe gladly. When Jem offered a drover's plain prayer, it rolled him a grey stone with an unbroken quartz ring, which he keeps in his shirt. [Lee: intention governs the Accords; probably no debt.]
- **Nothing at the Ford was ever paid.** The S3/S4 "teeth-coins" were silver held against the dead Aubric, not an offering to anything here [Lee].

### The camp at the edge of the Court woods (night of Day 3)
- **The clearing** @@*: the ring of eleven, with the picket line on the east side and water barrels by wagon one. The dark is deepest on the north-west side, behind wagons 4-6, toward the Court. The Court woods have an old hum, deep and watchful but not unfriendly.
- **An old caravan fire-ring** half-sunk in the turf at the east edge, and a mossy grey **boulder** half-buried at the treeline where the picket rope ties off @@*.
- **The picket cairn.** Cari stacked fire-ring stones by hand into a three-sided hood by the picket, open to the eastern trees. Moved stone stays put [Lee's Stone Shape ruling], so it needed no spell. It holds a persistent light that throws bright bars twenty paces in among the trunks. **It has an ember-red core and a faint sulphur tang**, and the whole watch saw it flare red. The oxen shied.
- **Lights:** Korrin's brass lantern (persistent), carried by the watch at the north-west wagons. Jem's tin lantern, hooded with a scrap of oiled leather and a twine hinge (persistent). Nothing over the middle of the ring: "I'd sooner not tell the whole wood where we're sleeping."
- **The downwind fire** at the west-southwest edge, where Asher took his ambergris offering in a little oak house of three crossed splits.
- **At the treeline** @@*: two eyes at fox-height, green-gold, unblinking, then gone. Hollis didn't stir.

### The Weeping Court
- **The House** [Lee]: she IS hospitality. She never meant Jem ill; it's beyond her to think that way, and it grieved her that he was slowly dying in her care. She is dying herself, slowly, over many mortal lifetimes, and she needs someone to take care of. Cari means to find her someone who can thrive there.
- **The Court hearth** burns with Cari's permanent fire (S03), and an anchor line runs from it to her. Asher visits; she likes the visit.
- **S08:** not visited. Korrin's terms were back by full dark, and Jem was a flat no. Jem wants her told he isn't angry. Asher reports that something came down the line from the north and knocked; the House did not open.
