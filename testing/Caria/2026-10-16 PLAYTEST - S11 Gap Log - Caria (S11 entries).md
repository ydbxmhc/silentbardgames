## 1. Tokens, Karma and the Hook

- **OK (Lee) -- Per-scene Token refresh.** One Token at the start of every scene if there is room (luck.md). The GM missed this for ten sessions. It is worth bolding in luck.md or the GM guide; the sheet's "4 per session" line is incomplete. The GM starts a session with 4 (players or highest PC Level) and gains 1 per scene.
- **OK (Lee) -- A scene is one camera shot.** Rapid moves between nearby sites in one continuous sequence are a single scene. The GM may break a scene where fairness calls for it (a recovery, a time skip). Breaking a long single-location scene with a flashback is also allowed. Scene breaks are a pacing tool, judged by fairness.
- **OK (Lee) -- Anyone can spend a Token on any roll.** Tokens are player-facing luck, narrated as will, luck or divine intervention. A player may even spend one on the GM's rolls against them. -> The Token text should say "any roll" outright. (The GM hesitated over a Token on Asher's roll.)
- **OK (Lee) -- Tokens can exceed the cap** when they come from rolls (Extreme successes) or GM kudos.
- **OK (Lee) -- NPC Extremes pay no Karma.** The free Token from an NPC's Extreme goes to the GM's pocket. extreme-results.md says that when opponents spend a Token against an Extreme, the roller earns an immediate Karma. Lee likes that reading but flags the rule as a whole for review. @@ OPEN
- **@@* GM Token principle.** Spend GM Tokens to keep an NPC in the story (avoiding an unearned Down or death), not to cancel a success the player has paid for.
- **OK (Lee) -- The Hook's per-scene budget is a hard ceiling.** Once the 4 points are spent, "leaning on the Hook" colours the fiction but adds no dice. (Ruled by the GM and accepted.)

## 2. Harm, recovery and illness

- **OK (Lee) -- Harm stays undifferentiated until the recovery roll.** Do not call it Injury beforehand. A Veil is usually a Hindrance or Complication. A fist is usually a Complication. A knife is just Harm until the roll allocates it. (The GM kept pre-labelling knife Harm as Injury.)
- **@@ OPEN -- The procedure for splitting undifferentiated Harm at recovery** needs to be written down. In play: the recovery roll's result was the portion that turned out to be Complication and cleared; the rest stayed as Injury.
- **OK (Lee) -- Intimidation is an attack.** A look, an accusation or an interrogation meant to frighten deals Harm as Complications. types-of-harm.md says so ("as good as a crack to the head"). The GM first ran it as a table of outcomes.
- **OK (Lee) -- Illusions deal Complications.** If you believe you are dead, you pass out. (Sever and the steward, Veil-fire.)
- **OK (Lee) -- Chronic illness is a permanent Hindrance on health rolls.** Only supernatural intervention clears it, though remission is possible. A won dawn heals, capped at 1 Injury per day, at least for chronic conditions.
- **OK (Lee) -- Care is an Apothecary roll whose effect becomes a Boost on the patient's recovery,** capped at the healer's Healer rank (the Persistent wager cap). Mundane gear can add +1 (the worry stone).
- **OK (Lee) -- Post-fight recovery.** The GM offers it after a fight or with medical care. Armor 1 grants a second one. A superficial cut is cleared once it is properly dressed.

## 3. Dice, opposition and commitment

- **OK (Lee) -- Dice committed to an action are unavailable for defense.** A character who sets none aside gets **no** defense roll. A character doing nothing always has their base dice. This is the frightening edge of the system, and it is why the Kamikaze Blitz works. -> It deserves a bold line in the combat/actions text with the Blitz as the example. (The GM gave Sever, surprised and all-in on his channel, a resistance roll; the resulting "fumble" was retracted, because he can't fumble a roll he couldn't have made.)
- **OK (Lee) -- Everything rolls.** A Difficulty is rolled as dice, and the two sides compare results. It is never a fixed target number. (The GM treated DL as a target twice in one exchange.)
- **OK (Lee) -- Free-action pre-existing Boost.** A factor already in the scene can be rolled as a free action if the GM agrees; its value adds to the attack EL (as extra dice). Used for the Prior's "morning walk," the conduit through Sever, and the collapse of Calder's cabal.
- **OK (Lee) -- The side effect of an Extreme success scales with the roll.** The steward's Extreme on 4 dice gave Boost 4, not 2. A player may propose a better side effect: Cari's Extreme push became "someone notices she's being merciful."
- **@@* Detection and effect are separate rolls.** One roll answers one question, and an Extreme on a reading roll does not bleed into whether the target noticed. (Lee's caution, accepted.)
- **@@ OPEN -- Excess effect against a destroyed barrier.** When a ward is broken with effect to spare, does the excess carry through to what it shielded? At the table the GM spent it on the door, not on Calder.
- **@@ OPEN -- Opposition vs resistance in a spiritual clash.** There is no free resistance after an all-in opposition; split first if you want both. And when an NPC is "a working," whose EL opposes? Worth a line in the Conjuror text.

## 4. Spellcraft

- **OK (Lee) -- Required Traits count toward Difficulty.** Scry always carries its required Traits. Playtest version: Insidious, Subtle, Persistent and Blanket are required, giving a minimum DL4. Guidable is optional (+1, DL5) "but it *greatly* increases the value of the spell." -> The Difficult-spell text should say plainly that required Traits count. (The GM assumed they were free.)
- **OK (Lee) -- The Improvisation hold.** The spell rolls its DL, and the caster rolls to Work the Improvisation. Spell Improvisation ranks count. Fail and the spell falls apart (a Bound spell releases its rank). Hold and the construction succeeds; the "casting" roll is then the seeking or targeting roll at a DL the GM sets. Scrying on your own location is Astral Projection.
- **OK (Lee) -- Dismissing is not Releasing.** Dismissing a standing effect gives nothing back, though the GM may ask for a roll to *end* it when that matters. Moving a Persistent, Guidable effect is free. *Attacking* with it costs an action. Release means unbinding: it spends the binding energy entirely, loses the spell until rebound, and dismisses any outstanding effects. That cost is what buys the free action. (The GM had called the Fax light a "drawn weapon" that could be Released for free.)
- **@@* Dawn rule.** A tie holds (dims by 0). An Extreme failure on the casting's own Dawn roll SNUFFS it (Jem's tin lantern).
- **@@* Bound ranks feed Boosts toward their own spell** (Stone Shape gather). The GM ruled yes.
- **@@* Asher in a foreign flame or metal** rolls fire EL7 against opposition. Riding Cari's own flame or metal costs no roll. A spirit can Manifest with no fire present, at a raised DL (S11: DL4 by running water).
- **@@* Sympathy lowers seeking DLs** (the dead woman's own mirror: -2).

- **OK (table) -- Extreme credit, applied:** karma.md's optional Extreme rule credits 1 Karma toward the score rolled, or toward base Level if no Role was involved. Cari's Extreme push credited 1 toward Level 5. The GM nearly missed this; it's worth a line on the sheet template.
- **Role-play-only lore** (the Order's documents, rotas, robes and history, never rolled) is the natural case for a Maneuver bought on fiction alone. Precedent: Loremaster 1 -> 2 at the S08 save.
- **@@ OPEN -- The Maneuver limit.** maneuvers.md says "shouldn't have more Maneuvers than ranks." Does that mean the count of Maneuvers, or total Maneuver ranks? It decides whether Conjuror (True Name of Asher 4) has room for a Second Sight Maneuver; Sight was rolled bare about ten times this session. Also open: what a brand-new Maneuver costs on its own.
- **OK (Lee) -- Maneuver limit, answered.** A Role may hold no more Maneuvers than its rank: it's a COUNT of Maneuvers, not a total of their ranks. (Each Maneuver's own rank is capped by the Role.) A NEW Maneuver at rank 1 costs 1K. The intent: a character's highest Roles hold the most Maneuvers, with the deepest ranks, and that represents her focus. Without new Roles or Level, a PC can spend a great deal of Karma deepening what she already does. -> Worth stating outright in maneuvers.md: "count, not ranks," plus the cost of a new one. The GM had been adding up ranks since S07.
- Cari's open slots after the S11 save: Conjuror 3, Performer 2, Healer 2, Spellweaver 1.
- **OK (Lee) -- New Roles are always cheap (1K),** but a point comes where 1K toward something thematically right for the PC outweighs a thematically *odd* Role. The opportunity cost is the brake; no rule is needed.
- **OK (Lee) -- The efficiency nuance.** When you rank up a Role, its free Maneuver rank is worth most on your *best* Maneuver, because raising that one by Karma costs its high current rating. Pay for the cheap things (low ranks, new Maneuvers at 1K) separately.
