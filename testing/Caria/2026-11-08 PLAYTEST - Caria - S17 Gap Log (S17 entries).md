## GEAR and in-game work (Lee asked for these first)
- **[RULED] Item rating vs effect rank.** An unrated Trait raises the item's RATING (the pot, and the EL it opposes the maker with) without raising its EFFECT. The bend lamp: Light/Fax 1 + *Persistent* = **rating 2 item, flame rank 1**. "It makes the item harder, without adding to brightness." -> item-creation.md (The Rating Is A Budget): one line, with this example.
- **[RULED] Gear castings are ANCHORS.** Castings bought with Karma, or built as Gear, are Anchors: traceable back to the maker, a place a spirit can land, and they do NOT hang on the maker's binding or strength (why the Cold's spike stands while the Cold is Down). -> item-creation.md / spellcraft.md (Binding) / register sec 12.
- **[RULED] Fuel is mundane.** The lamp's Persistent flame is "just a candle flame without a candle," so it never goes out. Fill the lamp with oil and it lights its own wick; trim the wick to make it brighter. Nothing fancy -- no Boost rule needed. -> item-creation.md (an example of a Persistent item meeting ordinary use).
- **[IN BOOK, applied] Permanent work is downtime only, one roll per session, no Luck Tokens (Karma may stand in), the item opposes at its rating.** The lamp asked for mid-session is this session's downtime roll (at the wrap or before she leaves). @@ Is "before she leaves on the round" a GM-called downtime (spending-karma.md allows one), or must it wait for the session break?
- **[@@* GM] Magical Gear can be broken, disarmed or stolen** ("stuff breaks") and resists at its own frame -- applied to the Cold's anchor spike (Lv3: Necromantic Anchor 3, Obscurity 1). Not yet tested by an attempt. -> item-creation.md / what-gear-does.md.
- **[@@* GM] A running Persistent casting as a free-action Boost** -- roll the recast EL vs DL1; extra dice capped at the casting's current STRENGTH (the Fax "shawl" round Colm). -> register sec 11.
- **[IN BOOK, applied] Binding a simple common spell** -- full ritual, DL0, bind one Role rank per point kept (Stone Shape bound to the pebble, effect 2, one rank kept). Everything else she casts dropped by one. -> spellcraft.md; character sheet at the wrap.
- **[RULED] Reshaped stone reverts to its PRIOR shape, holes and all** -- Stone Shape on the bracket's setting: the bolts moved out stay out; the bolt-holes remain because they were the stone's shape before. -> spells.md (Stone Shape).

## Rules from play (headlines; full text goes in at the fold)
- **[RULED] DOWN is a coma.** A Down spirit does nothing but its dawn recovery roll. Its standing workings persist as THINGS: perceivable, opposable, dismantlable at their own frame, but they take no actions. The S16 leash and S17 knot "acting" were GM errors. -> register sec 8, 12, 14.
- **[RULED] Active summoning or tending can work like Healer rolls on a Down spirit's Harm.** -> sec 8.
- **[RULED] Frames obey the caps:** Level caps Roles, Roles cap Maneuvers; Boosts may be bigger but transient; the same stats resist the Boost and the task. -> sec 19, 25.
- **[RULED] Wagers are pass/fail with no effect of their own; a won Wager is REUSABLE DICE for solving the problem** (any work that solves it), not automatic successes. -> sec 3.
- **[RULED] Fatigue as a HINDRANCE** draining the PC's own dice (the +1-opposition-die way is "more like a temporary Hook," also fine). -> sec 9.
- **[RULED] A separate DL0 roll to OPEN the Sight** when the GM thinks it warranted (it is not a free action). Resolves the old UNCLEAR. -> sec 14.
- **[RULED] Axe-and-door:** no active resistance and no time pressure -> no further rolls; it opens eventually, at the cost of time (accumulated-progress.md). Offer "another exchange," not only a Token. -> sec 1.
- **[RULED] Hooks apply even when not manifesting** (A Hint of Brimstone with no smell present). Self-hook taken: +1 Karma. -> sec 7.
- **[RULED] NPC frames: POWER = total Karma; INFLUENCE = top EL in a relevant skill, maybe with the relevant Hook.** -> NPC Frames policy; register sec 19.
- **[TABLE OPTION, not a rules change] Real INJURY heals at most 1 point a day** -- a grimmer option applied to THIS game in THIS setting (Caria playtest / Averond), on top of recovery.md's once-per-day roll. Adopted when the Cold's odds came up. Do NOT fold into recovery.md; at most, note it in the register as a dial a GM may turn. -> register sec 8 (as an optional dial).
- **[RULED] Spirits recover at the same speed, for different reasons. A BANISHED spirit stays gone longer than a day.** -> register sec 8, 14.
- **[RULED] Extreme fallback.** When an Extreme success has no obvious benefit, it Boosts the NEXT roll by its dice. An NPC's Extreme pays the GM a Token. -> register sec 2 (Extremes).
- **[RULED] Improvisation dice never go on the CAST.** Spell Improvisation adds to the HOLD roll only; the cast (= strength) stays at the plain recast EL. -> spellcraft.md (Improvising) / register sec 11.
- **[RULED] Persistent Fax recipe (corrects S15).** Separate HOLD roll (recast + Spell Improvisation) vs DL = 1 per useful Trait; failing LOSES the bound spell. Then the CAST at plain recast EL. Trying the modification snuffs outstanding non-Persistent castings of that spell. -> register sec 11.
- **[RULED] Losing a bound spell puts out every Persistent instance of it anywhere,** unless built with the Gear system. -> spellcraft.md (Binding) / register sec 11, 12.
- **[RULED] Recast EL counts the bound rank** (Level + all unbound Role ranks + ranks bound in the spell + its Maneuver ranks). The sheet had been one die short since about S14. -> sheet only (book already says so).
- **[RULED] Stone Shape lasts while she FOCUSES; after the cast it's skill at using it,** and axe-and-door applies (nothing resisting, no time pressure: it gets done). -> spells.md (Stone Shape).
- **[APPLIED] Scry procedure.** HOLD at ritual + Spell Improvisation vs DL5; CAST at ritual EL = the sensor's strength; no seeking roll when the target is already found and nothing resists; the mirror can be turned so others see; Display from astral by fire on Conjuror ranks. -> spells.md (Scry) worked example / register sec 11.
