## Spells in items (wands, robes, rings, scrolls)

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Design] A spell in an item is a Trait (Lee, 2026-10-11). "An item that encodes a spell is also just embedding a Trait." A spell in a wand, a robe, a ring or a scroll is a Trait, and it is UNRATED: "Some Traits can have ranks, but that one cannot." The item is the construct, not ranks.
- Use: if Cari enchants a willow wand with Veil, "it's an item she can use to cast the spell in combat time, without binding it." The recast uses her own EL (unbound Role ranks plus Maneuver ranks); the wand adds none. (@@* Lilita: same number as binding it, since unbound ranks apply to every spell, and she keeps that rank free for Artifice, counterspelling and everything else in the Role.)
- Failure: "A failed cast fries the wand, and it's just a willow stick, so Gear rules apply: it can be disarmed, or destroyed, or stolen."
- Ranks: spending a Maneuver on the spell is the PC learning it ever better - the PC's ranks, not the item's. "The wand isn't the ranks." An item that itself carries ranks requires real Karma spent and uses the Assist rule - "one hell of an item... but it's possible." (assist-rule.md caps the Boost at the user's own Role rank; no rank, no Boost.)
- Many items: an Artificer can have several items that store a spell and use them instead of binding Role ranks, which makes him vulnerable. The binding cap does not limit items; that is intended. "Character power increased significantly at the cost of dependence on the item." ("Saruman - your staff is broken!")
- Availability: "A world-class swordsman is dependent on having a sword, but he can usually take one from an enemy." A wizard can seize an enemy's wand and try to use it, "with appropriate hijinks ensuing," but wands are far less likely to be available: "Not everyone carries wands the way most do knives." The wizard's advantage is that the spell he knows might not be currently bound, but he can bind it if his wand is destroyed, once he gets the opportunity (full ritual; not mid-fight).
- Lee: "Damned handy, and a major tool for Spellweavers, but vulnerable, and even just a little bit dangerous."
-> Belongs in item-creation.md and in spellcraft.md beside Binding. @@ OPEN: releasing (fast-casting) a spell from an item - does it spend the Trait and leave a plain stick, making a one-shot item a scroll? @@* Lilita: a wizard's wand is a target; enemies who know how wizards work go for the willow first.

## Spirit familiars: sheets and growth (Asher) (2026-10-11)

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Design] Points model for a PC's spirit (Lee). Any points or Karma the PC spends on a spirit's True Name translate to points on the spirit's own sheet, for the spirit to spend; player and GM negotiate how it spends them. Template on the smallest case: a spirit like Asher, created to grow with the PC. (A spirit met in play and bartered for as Gear may be far more powerful; that is a separate case, to come back to.)
- **The first True Name rank establishes the familiar.** It would have cost 1 Karma had she bought it rather than spending the free Maneuver, so it is worth 1 point, and it makes the spirit permanently associated, effectively a Familiar. From then on it can be written up as an NPC with a sheet. Like a PC, it gets base Level 1 for free. (conjuring.md: weak spirits "might not even have a Level," existing almost only as a Hook.)
- **Defining the spirit defines its Role.** Rank 1 of the Role carries one Maneuver rank; for an ember sprite, Manifestation makes most sense. Result: `Asher - Level 1 Ember Sprite 1 (Manifestation 1)`. Lee: "tadaa! - ecce daemonium."
- **Hook:** *Smitten with the Child of the Contract* [Lee] (rank on the sheet: "@@ assume 1").
- **Identity is the Role (Lee).** Ember Sprite is Asher's identity, so it is both his nature and his Role. Mortals take Adept to represent mastery of some supernatural aspect of themselves, a "professional" skill of being that thing; for a spirit it is the natural state and can't be separated. Written as an Adept, practically nothing would change except the cost: a mortal is bound by what the mortal form can bear (Overchannelling -- a resistance roll against the overage, which a well-managed Adept usually makes). A spirit uses practically the same rules without the possibility of overchannelling. "He *is* fire - it doesn't hurt him at all to try as hard as he can."
- **Growth, rank by rank (the Level ceiling applies).** True Name 2 gives him 1 Karma. He can't raise Ember Sprite above his Level 1, and Level 2 costs 2, so it can only buy a new Role at rank 1 (Lee: maybe Loremaster or Naturalist, from what he has done and seen) -- or he banks it, more likely, counted toward increased loyalty and avidity. True Name 3 gives 2 more: with 1 banked, that buys Level 2 (2K) and Ember Sprite 2 (1K), whose Maneuver rank goes to Manifestation 2. Fire EL 6. Lee: fair and reasonably balanced -- "significant, but bounded by GM interpretation of the Accords as a governor when needed." (Lilita, corrected: conjuror.md meters a FORMALLY contracted spirit -- its Contract Hook drops by one per service unless paid some other way -- but the S01 sheet says Asher has no formal Contract. His meter is the Accords debt ledger: every Query and favor is owed and paid.)
- **RETCON owed:** the master sheet has Asher at Level 3 at True Name 3 (a GM call marked "Lee may veto"). Under this model he is Level 2, Ember Sprite 2, Manifestation 2, nothing banked. Update the sheet at S09 start (ask Lee first). -> conjuror.md's line that True Name ranks "can raise the base Level of a weak but friendly spirit" needs rewriting for the points model.
- **His limits are learned, not built in (Lee).** Manifestation means he can inhabit an *effect*: he could make himself a physical avatar, or spawn a fire out of nothing. He just doesn't understand Mundis that well yet. It's far easier where the physical world already "agrees," such as where a fire already is -- he is taking advantage of small pre-existing Boosts. (The S08 river-water struggle is that, seen from the table.)
- **@@ OPEN - Hooks on credit for a familiar (Lee: "interesting, but a bit of a rabbit hole, and maybe even a cheat").** Pinned for later. Lee's starting position: the spirit *might choose* a Hook rank on credit to represent deeper devotion, generally up to the GM, and if it does, the PC should take one herself to balance it. Unsettled: whether her Hook is the price (a lever, no Karma) or also on credit.
- **True Name bonus on her own rolls -- answers the S08 "double payout" note.** Cari gets her True Name ranks on Conjuror rolls involving him, but rolling *against* him would be adversarial and would damage their working relationship, so it rarely comes up. It applies when she compels him (he refuses something), summons him back after he is disrupted and cast into the Far Reaches, reaches him through a barrier (S03: calling him from inside the sidhe house, EL6 vs Diff 3, and through the hearth-fire, EL6), and **defends him** against another's compulsion, banishment or binding (Lee: "that's good!"). In those cases it "feels more like tapping a pre-existing Boost" [Lee]. -> Not a double-dip problem.
- @@ UNCLEAR - adepts.md: the Adept EL line says "as much of their base Level"; only the Marcus example shows it means Level capped at rank. One clause would fix it.

## Followers, companions and apprentices: one rule, several hats (2026-10-11)

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Design] Companions across Roles (Lee). Beast Handler and Conjuror line up; where they diverge, it is mostly "them covering different parts of continuous rules."
- **Kade and Hollis:** Kade probably has Hollis as a Maneuver. For Asher we say "True Name" for color, but the application is much the same: for a Handler, Maneuver points represent time and effort in training; for a Conjuror, relationship-building; "pretty much the same from Hollis' perspective."
- **Extra Karma:** both allow extra Karma to be dedicated, for extra time and energy. A Handler is assumed to put more of the PC's time into bonding and training. Cari could teach Asher (Display glamours using Veil; Possession using Performer), but those don't fit their relationship; she might teach him to read mortal auras, eventually.
- **Kade's trade:** higher companion vulnerability, compensated by flexibility. Kade can't see the Borderlands himself, but Hollis can, and Kade can train the hound's innate special abilities up. Beyond the Karma in Maneuver ranks, free Karma can always be spent on the companion.
- (Lilita: in beast-handler.md the handler spends Karma on the animal's sheet and can split EL to act through it; Asher spends his own points and rolls his own EL as an NPC. More payout per Karma for the Conjuror, less control. And the companion cap: can't outlevel the PC; can't be taught above the teacher's skill.)

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Design] Followers (Lee). Jem is "the more generalized case of a follower, outside the specifics of either." Points spent on him, GM-approved, follow much the same rules with much the same results; followers are Gear (gear.md lists "followers" with bases, businesses, spells and spirits).
- **Points represent character growth, capability and influence.** Spending a point on a Warrior's *GhoulBreaker, my Hammer* makes it a signature item with an extra die from Synergy. Spending points on Jem "means basically binding him, making him loyal and available and capable for at least that many points' worth."
- **Unpaid, he stays GM's.** If she never spends points and only role-plays it, he remains a purely GM character. If the RP warrants it, the GM might grant Good Fun Karma and allocate it.
- **The host Role matters: it limits the number of other Maneuvers for that Role.** Loremaster is the default for *teaching*, Performer for *leading*, etc. A dedicated custom Role (Mentor; Commander for a mercenary leader) is reasonable when/if it becomes important. Points can also go into Spellweaver or Conjuror. (Lilita: Cari is already paying this with Asher -- all three Conjuror Maneuver ranks are True Name of Asher. Loremaster 2 is full with Scry and Light, so hosting Jem there needs Loremaster 3 or a GM call; a new Role at rank 1 costs 1K and brings its Maneuver. A custom Role's rank should still do something itself -- teach, judge a pupil, rally -- or it's a tax. No current /L1/roles/ page covers leading or teaching people.)
- **Not a puppet: two pools.** Jem should not be limited to the points she devotes unless we want a little puppet. "No points devoted is a great way to have a student turn into a nemesis later; ONLY points devoted relegates him to a sidekick... ok, but not really the plan for Cari." Spending a point makes him a Follower/Contact but doesn't stop him growing on his own. The more capable he is aside from her points, the less control she has over his actions, but the more he can do when she asks -- if he agrees.
- **Release.** There should come a point where he can be set up somewhere to research and practice ("mind the tower"). Once he has a first rank of an actual Role in Spellweaver (or Conjuror) he is a capable professional in his own right, and she could release him to go do his own thing. (@@* Lilita: under the Accords, teaching is a gift accepted, so a released apprentice is a Contact who owes her.)
- **Invested points don't go away (Lee).** "The less often he appears in the storyline, the more they should be worth when she does call on him." If he sets up his own practice in a small shop in Ganth, she can visit or send messages and get help according to how many points she has invested. "It's much like points she spent in Performer; there might be entire sessions where they never get used, and that's ok. They are there when the situation calls for it."

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Design] Spirits can grow outside her points too (Lee). Asher might make Agreements with the House; he might pick up ranks of Fey or Demon. ## GM craft: NPC knowledge and provenance (adopted 2026-10-11)

@@ UNCOMMITTED [GM craft] Partitioning NPC knowledge (Lee raised, 2026-10-11). Lee: the GM's "one ongoing minor faux-pas" is keeping individual NPC knowledge partitioned, which "occasionally bleeds over into you attributing things you created to me, too." Not a problem, "just occasionally I notice. It happens pretty often at a physical table too." -> Cause (Lilita): everything in the GM's context is one pool with no record of who was in the room; and summaries flatten "who said it" into "established," so a GM invention can come back as "Lee said." -> Lee: "Let's do that going forward." Adopted from S09:
- **Present line in the ledger.** Every beat records who was there: "Present: Cari, Jem, Kade, Hollis."
- **Who-knows-what table** in the GM ONLY brief (the CAST card stays player-safe). One row per NPC, especially what they have NOT been told (e.g. Kade: not yet told the ring-stone was lifted). Check it before voicing anyone.
- **Sourcing check.** Before an NPC states a fact: saw it, was told, rumor, or worked it out? If none, he doesn't say it, or gets it wrong the way people do.
- **Provenance tags survive every summary.** [Lee] and @@* carry through. An untagged line is of unknown origin: say "the record has it," never "you said."
- **Lee's flag:** `<how does he know that?>` -- the GM stops, checks, retcons in fiction if needed, and logs it.
