## Deep history

- **SARINA**, Saint of the Wild, ruled the region until about a THOUSAND YEARS AGO. She kept the few humans who made it here suppressed; she didn't care for them much.
- **TAHANOS** is an EQUATORIAL Saint, aggressive and acquisitive. His sphere is NECROMANCY: great power over the dead, and over time significant influence over DEMONS through Necromancy's adjacent rituals. He has NO significant direct authority over elementals or fey. He wiped out several of his own cities to fuel a direct assault on CAPITOL, Carterion's home city at the foot of one of the Pillars, and was rebuffed. He travelled, fumed, found this region, and moved in. He DESTROYED Sarina -- but not before an old rival followed him here: EIDOLON.
- Tahanos left the region about a millennium ago and hasn't come back. He set **MALISONDRE** as his LIEUTENANT here. He demands a report now and then, meddles, micromanages from a distance, and goes dark for decades. Malisondre is Eidolon's real adversary in the region, and a worthy one.
- (Supersedes the vaguer "centuries ago" framing in `crossroads_west_setting.md`.)

## The Order and the Mountain

- **The ORDER is almost a thousand years old**; the MONASTERY was built pretty early after that.
- **MOUNT CUNAE** -- "the CRADLE" (Lee, S14 Day 18). Mildly volcanically active.
- **The spirit of the mountain:** stone and fire, a bit of faerie, Hook SLOTH ("the mountain sleeps"). The strongest natural force in the region. **It is a DRAGON** -- a HALFLING (in the physical and spiritual realms at once), which is WHY Necromancy can get any purchase on it.
- The HEARTH-SPIRITS of the house are the mountain's small kin, half-sick with the Cold's pull. Telling a hearth about the Cold may stir the sleeper.

## The Cold

- Something NECROMANTIC Tahanos set on the mountain to LEECH power from the sleeping dragon, and maybe someday control it. Hopelessly outclassed, but the mountain sleeps, so it works insidiously over geologic time; Tahanos's pride can say he hasn't been thwarted *yet*.
- **MALISONDRE convinced Tahanos the leech was his own idea -- on purpose, hoping it would blow up in his face.** She is not the Cold's friend.
- The Cold is a SPIRIT of its own: aware, sneaky, patient, an OPPORTUNIST. It CORRUPTS those who "take hold" of it. If its attention leaves the mountain too long, the dragon may WAKE, realise it has been attacked for centuries, and be angry.
- It arrived not long after the monastery was founded; it has surfaced in an EVENT every century or two since. CUTHRED's time (~200 years ago) was one; Cari's is the latest.
- It has just enough influence over the mountain to lean on ALL THE FIRES IN THE MONASTERY (it pulls them from underneath, "like roots"). It wants the Founder's pages EATEN: they hold the means to thwart it, and they are the work of a Saint its master hates.
- **Everything in the storyline so far is the Cold trying to get a handle on the mountain** (and opportunists around it). Malisondre is probably NOT directly involved yet.
- **S14 state:** Cari's Rebuke put the Cold's LOCAL presence DOWN (Harm 9 vs Level 4, Day 16). It recovers like anyone: 4d vs its Harm 9d each dawn (no recovery Days 16-18). While it's Down, its grip on the house's fires is broken, the leech slackens, and THE SLEEPER STIRS (a sigh through the stone, Day 16 night). Accumulating.

## The Founder's letters (the EPISTOLAE FUNDATORIS)

- Eleven letters to early Priors, each a single dense sheet of Celestial both sides, signed with the same flourish under various names, dated across the first centuries. On their face: personal, practical, warm. Under them: a CIPHER SUBSTRATE.
- **The letters encode the SYSTEM and CAPACITY for secret messages without specifying any.** They are a PLATE. The KEY (a separate leaf) chooses the marks; Eidolon can rewrite the KEY to make the same letters say anything.
- **Most letters PREDATE the Cold.** Eidolon builds documents early so he can reference them later and be RETROACTIVELY PRESCIENT -- "a Little Orphan Annie decoder ring," custom-tuned when needed. (Same principle as the Charter Clause, S12: prophecy is planning and presentation.)
- The CHARTER CLAUSE ("Accept not the Cold which burns the Hand") was written to sound specific without being clear -- leverage against APATHY and POLITICAL CRUELTY that exploits the poor; the literal Cold arriving later made it look prophetic.
- **How the cipher works:** the verse is a verbal TREASURE MAP written like a song (rolling iambic heptameter aloud; rhymes; double meanings filter the reading). Its words are heavy with NUMBERS -- bells (Prime, Terce), seconds, quatrains, fifths, sex/sextant, the sept, octants -- and arithmetic words (from, times, with, plus, add, by, over, under). Method: identify the LEAF, count LINE then WORD, take the CAPITALISED letter or the letter MISSING from an apostrophe'd abbreviation, sometimes doing the math to get there; the verse can work like a D.S. AL CODA. **Unimportant words (a plain hyphenated word, no marks) are STOP markers**, as in a telegraph. The message may have INTENTIONAL HOLES to seem more believable.
- **Copies** carry the code only if every letter is faithful -- case, odd abbreviations, spacing. No copy is perfect; the copy's quality LIMITS the result.
- **History of the bundle:** the leaves were SCATTERED through books in the stacks. A KEY (the twelfth, unaddressed, verse leaf) came into PRIOR DORIN "the Quiet"'s daily post, between envelopes, with a scrap in the Founder's hand pointing to where the needed leaves were. CUTHRED intercepted it, found the leaves (and a couple of extras), decoded it -- it named him -- and sealed the lot, warded against the Cold, "I could not burn them." More leaves may still be in the stacks if we WANT them (not set in advance).
- **The decoded message** (S14, from the key Cuthred intercepted):
  - The Dragon sleeps. / The death-cold creeps. / And Cuthred still his secret keeps.
  - (Closing couplet of the verse, verbatim [Lee]: "At last, to find the root of all the trouble, as you sought, / We turn the key upon the page that's numbered aught and naught.")
  - The key turned on ITSELF (the undated verse leaf): SAY IT NEVER ALOUD / NEVER THIS NAME ALOUD / MALISONDRE OF TAHANOS.
- Leaves 9, 10, 11 and most of the verse's words are deliberately UNWRITTEN (the GM's recovery trope): fill when needed, never contradicting what was said.

- **Leaf 11 of the Rule defines the black art** [Lee, trued "There it is"]: "That art is black which binds a free will outside the Accords: to wear another, or to Accept being worn." (l.2571)

"The Order sheds no blood" STRUCK -- Rikitak is not above genocide when it suits his inscrutable purposes; not warm and fuzzy, though uniformly civil and witty; he might be rather piqued by souls as goods.

### The Charter (the Founder's founding charter of the house)
- **The Charter stands ABOVE the Rule** (it authorises the Rule).
- **Leaf 2** [Lee, working canon]: the monastery is not so much GRANTED as CHARGED to HOLD the BLUFF on which it sits, with the ARTESIAN SPRING behind (the stream behind the stables), and the flat large enough for all the buildings, the garth and the gardens. GRANTED: the service of the TRUE FLAME, PURE AND BRIGHT, without explanation. CHARGED: "Defend the region and the Free City of Ganth, and their right to Self Determination." Considering (kept, unobjected): "Be such a Light unto the land that all Prosper by the Accords." And: "The Charter members shall write the Rule, and the Rule shall bind them in Accord."
- **CHARTER MEMBERS** [Lee]: the Founder APPOINTED them, and he was one of them. It has been argued and accepted IN THE RULE that "charter members" means those IN VOWS and RECOGNISED BY THE CONVOCATION -- so any alteration of the Rule must go before the Convocation; folk are slow to change their own foundations. CUTHRED was setting the stage with the CUSTOMARY to LOAD THE DECK so he could MUTATE THE RULE.
- **Leaf 3** [Lee]: the READING CLAUSE ("that none may say he was not told"); the SANCTION (to be SEEN for what one is, in terms of Light and Flame); the ROLL of charter members, each name BRANDED into the vellum, the Founder among them under an assumed name; the Founder's SIGIL bold beneath with his flourish. Date handwaved (before 4287 by letter order).
- **"FREE CITY"** [Lee]: Ganth has NO GOD-KING -- no Saint that defines it and dictates by fiat; its politics are subject to itself; run by a MERCANTILE COUNCIL, Byzantine in its politics, which may hand out land and titles under fealty (the COUNT stands, a council-made title). "Weirdness is the norm here."

### Old Soot
- **OLD SOOT is an original CHARTER MEMBER** (unrealised by the house), and through him the Charter is an **ACCORD WITH THE MOUNTAIN**. (l.2844)
- **His TRUE NAME is branded among the signatories.** Only someone who KNOWS it is a True Name, or is trained to use one, can USE it. Read aloud without that knowledge -- and pronounced properly -- it only PINGS him (he notices, may flare, and hears what follows); the ping is invisible to all but open Second Sight. The Charter, a strong standing Contract, PROTECTS him while it is in force. The reading of the Charter also gets his metaphorical EAR in the room.
- He is NOT out of the loop: all the house's GOSSIP goes through the KITCHEN, which is why he's usually there. Cuthred cost him only his FORMAL ear in the Convocation, not his news.
- **OLD SOOT is, basically, THE TRUE FLAME, PURE AND BRIGHT** -- here to GUIDE and DEFEND the Order. Leaf 2's grant reads both ways: Soot's service to the house, and the house's to him. GM-ONLY; the house doesn't know.
