## 1. Getting there, and why this stop matters

- **Southbound timing (S3):** Crossroads at dawn, the fork by late morning, the road skirting the Weeping Court woods from about 1 to 3 pm, and Tanner's Ford by about 6 pm. The fork to the Ford is about 14 miles.
- **Northbound:** the camp at the near edge of the Court woods puts the caravan past the Court and the fork by midday on Day 4. **Crossroads by evening of Day 4** looks likely @@*.
- **It has been only about a week since Cari was last here** @@ (check my count). Rook has kept the bowls for days, not months. Berra's silver dust is fresh in the loft, and the gossip is still warm.
- **The Monastery is ONE day north of Crossroads** (S2: Monastery to Crossroads by nightfall). So Crossroads is very likely **the last night before the Mountain.**
  - Kade reports to Brask the day after.
  - Fennick decides what he hands in, and to whom he leaves copies.
  - Cari decides how she means to go after Wenna.

  Whatever needs saying, or hiding, or arranging, Crossroads is the last good place for it.

## 2. Crossroads as Cari saw it (S2)

- **Size and role** (setting doc): about 300 souls; a trading post, rest stop and local seat for the villages around it. It has an inn and tavern, a market square with weekly market days, a shrine cluster, a smith, a miller and a small dock for river and coastal trade. The council is elected from the trades and the farmers.
- **The Crossroads Inn & Tavern.** Fennick took a room here in S2, under the loft.
  - **The kitchen** is Berra's: a bake-oven, and a back door that feeds whoever comes hungry (Aubric's price).
  - **The loft** is low and warm from the chimney, over the kitchen. It has a straw pallet, mice, and a small shuttered gable window facing east toward the road and the fork.
  - **Carved in the rafter** above the pallet: "JEM" and a small bird (a wren) [Lee: Jem's carving].
  - **Silver dust:** Berra ground Cari's silver coin and scattered it in the loft, with intent. @@*: it denies dream-reaching there.
- **The market square:** the well, with the shrine cluster beside it. The lane from the inn yard runs down to it; Rikitak's bowl was visible from the kitchen yard.
- **The shrine cluster:** Grolikarnigan, Susheil, Rana, Carterion and Rikitak. The circuit priest hadn't come in two months, and Rikitak's horns had been scratched out of spite at the tithe cart, not at the god.
  - **Cari's oath** (S2 dawn, before witnesses): she left **Rikitak's bowl EMPTY**, to stay empty until she found out why his shrine went untended and why his healers extort.
- **The current:** to Cari's spirit-sense, the crossroads current ran strong but THIN, "like a river running low and too fast." The drain was the Court's dream, fed on the current to keep Jem. With Jem home, it "will run fuller within days" @@*. **Payoff:** she should feel the difference at the shrines.
- **Garrow Hask's forge** @@*. A small iron-and-bronze Carterion icon hangs over the fire. Old Bellows lived in that fire.

## 3. People she knows there

**Berra** @@*, cook at the inn
- Who she is: Mags's late brother's widow, in her forties. Broad, flour to the elbows, a burn-scarred left hand, a wooden spoon for a pointer. Sharp-tongued like Mags; warmer underneath and less patient.
- Gave Cari the loft, oil, herbs, salt and bread ("the oven paid for it"). Asher kept her oven all night, and she slept through for the first time in twenty years.
- Agreed to tell the lane the blue bowl was a pilgrim-priest's blessing, "Aubric's kind."
- Ground the silver into the loft on Cari's word: "Hush."
- **What she knows of Aubric:** only that he "died." He set her hand twenty years ago, and his price was "feed whoever comes to your back door hungry." She doesn't know that he was the Hospitaller, that he was murdered, that he was raised, or that Cari laid him to rest in Saltwind.
- Her last words to Cari: "Kiss Dunny for me. I'll try to stop on the way home."

**Dunny** @@*, 12, Berra's pot-boy
- Tells everyone everything. Has a pink oven-door scar on his palm.
- **He knew Jem.** Jem slept in the loft the night before the fork and woke saying he'd dreamed his ma singing "Little Brown Wren." Dunny thinks Jem is LOST.
- Learned from Cari why you never ask a priest of the Order for help ("'Cause if you ask, it's a bargain. And you don't bargain with fire").

**Abbot.** Berra's old grey lurcher: "eats everything, does nothing, sleeps on the best blanket." He minded Cari's robes and liked Bellows's fire-pot.

**Garrow Hask** @@*, the smith
- In his fifties: bald, scorched forearms, a leather apron. Carterion's man. Wary of monastery robes; respects craft and fire.
- Sold Cari amber, brimstone and applewood charcoal for 2 silver, and gave her the fire-pot.
- Said he'd send word of interesting goods to "Caria of Green Haven, at the Monastery." His late wife's sister lives in Green Haven.
- Half-believes in Old Bellows.

**Old Bellows**, the spirit of Garrow's forge
- His three-hundred-year wish was to touch the sea. Cari carried his coal, and at the west cove below Mira's, Shimmer held him for the quench (S6).
- He came up TEMPERED: black and glassy as jet, with a live ember sealed deep inside. He needs no fuel now.
- His words (via Mira): "Carry me home when your road bends that way. I'll have a story no fire in Averond can match." A gift, not a debt.
- **Where is he now?** [Lee] In his pot, riding north with Cari. Carrying him home is "a debt, of sorts." He needs no feeding, but she should check in on him now and then, to be polite.

**Rook** @@*. Carter's son, early twenties, the lane's monastery-hater. He scratched the horns ("it was the tithe cart I was angry at, not him"), then swore in the square to keep the bowls himself until a proper priest comes.

**Old Tansy** @@*. A widow. She left rosemary in the burning bowl, and knelt at the empty one.

**The square crowd at dawn:** a carter and his wife (she started the story going), and two girls with a water yoke.

**"The Burgher at Crossroads"** @@*, unnamed. One of the people Cari named as a copy-keeper for Fennick's true account (S6).
