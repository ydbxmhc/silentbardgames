Session 4 - The Hospitaller's Keys

Level One solo playtest, Averond - Caria Greenhaven, Initiate of the Monastery of Strife. GM: Lilita. Played 2026-09-29/30. Counted as two sessions for advancement (split at dawn at the Ford).

At a glance

A night and a morning. Caria brings Jem home to Dasha Korrin on a dark road, is visited in a dream by his dead mother, and learns what walks the coast. It's a ghola, and it's Brother Aubric: the Hospitaller who taught her to read the tenets, murdered and raised and walked on a leash toward Saltwind. She burns the leash out of Hierarch Calder's hands, takes Aubric's True Name as a gift, and lays him to rest in the reeds of Tanner's Ford.

In the morning she washes in the river, puts on a Healer's orange, and rides into Saltwind in mourning. The town expected a Monastery spy with a fire-demon. The blind keeper of Susheil's well-house puts the girl's hands in the sea's water and finds something else.

Ends at the well-house at midday, with Thousand-Voices sent for.

Scene 1 - The Road Home

Korrin's wagon stops dead. She reads the answer in Caria's face before a word is said, nearly falls off the box, and touches the boy's cheek like testing bread. "Mes Korrin, you'll skin me." "After I feed you." She wraps Caria in her own oilskin coat and offers whatever the mountain charges.

"No mountain. Just me, an honest pilgrim, and Kade, and Hollis... and the tenets I was taught, whatever the men in that building say."

Kade asks only a bowl for the dog, and that nobody write his name in anything. Caria falls asleep in the wagon bed, curled round the boy.

Scene 2 - Tessa's Kitchen

A dream, and she knows it: a south-country doorstep, a woman shelling peas who has Jem's mouth. Unlike Gram, this one asks her in. Tessa, two winters dead, stayed because her boy was neither here nor gone. She thanks Caria and warns her. Something that was a man once stood at the edge of the Ford's firelight, hungry and sad, with a string on it "like a dog's lead," and it has smelled her boy. It isn't fey. It's dead, with something under the grave-smell "like a match struck in a shut room."

"Silver for the demon. Salt for the dead."

For the warning, Caria offers payment in kind and carries a message for Jem: the barley, dry feet, and all the verses.

Scene 3 - Salt and Blood

She wakes gasping for salt. Korrin empties her fish casks without a question. A finger-thin line goes round eleven wagons and a thick one round Jem's. A drop of blood from the silver pin wakes the outer ring like frost. Ash from Asher's fire and two drops more wake the inner ring like embers, so that anything dead touching it touches him. Asher's patrol comes back with a confident all-clear. He's wrong.

"Like calls to like." Caria finds it by the smell of her own brimstone, forty paces out in the reeds, and it feels her find it. Her guided flame shows the drovers a grey, stooped dead man. At his belt hangs a ring of brass keys, and one iron key with a scrap of orange ribbon that she watched him tie when she was twelve.

She calls Brother Aubric to his wits by name and title and love, and he answers: "...Cari. Don't look at me, little sister." He tells her that someone walks him to the sea, "to the woman who shamed him." The leash goes taut.

"How DARE you?!"

Silver, fury, a Token and her brimstone go down the line together, and it burns out end to end. At the far end are candlelight, a tattooed scalp, burned hands and a high voice screaming. Hierarch Voss Calder knows her taste now, and she knows his.

Aubric gives her his True Name. His price is the one he always asked: feed whoever comes to your back door hungry. She lays him to rest with it, and the taint burns out in her salt. "Fire taught you something worth knowing. Well done, little sister." Then she holds what's left of him in the mud and weeps until the dark takes her. Kade carries her back to the wagon and puts the keys in her pocket.

Scene 4 - Morning at the Ford

A river bath behind Korrin's sailcloth, with every drover facing the oxen. She washes away the grime and the grief and keeps the indignation. She works to let go of the pleasure she took in Calder's pain. She thanks Grolikarnigan for footing, Susheil for water, Rana for cold clarity, Carterion for the craft of a warm bath, and Rikitak for the little spark. Asher, thanked to his face, has nothing to say for once. She comes out in the Healer's orange.

Kade is taking Aubric to the Saltwind churchyard, not north. He offers to shade his report to the Almoner. She refuses: the absolute truth. He will write that the Sister laid the Hospitaller to rest with his own name and hers.

Scene 5 - Saltwind

Korrin won't be paid for eels. She takes Caria's four silver "toward the boy's keep" and gives her the tooth-marked nail she dropped on the road: "worst trade I ever made." The sea comes up over the last rise. Old Bellows's coal nearly went out overnight, and it sulks until Asher tells it the whole story. After that it only wants to hear about the fire that burns on nothing.

Caria and Jem make charcoal mourning bands out of torn linen. At the gate, old Harl Pennock knows her by the white lock and the fire-pot and sends Davey running. Fennick, who once copied "misadventure: ill-chosen mushrooms" into the House register, says he will be correcting an entry. He tells her the orange suits her.

Blind Karinna comes down alone to see the Monastery's spy. Caria lays sword and staff aside and prostrates herself on the cobbles. Karinna names the penance: both hands in Susheil's water. The water steams, then holds her.

"You're no spy of that mountain. You're something it made and couldn't keep."

Caria won't accept that penance is done while her Order's wrongs stand. "Then stay and do more. Round here we call that being neighbors." Nell runs to fetch Thousand-Voices.

Rulings and canon from this session
  • Dream lucidity: a visited dreamer rolls Conjuror (or Level) against the dream's hold. A success means she knows it's a dream and a visit, and keeps her senses and Maneuvers. "Lucid Dreaming" is a Maneuver candidate.
  • Luck Tokens can be spent on any roll: your spirit's, another PC's, even an enemy's. The GM may spend GM Tokens on a player's roll.
  • Hooks: the owner may split her rank across the scene. Anyone else tapping it rolls it as dice. "The character is the power play."
  • A Boost and a Ward are separate rolls. Wards are wagers, Persistent and Reactive, with Area Effect free. A caster's own Ward doesn't fight her own spells.
  • A ghola carries a trace of demon: salt for the dead, silver for the demon.
  • A True Name freely given works as a strong Boost on the night it's given. The giver names the price.
  • Karma supply: the GM can pay Tokens instead when Karma runs hot. A double-length session can allow two improvements, but only one attendance Karma.

Full detail, including the New-GM notes, is in the gap log.
