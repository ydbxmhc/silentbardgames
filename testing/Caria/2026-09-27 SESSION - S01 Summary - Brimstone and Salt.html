Session 1 - Brimstone and Salt

Level One solo playtest, Averond - Caria Greenhaven, Initiate of the Monastery of Strife. GM: Lilita. Played 2026-09-26/27.

At a glance

A demon-marked young Spellweaver asks her ailing mentor, the Precentor, for leave to go on pilgrimage - and leaves the Monastery with his sealed letter for Saltwind's shrine-keeper, a spirit-scouting report she half regrets, the Almoner's clerks inventorying her belongings, and a spirit-hunter riding the same caravan.

Ends the night before Korrin's wagons roll at first light.

Scene 1 - The Library

Caria comes to see Master Ivro and is stopped by his clerk, Tobin. Behind the door, Hierarch Voss Calder presses Ivro for the lesser ritual chamber's book. Calder emerges, names her "the Precentor's little ember," and she offers to have him bring Ivro "a nice mushroom tea" - in front of Tobin. Calder is delighted, and remembers.

Scene 2 - The Precentor's Study

Ivro is ill - old lungs, poppy syrup - and forbids her from buying his years with any bargain; she answers "Understood," not a promise. Calder wants the chamber book, where her name and her workings fill most of the pages. She asks leave for a pilgrimage to Green Haven, Saltwind and Rana's bluff. He grants it with a writ, and entrusts her with a letter under his private salamander seal for the Saltwind shrine-keeper called Thousand-Voices - into her hand only. "You are a pilgrim. Only a pilgrim."

The Merchanter's Rule: worth, not cost.
Scene 3 - An Afternoon of Small Errands

Merrit Holloway, library novice, becomes her devoted helper: a regional map, the circuit register (Saltwind's shrine kept by blind Karinna, with "an unordained hedge shrine-keeper"), and word that the Almoner's office booked two places on Korrin's caravan - Clerk Fennick and an unnamed brother. She apologises to Tobin. In her cell, Second Sight shows a watching spark warded into Ivro's seal.

Asher appears unbidden; she asks him to scout Saltwind - the people's mood, what would burn and what wouldn't, the strength of Susheil's shrine. His price: a secret she has never told.

Scene 4 - The Kitchens

Old Mags feeds her and scolds her. The kitchen's gossip: the Saltwind priest routed by a shrine-keeper and her village; the Hierarch's men calling his lost property "it"; Wenna Tarro, twelve, held in the west wing as surety for her father's grain; the Hospitaller's death cap mushrooms "drawn from the kitchen stores" - which Mags swears never held one. And the laundresses have been asked whose robes smell of sulphur.

Scene 5 - The Refectory

Ivro's seat is empty. Caria opens her Third Eye in the middle of breakfast - an Extreme Success - and sees the house clearly: a grey, shuttered Prior; a contented Almoner with something cold beneath him running toward the west wing; a feverish Calder; and a stranger with his aura banked down tight and a grey spirit-hound at his feet. The hound looks straight at her. Its master notices. The Prior closes the lesser ritual chamber pending the Almoner's audit.

Scene 5b - The Library Again

Clerk Fennick carries off the chamber book. Caria's fishing tips him that the scriptorium talked, and Merrit fears for himself; Caria names the Precentor as her source. Tobin will say only that Ivro is resting on the Infirmarer's orders - and writes her visit down. Merrit gives her waxed scroll-cases, a coastal chart with the Gallows Teeth marked, and a promise to send word of Ivro by the carters.

Back in her cell: her satchel has been searched and her flowers of sulphur "retained pending audit - F."

Scene 6 - The Chapel

Brother Anselm Kade, the Almoner's Finder, kneels at the back. His dog noticed something small and hot leave the house for the coast. Caria answers plainly: "Asher belongs to Asher," and he went to Saltwind for a better picture of the town. Kade warns her never to say "what might burn" where Calder can hear; tells her he is hunting the Hierarch's lost property on that coast; recognises her teacher as Oriane Vesk. He'll be on the third wagon; Fennick on the fourth.

Scene 7 - The Kitchens, Late Afternoon

Mags packs her bread, cheese, dried apples, and honey for the shrine-keeper, and a name: Berra, cook at the Crossroads Inn. Caria hugs her. Nobody at the troughs says a word.

Scene 8 - The Last Night

Asher returns half-drowned: a water-spirit (an old undine) caught him in Saltwind and nearly put him out. His report - a proud, watchful village; tarred nets and thatch that would burn; a deep, strong shrine he couldn't approach; a one-eyed goblin awake on the step of a cottage by the trees, clutching a pot of butter-fat; a sleeping woman "full of prayers" inside; the undine living there too. He let slip that he was sent by "the one with the white in her hair, from the mountain house, coming with the wagons." The undine's message:

"Tell your white-haired friend the sea remembers who comes asking what will burn. Tell her to come to the shrine with her hands open, or not at all."

Caria pays her secret. Asher keeps it.
