@@ MISSING [Spellcraft] Dismissing your own Persistent casting. No /L1 page says whether a caster can end her own Persistent spell at will, or what it costs. @@ UNCOMMITTED [Spellcraft] How long a Persistent casting lasts. Nothing in /L1 sets a lifespan for Persistent; S08 left the lanterns "persistent until the GM says." Lee (S09, proposal): each dawn the light resists the Dawn; the morning rolls as many dice as the casting is days old. "Could snuff on day one, or last weeks." -> Played provisionally from S09 Day 4. GM math: with the spell rolling its casting effect and the dawn adding a die a day, survival is steep -- effect 6 is ~60% through day 3 and ~2% through day 7; even effect 8 is ~0 by day 10. "Weeks" needs a gentler curve (e.g. dice by weeks, or a loss dims the light by the margin instead of putting it out). RULED (Lee, S09): the curve is right -- "weeks is VERY unlikely." Dimming by the margin is good "for things that make sense to do that way"; and "it's *magic* -- it doesn't even have to be consistent," so the GM chooses snuff or dim per casting from the fiction. -> @@ UNCOMMITTED: spellcraft.md beside Persistent. Pairs with the dismissal entry above and with permanent (Karma) castings, which skip the test.

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Engine] Extra Effort on a recast: going over IS a zero (Lee, S09). spellcraft.md: "Going over or rolling a zero always destabilizes the spell." The S08 entry called "going over" a dead clause in a coin pool; that missed Extra Effort (dice.md), which makes overshooting possible. Lee: "rolling over EL *is* a zero. It absolutely costs the spell." -> Refines the S08 "only a zero destabilizes." @@* Lilita: say it in one line in spellcraft.md ("an Extra Effort total above your EL counts as a zero"), and cross-link dice.md. Also: a Token on an Extra Effort roll sets the result up to the EL, not up to the number of dice rolled (luck.md "usually the value of your EL") -- played that way in S09; worth an example.

@@ UNCOMMITTED [Spellcraft] Stone Shape reshapes; it does not fling (Lee, S09). It cannot make stones fly, or really roll. Reshaped stone still reverts when focus ends, in its own shapes where it lies (S08 ruling). -> spells.md's Stone Shape text ("Manipulates existing stone within range") would benefit from one sentence: it moves stone only by reshaping it, never by throwing it. Range at twenty paces in plain sight was played as fine; "within range" is not defined (@@ MISSING).
