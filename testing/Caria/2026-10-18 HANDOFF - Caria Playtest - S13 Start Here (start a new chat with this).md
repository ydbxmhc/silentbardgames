## Rulings added in S12 (details in the gap log)
- **No ladders.** No outcome tables keyed to effect. Use opposition, attrition and Harm types.
- **Oracle drift:** before reaching for a yes/no roll, ask who or what resists. Most beats settle from situation and character with no roll.
- **No Extremes on fewer than 3 dice.**
- **A Boost uses its creator's skill.** Building one takes an action; a pre-existing factor can be rolled free.
- **Reading a damaged text is an attrition contest** against the damage's rating.
- **Framework strength:** a Persistent effect from a multi-roll casting (hold + seeking) gets its own strength roll at the casting EL. That roll is what the Dawn tests.
- **Mundane care can contest a spiritual residue** in a wound (as attrition).
