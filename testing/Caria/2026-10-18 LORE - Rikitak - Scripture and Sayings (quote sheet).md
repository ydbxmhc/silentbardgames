## The Tenets [Lee]

```
We serve the god of Chaos by the Order of the Absentee Saint;
  of Demons, that we learn to Order what Plagues us;
  of Fire, as what we Tend will slip its Limits;
  of Medicine, that we Tend our Plagues and Burns;
  of Magic, to Limit our limits and reach Beyond;
  of Strife, that we ever *Strive*.
```

## The Toll [Lee]

```
The Toll -
There Must Be:
  Cold to give Fire *Use*,
  Fire to give Cold *Meaning*;
  Order to give Chaos *Shape*,
  Chaos to give Order *Meaning*;
  Wounds to give Healing *Work*,
  Healing to give Wounds *Meaning*;
  Rites to give Demons *Limits*,
  Demons to give Rites *Meaning*;
  Laws to give Magic *Ends*,
  Magic to give Laws *Meaning*;
  Lessons to give Strife Meaning,
  Strife to give *Lessons*.
```

## The Charter of the Monastery

### The original, in the Founder's hand [Lee, working copy]
The first line is known to every copyist. The two after it were scraped from the leaf and read again by raking light (S12).

```
We are set upon this Mountain as a lid upon a pot, that what Lies beneath may slumber.
  Accept not the Cold which burns the Hand and wear thee as a Glove.
  Tend to the Hearths that the least of the House take up the Brand to rekindle Hell.
```

### As the copies have it (the Cuthred exemplar and all its descendants) [Lee]

```
We are set upon this Mountain as a lid upon a pot, that what sleeps beneath may sleep.
```

Later copies and commentaries write "the Least," capitalized. The original never does.

## The fells graveside verse @@*
Sung as the coffin lid goes on. The kin call; the answering side sings for the one in the box. One voice holds a low drone under the whole.

```
[call]   Here is the lid, and here is the pot,
[answer]   close it, kin, and keep it so;
[call]   and what lies under, slumber.
[answer]   I lie under, and I slumber.
[call]   Take not the Cold, though it give thee its hand;
[answer]   it wore me as a glove.
[call]   tend thou the hearth, and slumber.
[answer]   and the least of the house take up the brand,
           and kindle me home from under.
```

The fells won't sing the last word of the charge over an open grave: "home," not Hell.

> Let the Prior of this house keep a hand upon what sleeps beneath, and never let it go.

### The Rule, leaf 11: the black art [Lee]

```
That art is black which binds a free will outside the Accords:
  to wear another, or to Accept being worn.
```

### The dusk office at Crossroads (Day 27) -- C. Greenhaven [Lee]
Said first in Celestial "the way a stickler would," then again in Common. A turnip into each bowl at each line of the dedication.

```
Hear me, gods of Heaven who watch over us.
The people of this town have honored your laws, offered up their work and their prayers,
  and ask only that you remember them kindly when weighing mercy and justice in the scales of Fate.
  Bless and guard their loved ones, here and afar, and the innocent beasts displaced,
    who meant no harm.
  Guide them, protect them, and forgive when they go astray meaning well.

For the watchful sky above;
For the bountiful earth below;
For the seas which give us rain;
For the fires which give us warmth;
And for the crafts which teach us to make of these the dutiful and prosperous lives we seek.
```

The Celestial opens: "Hear me, ye gods of Heaven who watch over us all -- / The good citizens of this town..."
