# PLAYTEST - Caria - S12 Gap Log (S12 entries)

Session 12 ran 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-18 in one chat, with one compaction and a day's pause for Lee's weekly limit. In-fiction it covers Day 8 from late morning to night, at the Monastery. There were no fights. It was a session of reading, healing, counsel and politics, and it tested the system's quiet end.

Tags: **OK (Lee)** = ruled at the table by Lee. **@@*** = a GM proposal or reading, not yet ratified. **@@ OPEN** = no answer yet.

## 1. Resolution: think in L1

- **OK (Lee) -- No ladders.** Do not use outcome tables keyed to effect ("effect 1-2 shows X, 3-4 shows Y"). That's Fate or old-school. Go back to an OPPOSITION roll. Accumulate effect as progress, run mutual attrition, and let Harm types carry the results. The S11 habit of "GM truth, revealed by effect" is the same thing; retire it.
- **OK (Lee) -- Reading a damaged text is an attrition contest.** The erasure has a rating and rolls its dice each exchange. The reader's effect accumulates like Harm, and the text is read whole once the total exceeds the rating. Losses cost the reader Complications (eyestrain). This worked well: 18 dice against 4, broken in one pass.
- **OK (Lee) -- Oracle drift.** Everything in L1 should generally be an opposition roll. When the GM feels the need for an oracle, that is the moment to stop and discuss why, and how L1's own model would handle it. It's not a show-stopper. In practice, after the correction, most beats ran from situation and character, with nothing contested and no roll; contested beats got an opposition. -> Worth a short paragraph in the GM guide: "When you reach for a yes/no, ask who or what is resisting."
- **@@* Situation first.** Many "does X happen?" questions answer themselves from the clock, the place and the character (the Prior is at vespers because it's his office). Use that before any roll.

## 2. Dice and Extremes

- **OK (Lee) -- Extremes need 3+ dice.** The GM called a 0 0 on a 2-die Boost an Extreme failure and narrated a fumble; it was retracted. -> Already in extreme-results.md; it's worth repeating in the GM crib.
- **OK (table) -- An opposition's Extreme failure lands on the opposition.** Cari's tea against Teague's cold-burn: the opposition rolled 0 0 0 0, so the borrowed cold lodged in his blisters let go. That was a lovely, unforced result straight from extreme-results.md.
- **OK (table) -- An Extreme success on 6 dice:** a free Token (over the cap), +1 Karma credited toward the Role rolled (Loremaster), and a side effect scaled to 6 dice (a Boost 6 on her next Loremaster roll). The side effect was proposed by the GM and stood uncontested. It lapsed unused at the wrap (see 3).
- **OK (table) -- Extra Effort on a big pool.** With EL14 and +4 Extra Effort (18 dice), the total of 8 was valid, at or under EL. dice.md was read before ruling.

## 3. Boosts

- **OK (Lee) -- A Boost uses the creator's skill.** The fells verse was first given a flat EL3; it was re-ruled to Cari's Loremaster (EL6) against the folk wear on the words.
- **OK (table) -- A free action vs. a Boost action.** A pre-existing scene factor can be rolled free (S11). Once the character spends time building it (teaching Ivro the verse, setting up the raking light), it is a Boost action rolled with her skill.
- **OK (Lee) -- Unwagered Boosts are one-shot.** Lee knowingly didn't wager the Boosts as Persistent, and rolled big with Extra Effort to win in one pass.
- **OK (Lee) -- Boosts are generally ephemeral.** One that goes unused doesn't carry over to a later scene or session, though the GM can always rule an exception. The Extreme's Boost 6 was never used, so it lapsed at the wrap. The GM had carried it forward on the sheet as a "standing" Boost; that was wrong.
- **@@* Scene-factor gear Boost (desk, lens, lamp) at EL2.** A plain failure gives nothing. Fine as is.

## 4. Spellcraft

- **OK (Lee) -- Framework strength for a multi-roll Persistent spell.** The Improvisation hold only tests that the spell doesn't get away before it's cast. The seeking (or targeting) is a separate roll. A Persistent standing effect built that way needs its OWN roll for the strength of the framework: what the Dawn rolls against. Roll it at the casting EL. If it fails, spend a Token. The Scry eye at Maren Tamsett's stone rolled 4. -> spells.md and beyond.md (Scry) should say so.
- **@@ OPEN -- Selective: +1 or -1?** A Selective Fax ("burns only dragons") on an unlit taper, used as a harmless raking light, was counted as useful (+1, DL2), per the S06 Fiat Candela precedent. But in that use the restriction mostly *limits* the spell. traits.md could say which way Selective counts when it makes a spell safer rather than stronger.
- **OK (Lee) -- Which roll is a Persistent effect's strength.** The taper Fax was cast *as* the raking-light Boost, so that Boost roll was its casting roll and its strength is that effect (4). The hold only tested that the spell didn't get away before it was cast. The general rule:
  - When the casting roll produces the effect itself (a light, a ward, a Boost), that roll is the strength.
  - A separate framework roll is needed only when the casting roll is spent on something else, such as Scry's seeking.
  - The taper was dismissed at the wrap, so its strength was moot.
  - The rules text needs a little clarity on this: spells.md, under Persistent and Difficult spells. The GM first proposed the hold result (5) as the strength, which was wrong.
- **@@* Fax Iacta as a raking light.** A flame held low to the leaf raises the furrows of scraped ink, which made the light a Spellweaving Boost to the reading. It's a good, ordinary use of a cantrip that a scriptorium-raised character would know.

## 5. Healing and the Cold

- **OK (table) -- A residue of a spiritual effect in a wound can be contested by mundane care.** Warmth and honest tending (Apothecary or Healer against the residue's rating, as attrition) drove borrowed cold out of Teague's burns and the Prior's frost-mark. No supernatural roll was needed. That reads well: the Saint's charge is literally "tend to the hearths."
- **OK (table) -- See the Root as a diagnosis roll, separate from treatment.** The look (Conjuror vs faintness) answered what was there and what wasn't. Treatment was its own roll. The Sight's Boost to treatment was available but not called.
- **@@* No-poppy pain draught** (willow bark, hemp, mint, maple, small beer) as Apothecary care. Hannet's rule is that every ingredient is inventoried before brewing.

## 6. Tokens, Karma and the Hook

- **OK (table) -- The Hook offered once, plainly, and taken.** At vespers Cari wanted to be inconspicuous, and the Prior named her mercy from his chair. Lee took it for +1 Karma, and the trigger made the scene.
- **OK (table) -- Tokens over the cap persist** without a refresh until they're spent below the cap.

## 7. GM procedure (Lilita's checklist, added to S11's)

- No ladders; think opposition and attrition.
- No Extremes on fewer than 3 dice.
- A Boost uses its creator's skill.
- Flag an oracle before rolling it. Better still, ask who or what resists.
- Name-table results: check the result against the record (the "two fire-witch brothers": Teague was one of them).
- Don't let narration lock in a text the player is designing (the charter's first line). Retcon cleanly in fiction (Ivro recited it from memory).
- Flag player-character slips OOC without correcting the character (Cari said "broken ribs" when her Sight read bruised; she conflated the Watch book with the charter exemplar).
