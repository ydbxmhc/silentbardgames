## What S12 settled (GM ONLY)
- **The Charter Clause** (the original, read whole by raking light). Lee's working copy is filed in the Tenets lore doc:
  - We are set upon this Mountain as a lid upon a pot, that what Lies beneath may slumber.
  - Accept not the Cold which burns the Hand and wear thee as a Glove.
  - Tend to the Hearths that the least of the House take up the Brand to rekindle Hell.
  - The deepest furrow on the leaf is under the lowercase "least."
- **CUTHRED** (Prior, about two hundred years ago @@*) scraped it and wrote the Priors' Watch's first charge in his own hand: "Let the Prior of this house keep a hand upon what sleeps beneath, and never let it go." He made the exemplar himself, the FOUNDERS' BOOK, chained on the bottom shelf of the charter-press, and wrote a lying colophon: "copied fair and whole by mine own hand ... spoilt by damp ... to be kept shut and not read." See the Root on the scraping read a deliberate, pleased, HUNGRY hand with the Cold's taste, generations old: he wanted the line for himself. All of this is strong evidence, not a confession.
- **The fells graveside verse** (oral, folk-worn). Teague has the call half and the WATCHER the answer half. The fells kept "slumber," changed "Hell" to "home," and kept "least" small.
- **The Prior** now has:
  - the clause, with Ivro's seal
  - the colophon copy
  - Cari's counsel: put the true text before the Convocation, and found an OFFICE to keep the Founder's truth and practice, held by the house and not the Prior's administration, an "Inspector General" that must not become an Inquisition, modelled on Rana's Wardens.
  - He said: "Bring me the recommendation. Written." Also: "If you can build it so it doesn't become Voss in a new coat, I'll put my seal to it." HOW the text goes to the Convocation is UNDECIDED "till he's slept."
- **The Prior is clean of the Cold:** no splinter, no keyhole, no downward pull. Only a frost-burn is left.
- **The Prior at vespers** named, from his chair before the whole house, "the one who held the fire off them." That is a public signal of where he stands on the eve of her hearing.
- **Teague** is clean of the cold. He argued about the sugar and lost.
- **Calder** lies awake, watching Asher's light, and hasn't stopped. Hannet: his palms are "cold as a well-stone" through two layers of linen and numbed her fingertips. Nobody touches them bare.
- **Sever and the steward** are in the inner ward, bound, with two of Ruddock's men at the door. Cari told Hannet: "The wounds you see are real."
- **On the road** (Asher's report, Day 8 afternoon): Korrin's train is rolling well. Wenna is counting milestones. Fennick is writing. Kade went to Crossroads at the fork as planned. JEM is carrying an ordinary candle-end in his snuffed tin lantern "so it wouldn't be empty."
