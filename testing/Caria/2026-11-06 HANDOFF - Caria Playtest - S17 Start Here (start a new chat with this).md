## S17 is the microscope session [Lee]
Procedure first, even at some cost to the fun. Every roll that matters gets a short block BEFORE the dice: the question; who or what resists and by which rule; its frame (Level, the Roles and Maneuvers that apply) and pool; her EL, every part justified; the outcome read as under / equal / over its Level only (no ladders); one Token/Hook offer. Size opposition around her EL minus 2, by Context and Drama. Progress and Harm come off the EL on both sides. The opposition takes its own turns. Scenes are called on purpose. Extremes stay in the realm of the roll.

Format [Lee, S16]: end beats on pressure, not atmosphere. Don't prompt. When soap stalls the adventure, offer one OOC cut point as a yes/no with a default (`<cut to ...?>`).
