# GM ONLY - NPC Frames (spoilers) - Caria Playtest

Lee: attach, don't read (unless you want to). Simple stat frames for recurring NPCs. Started 2026-10-11 by Lilita on Lee's go-ahead ("simple frames... exactly game-policy-standard"). Everything here is GM invention (@@*) unless tagged [Lee].

## Policy
- **When:** a recurring NPC gets a frame the first time their numbers would matter at the table, or when they start to recur. Prose is enough until then.
- **What:** Level, the Roles and Maneuvers that define them, Hooks, companions, and an approximate Karma worth. Nothing else.
- **Sizing:** by their place in the world and the story, not by Cari's numbers. Karma worth is the yardstick: Level N costs 2+3+...+N; a Role at rank r costs 1 + (1+2+...+(r-1)); extra Maneuver ranks cost their current rating.
- **Budgets, quick-start style (Lee, 2026-10-11).** Give each NPC a Karma BUDGET, not just a finished frame. The frame is what's been spent; the rest stays unspent. When the NPC has to roll and the fiction calls for something not on the frame, spend from the budget: declare a new Role or Maneuver, or bump one that's already there, within the budget and the usual limits (nothing above Level; Maneuvers not above their Role). Same idea as the Recovery Trope for PCs (CLAUDE.md: start with Karma unspent, allocate retroactively -- "I always had this..."). Never contradict what play has already shown. Log every declaration in the ledger and here.
- **POWER and INFLUENCE [Lee, S17].** An NPC's POWER is their total Karma. Their INFLUENCE is their top EL in the relevant skill, perhaps with the relevant Hook added (the Prior's office is a Hook).
- **Frames obey the caps [Lee, S17]:** Level caps Roles, Roles cap Maneuvers -- no single Maneuver above its Role's rank, and no more Maneuvers than that rank (a rank-3 Role may hold three Maneuvers, each at 3) [Lee, maneuvers.md]. Keep frames LOOSE [Lee]: enough to roll, not a full sheet.
- **Growth:** by the same rules as a PC, from what happens on screen. Follower and companion points follow the 2026-10-11 rulings (addendum): the patron's points bind; the NPC's own points are their own.
- **Reconciling old prose:** keep what play showed; where an old GM note clashes with Lee's sense of a character and wasn't his, Lee's sense wins.
- For scale: Cari was about 31K at Level 4 (2026-10-11). @@ recompute at Level 5.
- **Notation:** `Role r (Maneuver m)`. EL = Level + Role + Maneuver.
