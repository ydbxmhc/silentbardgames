## Rulings and canon added in S17 (details in the register, tagged S17)
- Down means out of the fight -- it CAN mean a coma; still Down after the recovery roll means a real beating. Down things take no actions; their workings persist as things.
- **Table option for this game:** real Injury heals at most 1 a day (spirits too, at the same speed; banished spirits stay gone longer). Not a rules change.
- The Extreme fallback: no obvious benefit -> Boost the next roll by its dice.
- Wagers are pass/fail; a won Wager is reusable dice toward the problem.
- Fatigue may be a Hindrance on her own dice.
- Axe-and-door: nothing resisting, no time pressure -> no roll; the cost is time.
- Opening the Sight is a DL0 roll when warranted.
- Improvisation dice never go on the cast; the Persistent recipe; losing a bound spell ends its Persistent instances unless built as Gear; Gear castings are Anchors.
- Item rating vs effect (the bend lamp: rating 2, flame 1); fuel is mundane; when permanent work happens, the fiction and the GM are the real throttle.
- NPC frames obey the caps (no Maneuver above its Role, no more Maneuvers than the Role's rank); power = total Karma, influence = top EL plus Hook.
- Asher can manifest anywhere (it rarely occurs to him); he can always talk to her, and she can always summon him.
- Setting: the Rule's ward exception; the Accords and other people's debts; the region is small and its distances loose.
