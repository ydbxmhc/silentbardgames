# PLAYTEST - Caria - S15 Gap Log (S15 entries)

Brief notes only. Full text belongs in `L1 playtest rulings register.md`, under the topic named. ASCII only.

- **[RULED] Dismissing a casting at range, unseen** (Day 18, Kade's staff). A caster can let go of her own distant Persistent casting. If she can't see it, the GM may call for a roll: a fail puts out the WRONG one; an Extreme failure might cost the BINDING. Roll only if it matters: "If it's fun, roll it; if it's boring, don't bother." [Lee] -> register sec 11 (Dismissing is not Releasing).
- **[RULED] Applying medicine is not making it** (Day 18, Aldy's balm). Making a medicine from what's at hand needs the Role (Apothecary). Applying it (rubbing in a balm or liniment) is something ANYONE can do, so the user generally gets BASE LEVEL. It's a simple Boost, not the Assist Rule. Caution: the Boost's dice may be enough to allow an Extreme, "as likely bad as good." [Lee] -> register sec 9 (Medicine), beside the dose cap.
- **[RULED] Extremes by impersonal opposition** (Day 20 dawn, Ivro's sickness rolled all ones). "An Extreme should have a significant side effect." The main contest resolves on its margin (a tie still holds, so no Injury from the sickness), but the Extreme lands a side effect beside it: a coughing fit strains a muscle, a HINDRANCE scaled to the dice (3 here). It doesn't touch the sickness rolls, but it keeps him abed or in a chair until it clears. [Lee] -> register sec 2 (answers the open question on impersonal Extremes); harm.md (Hindrance example).
- **[RULED] Recovery when Harm far exceeds Level** (Day 22, the Cold at Harm 9 vs Level 4). Unaided recovery is the slow road: a win cuts Harm only by its effect, and Down lasts until Harm drops below Level. A PC can lie in a coma a long time; a spirit can be effectively banished for years, even centuries. The levers are aid: a Healer's Boosts and Tokens on the recovery roll for a PC; for a spirit, a Conjuror summoning it back, or a GM Token. (Odds note: 4d beats 9d about 1 time in 22.) [Lee] -> register sec 8 (answers the [UNCLEAR] "recovery when Harm exceeds Level").
- **[RULED -- Lee "I like that"] Extreme failure on a Down spirit's recovery roll** (Day 23 dawn, the Cold 4 for 4 blank). Second use of the S13 Merrit precedent: +1 Harm (here 9 -> 10), plus a visible side effect in the world (a frostless dawn) per Lee's S15 "an Extreme should have a significant side effect." Compare harm_recovery_ref "Critical Complications" (Extreme failure on recovery = infection/permanent problem). -> register sec 8 OPEN item; recovery.md should say what an Extreme failure on recovery does.
- **[RULED] A Persistent (residual) Boost keeps ONLY the wagered value** (Day 23, Cari's Rule notes). "Wagers only ever actually retain the wagered value. No matter how well she succeeds, it's pass/fail unless she gets a crit." The plain effect of the roll doesn't add to or tier the Boost. (The GM had tiered the findings by effect; retired.) Also played: (a) the Role-rank cap on wagered points (Loremaster 3); (b) EXTRA EFFORT dice on a wagered roll, with the over-EL failure measured against the EL left AFTER the wager (6, rolling 8d); (c) GM ruling, unchallenged: written notes outlast the scene -- the Boost lasts through the Convocation (actions-and-timing.md lets the GM extend). [Lee] -> wagered-actions.md: one line under Residual Effects saying the named bonus equals the wagered points, no more; and that extra effort counts against the reduced EL.
- **[RULED] Named (Persistent) Boosts stack, swap or sit idle like Roles** (Day 23, Rule Notes +3 and a proposed "Presentable" wager). "They stack when they sensibly each apply. They swap when one makes more sense than another. They sit idle when none are relevant. Same as Roles." [Lee] -> actions-and-timing.md (Boosts With Duration): one sentence. Also played: a Persistent Boost's wager is rolled when the state it names is MADE (dressing at dawn), not hours before.
- **[RULED] Constrained can COST, not pay back** (Day 24, Stone Shape pumping a column drum up through a library sill). spells.md lists Constrained among limiting Traits that pay one back. Lee: when the constraint is PRECISION that demands more effort (only the drum's own stone, touching none of the house), it's a +1, not a -1. Also: Guidable is INHERENT in shaping spells (directing is the shaping); Persistent optional but safer if line of sight will break. [Lee] -> traits.md / spells.md: say a Trait's cost depends on whether it burdens or relieves the working, with this example.
- **[RULED] Stone Shape release REVERTS if it can** (same). The bridge example "crumbles to dust" because it CAN'T go back -- a limit on the spell's power; spikes raised for defence behave the same. Released stone returns to its original shape where possible. [Lee] -> spells.md, Stone Shape: one sentence.
- **[RULED] A new PERMANENT shape is an enchantment** (Day 24, Stone Shape). Holding stone in a new form after the spell lapses needs an ITEM-CREATION roll with a Persistent Trait at least -- definitely nontrivial. A plain Stone Shape released reverts if it can. [Lee] -> spells.md (Stone Shape) and item-creation.md: a line each.
- **[RULED] A True Name read unknowingly** (Day 25, OOC design: a spirit's True Name branded among the charter's signatories). Only someone who KNOWS it is a True Name, or is trained to use one, can USE it. Read aloud without that knowledge -- and pronounced properly -- it only PINGS the spirit: it notices, may flare, and can then hear what follows. The ping is invisible to all but open Second Sight, and even that may miss it unless the name is invoked INTENTIONALLY. A strong standing Contract (here, the founding charter) PROTECTS the named spirit while it is in force. [Lee] -> roles/conjuror.md (True Names): a short paragraph; strong sidebar candidate.
- **[RULED] Ordinary trade tools need no Token** (Day 27, Hob's ear). A Role's everyday kit -- the tools anyone of that trade would carry as a matter of course (a healer's ear-scoop and needle, a scribe's penknife) -- is assumed present: gear is narrative permission. Uncommon or heavy specialist kit (a surgeon's full roll on the road) is not assumed; it needs a Token ("propose a story element") or a source in the fiction. -> what-gear-does.md: one line, perhaps with an example.
- **[CLARIFIED] Equal results: reads vs actions** (Day 27, Hob's ear; GM error caught by Lee). mechanics.md: effect = roll minus opposition, and TIES FAVOR THE DEFENDER. The playtest's "equal = a good read with the finest detail held back" convention applies to INFORMATION rolls (exams, reads, searches, where a tie still yields something partial). For an ACTION against opposition (getting a pea out, a lock, a blow), a tie is effect 0: nothing gets done. -> mechanics.md or resistance-and-opposition.md: one sentence distinguishing how ties play for information vs action, if Lee wants the reads convention in the rules at all.
- **[GM RULING, for Lee to true] Coaching Boost to a base-level act** (Day 27, Jem and the hag-stone). One character can coach another as a Boost action (actions-and-timing.md: the success becomes a bonus to a following action). If the helped act needs a Role, the Assist Rule cap applies (the receiver's rank in that Role; no rank, no Boost). If the act needs no Role -- a base-level act anyone can do, like looking through a hag-stone -- the receiver can use a Boost up to their BASE LEVEL. (Parallels Lee's S15 ruling that applying a liniment works at base level.) -> assist-rule.md or actions-and-timing.md: one line, if it holds.
- **[SETTING CANON + OPEN] A spirit as witness to a vow** (Day 27, the Crossroads shrine). Cari called Asher by True Name to witness a vow before the gods. Does a spirit witness add anything mechanical (an Accord-like weight; a standing Hook; a debt if broken; the spirit's attestation as evidence)? Played fiction-first for now: Asher can attest to it word for word. -> Lee to rule; possible roles/conjuror.md or Accords sidebar.
  - **[Lee, SETTING CANON]** The great Temples (Ganth's, say) keep attendant spirits, usually wind, who carry prayers directly to the greater agents of the gods, if not the gods themselves. A spirit called to witness is therefore not a clerk but a messenger: in principle, spirits are agents of the gods. Mechanics (if any) still open. -> Averond LORE; a Conjuror/priest sidebar candidate.
- **[RULED -- house rule] Advancement: one "New" per session** (S15 wrap). Per session: advance anything the character actually USED, as far as Karma allows, plus ONE "New" purchase. "New" = a Role or Maneuver NOT justified by the session's rolls or at least by relevant story events (Reputation after a public triumph is justified; Athlete for Riding after riding is justified; a sudden rank of Warrior is New). Advancing base Level is ALWAYS the New one. Free Maneuver ranks that come with a Role rank may stack on an existing Maneuver. -> spending-karma.md: a sidebar on the default "one score per session" and this variant.
- **[RULED] Maneuver synergy is earned, not automatic** (S15 wrap; Wards and True Name of Asher). Two Maneuvers can both add to one act when each plausibly contributes (Asher can reasonably add value to the ward); the GM judges it per situation. -> maneuvers.md: one line.
