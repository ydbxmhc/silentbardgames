# The Sweet Spot

#### Not Too Broad, Not Too Narrow

The key to good [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) is **conceptual coherence** - they
should represent a meaningful area of expertise with sensible boundaries.

**Blades** is too broad - straight razors and halberds both have blades, but
nothing in common in how they are used.  
**Broadsword** is too narrow - it excludes sabres, cutlasses, saxes and
battlefield rapiers for no good reason.  
**Swords** is just about right - it leverages the shared principles of edge
work, distance, timing, and technique, and though it won't teach the specific
uses of katana, epee, or khopesh, it could reasonably be used to make any of
those effective.

[Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) *must* be:

- **Specific enough** to create clear situations where they apply
- **Broad enough** to be useful regularly throughout play
- **Thematically consistent** with their [Role](roles.md) and your character concept

[Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) *should* be:

- **Helpful** - don't waste [Maneuver](maneuvers.md) ranks on rare options!
- **Interesting** - they should support character development
- **Clear** - make sure player and GM have the same idea

[Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) *can* be:

- **Simple** - most [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) just add their rank to EL when applicable
- **Tactical** - it's fine for a [Maneuver](maneuvers.md) to have a cost for an extra benefit
- **Creative!** - who says Warriors can't have **Flaming Rage** ?
