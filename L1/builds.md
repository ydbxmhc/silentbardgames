# Example Character Builds

All these use 5 starting [Karma](karma.md).

### Warrior Monk/Nun

**Level 2, Warrior 2**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *I will perfect my swordsmanship; nothing else matters.*  
Single-mindedly focused on being the perfect fighter, she has no time for anything else.  
Rather than taking another [Role](roles.md), she spent her last starting [Karma](karma.md) on another [Maneuver](maneuvers.md).  
Swords +2; Parry +1 (an extra bonus when defending with any weapon.)

### Hand of Mercy

**Level 2, Healer 1, Loremaster(Religion) 1, Adept(Divine) 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *I devote my life and soul to the will of my gods and the care of my fellow man.*  
Devoted to his calling, he will always seek *nonviolent* solutions, and is compelled to help all.  
Healer [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Treat Contagion 1*  
Loremaster [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Medicinal Lore 1*; a faith-based repository of healing knowledge.  
Adept [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Divine Blessing 1* - Once per day per patient may use a Token to trigger a bonus [Recovery](recovery.md), to a maximum number of times/day equal to the [Maneuver](maneuvers.md) rank.

### Holy Templar

**Level 2, Warrior 1, Conjuror 2**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *I will defend the faith against all evils!*  
A warrior zealot, tasked by the gods to seek out evil and smite it, he makes no compromises.  
Warrior [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Shield of Faith 1* - Use of shields  
Conjuror [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md): *Detect Evil 1 (Third Eye), Protection from Evil 1 (Wards)*

### Far-Strider

**Level 1, Warrior 1, Sniper 1, Sneak 1, Naturalist 1, Beast Handler(Falconer) 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Bitter Faeborn Mercenary Hermit*  
The Far-Strider travels the wilds, a superlative hunter/tracker much sought as a scout.  
Warrior [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Quarterstaff 1*; may use to fend.  
Sniper [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Bows 1*  
Sneak [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Woodland Terrain 1*  
Naturalist [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Woodland Terrain 1*  
Beast Handler [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Hunting 1*

### Skirmish Guerilla

**Level 2, Warrior 2, Naturalist 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Pridebound Orcish Exile*  
Life in the wilds has hardened this lone barbarian mercenary in many ways.  
Warrior [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md): *Handaxe 2*  
Naturalist [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): *Mountain Terrain 1*

### Academy Mage

**Level 2, Spellcrafter 2, Conjuror 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Rhianna will notice me when I head the Academy Council!*  
Arcane ambition personified, for the oldest and most noble of reasons: a girl.  
Spellcrafter [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md): Fax Iacta spell; Tanglebones spell  
Conjuror [Maneuvers](maneuvers.md): The true name of a friendly level 1 sylph (air spirit).

### Grifter

**Level 1, Performer 1, Sneak 1, Tinker 1, Athlete 1, Loremaster(Underworld) 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Can't resist easy coin.*  
A charming rounder with a heart of gold…*your* gold, that he just picked from your pocket.  
Performer [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Sleight of Hand 1  
Sneak [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Urban Terrain 1  
Tinker [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Locksmith 1  
Athlete [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Climbing 1  
Loremaster [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Back Alley Shortcuts and Hideaways 1

### Troubadour

**Level 2, Performer 1, Loremaster 1, Adept(Glamours) 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Fame, fortune, and the easy life, in any order, as soon as possible.*  
Travelling inn to tavern, spreading gossip, stories and actual news like seeds in the wind.  
Performer [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Mandolin 1  
Loremaster [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Legendaria 1: knows all the old tales  
Adept [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Glamours 1: allows simple charms and phantasms.

### Baersark

**Level 2, Warrior 1, Beast Handler 1, Adept(Ursanthropy) 1**  
[Hook](hooks.md): *Nominally Controlled Ursanthropy*  
Gifted and Cursed with the aspect of a fierce beast, in spirit and form.  
Warrior [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Wrestling 1  
Beast Handler [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Bear Rider - can train bears as mounts.  
Adept [Maneuver](maneuvers.md): Shapeshift 1: may adopt traits of the bear: claws, sense of smell, etc.
