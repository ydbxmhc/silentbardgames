# Role Synergy

Armor and a sword alone do not a knight make! Planning to joust? Obviously
you'll want ranks with a lance... and some ranks of Athlete(Horseman). Your
opponent is probably adding his skill at riding to his roll, and it might give
him the win even if you're better with your lance.

The [Role](roles.md) explanations below list a few synergy examples, but
players and GMs should negotiate any that reasonably apply. If it makes sense,
do it!
