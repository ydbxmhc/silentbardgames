Core Mechanics

# *"Can I Try Again?"*

When you roll dice, we assume you're already doing your best, so the answer
is: "*It depends.*"

Something needs to change before another attempt makes sense. Find a better
approach, get help from an ally, or discover new information. Create a
[Boost](actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions) - a preparatory action that
improves your position for the next roll. If you have a *better* EL, you can
try again unless the GM thinks you're just metagaming the system (this isn't a
video game.)

Without change, you'd just get the same result. The exception is when the GM
tells you your effect *accumulates*.
