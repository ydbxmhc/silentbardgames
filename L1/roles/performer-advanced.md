# Advanced Applications

Performers are especially good at leveraging [Boosts](../actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions).

Looking to sing for their supper at the local tavern? Before they walk into the place they can ask around for the local gossip to set up a Persistent [Boost](../actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions) by knowing things that will get the crowd's attention. Take a moment to tap pre-existing conditions for free action [Boosts](../actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions) - roll against *Drunk Crowd* and *Popular Song*. Add the fact they often have the opportunity to talk and set up an immediate [Boost](../actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions), guiding the various pieces together with an introduction before playing a song, and then adding [Maneuvers](../maneuvers.md) for both singing and playing, and you end up with a Level 2 minstrel rolling with better than EL10.
