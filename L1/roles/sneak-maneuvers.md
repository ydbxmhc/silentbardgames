# Common Sneak Maneuvers

Sneak [Maneuvers](../maneuvers.html) are anything that makes them a little less conspicuous *somehow*.

### Environmental Specialization

- *Urban Ghost* - City crowds, rooftop routes, architectural blind spots
- *Forest Shadow* - Natural concealment among trees and underbrush
- *Social Camouflage* - the art of being an unremarkable background presence in plain sight

### Tactical Applications

- *Sleight of Hand* - palming, picking pockets, cheating at games
- *Cat's Eye* - detecting hidden things, quiet sounds, or odd behaviors, especially in poor light
- *Distraction* - tossing a pebble to draw attention elsewhere, or timing a move to match the flash of a spinning shiny
