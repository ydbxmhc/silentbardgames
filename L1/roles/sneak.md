# Sneak

*- You never saw me, and you never will.*

The sneak is a master of misdirection who understands that true invisibility isn't about becoming literally unseen — it's about being unnoticed. People see and hear what they expect and ignore what doesn't demand their attention. Through careful timing, positioning, and psychological manipulation, they become part of the background noise of reality, unremarkable until the moment they choose to act.

Sneak is a Role for concealment, infiltration, or covert observation: hiding from enemies, moving through guarded areas, gathering information without being noticed, setting up ambushes, or detecting others attempting the same. Sneaks exploit blind spots both literal and psychological, and understand how to create opportunities. This doesn't mean they're all skulking rogues in dark alleys — a Sneak might be a servant who overhears everything while being treated as furniture, a spy who blends perfectly into any crowd, or a scout who can disappear into terrain so completely that enemies walk past without a second glance. They understand that attention is a limited resource, and avoid claiming any of it. They weaponize the tendency to overlook the mundane.

---

## Common Sneak Maneuvers

Sneak Maneuvers are anything that makes a character less conspicuous somehow:

- *Urban Ghost* — City navigation, rooftop routes, architectural blind spots
- *Forest Shadow* — Natural concealment among trees and undergrowth
- *Social Camouflage* — the art of being an unremarkable background presence in plain sight

### Tactical Applications

- *Sleight of Hand* — palming, picking pockets, cheating at games
- *Cat's Eye* — maintaining hidden status among quiet sounds, especially in poor light
- *Misdirection* — tossing distracting objects to divert attention elsewhere, or timing a move to the flash of a spinning shawl

### Advanced Applications

- *Informational Operations* — Overhearing conversations not meant for outside ears or following targets to learn their routes and contacts
- *Psychological Operations* — Creating paranoia through barely-glimpsed presence, using the fear of being watched to manipulate behavior
- *Support Operations* — Discovering security measures before team infiltration, creating diversions that draw attention away from primary objectives
