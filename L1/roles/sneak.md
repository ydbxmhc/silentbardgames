# Sneak

*- You never saw me, and you never will.*

The Sneak is a master of misdirection who understands that true invisibility isn't about becoming literally unseen - it's about being *unnoticed*. People see and hear what they expect and ignore what doesn't demand attention. Through careful timing, positioning, and psychological manipulation, they become part of the background noise of reality, unremarkable until the moment they choose to act.

Sneak is a [Role](../roles.md) for concealment, infiltration, or undetected observation: hiding from enemies, moving through guarded areas, gathering information without being noticed, setting up ambushes, or detecting others attempting the same. Sneaks exploit blind spots both literal and psychological, and understand how to create opportunities. This doesn't mean they're all skulking rogues in dark alleys - a Sneak might be a servant who overhears everything while being treated as furniture, a spy who blends perfectly into any crowd, or a scout who can disappear into terrain so completely that enemies walk past without a second glance. They understand that attention is a limited resource, and avoid claiming any of it. They weaponize the tendency to overlook the mundane.
