Combat Roles

Sniper

- If I can see you, you're already dead.

Snipers are predators who turn distance into dominance, wielding projectiles with surgical precision from positions of tactical advantage. They understand that the perfect shot isn't about raw accuracy — it's about patience, positioning, and reading the battlefield like a three-dimensional chess board. A sniper knows when to take the shot, when to wait, and when to relocate before the enemy realizes they exist.

Snipers apply roles to any ranged attack beyond melee distance. They excel at finding and utilizing superior firing positions, reading wind and terrain, tracking moving targets, and utilizing the psychological warfare of being an unseen threat. Good commanders use them for reconnaissance, overwatch, and identifying enemy vulnerabilities into tactical security.

This doesn't mean they're helpless up close — most snipers understand that sometimes the enemy gets past their perimeter. While they can't use Sniper Role ranks at melee range, they can still use Maneuver ranks that make sense. Still, their true strength lies in controlling engagement range and making every shot count when it matters most. A sniper's greatest weapon isn't their bow or crossbow — it's their ability to be exactly where their enemy doesn't expect them to be.

Obviously, Snipers get excellent synergy with Sneak, and work well with Warrior and Naturalist.

Common Sniper Maneuvers

Good Maneuvers can be any weapon that works beyond melee range and maintains tactical situations. Exotic weapons like lassos and boomerangs usually require Maneuvers of their own.

Weapon Mastery

  • Bows — includes basic proficiency with all basic bows
  • Crossbows — from small one-hand designs to arbalest
  • Thrown Weapons — balanced knives, spears, hatchets
  • Blowguns — quiet pipe and breath weapons

Tactical Applications

  • Ricochet — reduces Hindrances for trick shots
  • Lockdown — create Hindrances with suppressive fire
  • Spotter — identify enemy Snipers and marked targets
  • Battlefield Tactics — recognizing/utilizing terrain Boosts

Note that Battlefield Tactics makes great Warrior synergy!

Advanced Applications

It's not all about shooting obvious targets.

Reconnaissance and Intelligence

Snipers often serve as the eyes and ears of their groups. Their training in observation and patience makes them natural scouts. They can use their Role for:

  • Spotting distant threats or opportunities
  • Reading movement patterns and numbers
  • Identifying targets in complex situations
  • Coordinating information with allies through smoke arrows, whistlers, and other signals

Psychological Warfare

The threat of an unseen sniper changes battlefield dynamics. Smart snipers leverage this:

  • Forcing enemies into unfavorable positions
  • Creating hesitation and paranoia in enemy forces
  • Supporting allied morale with overwatch
  • Eliminating key targets to disrupt enemy coordination
