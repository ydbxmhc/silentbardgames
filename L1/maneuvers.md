# Maneuvers — Specialization Within Roles

Each rank in any [Role](roles.html) comes with a relevant *Maneuver* - a specific trick that improves the function of the [Role](roles.html) when applicable. Every [Role](roles.html) has some typical examples a character might want to use, but players are welcome to invent new ones and negotiate the details with their group.

## How They Work

When your Maneuver applies to a situation, it typically provides an additional +1 to your EL per rank in the Maneuver. You absolutely can take the same Maneuver more than once to rank it up! You can even buy additional ranks with [Karma](karma.html), exactly like [Roles](roles.html), though you never have to. Maneuvers can never exceed their associated [Role](roles.html) rank, and *in general* a character shouldn't have more Maneuvers than ranks in the associated [Role](roles.html).
