# Examples in Practice

A Warrior with *Swords 2* gains +2 EL when fighting with any sword -
broadsword, rapier, even an unfamiliar katana (though they might use it
differently than intended.) The [Maneuver](maneuvers.md) represents
understanding of blade work that transfers between weapons in the category.

A Performer with *Stage Presence 1* gains +1 EL when commanding attention in
social situations - whether entertaining a crowd, intimidating bandits, or
rallying troops before battle.

[Maneuvers](maneuvers.md) are how you customize a [Role](roles.md) to match
your character concept. Want your knight to specialize in sword-and-board
combat? Take ranks in *Swords* and *Shields*. Prefer a scholarly
warrior-philosopher? Try *Battlefield Tactics* and *Unshakeable Morale*.
