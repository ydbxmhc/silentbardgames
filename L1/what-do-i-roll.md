Core Mechanics

# So…What Do I Roll?

Always include base Level in [Recovery](recovery.md) rolls, but not much else
will count. Being a Warrior doesn't teach you to heal faster! What *does*
count is [Boosts](actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions) from Healers providing
care, but *those do not stack*. Additional Healers may try to
[Boost](actions-and-timing.md#boost-actions) their senior member so that
*their* roll is better, but only one bonus may be applied for medical care
(though other rolls might usefully reduce relevant situational
[Hindrances](types-of-harm.md) .)

[Recovery](recovery.md) rolls are expected to include the penalties of accumulated *[Injury](types-of-harm.md)*.

GMs who want a harsher game can include active
[Complications](types-of-harm.md) . For a lighter game, don't include Harm
penalties in recovery rolls at all. Including unclassified Harm in the rolls
to determine how much was [Injury](types-of-harm.md) will make a game grimmer
and deadlier, while leaving it out is more cinematic. If adding a second roll
for armor, they don't *have* to use the same sets of modifiers…
