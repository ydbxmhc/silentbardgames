# Mechanical Function

Every [Role](roles.html) provides two things: **Narrative Permission**, and **Improved Effectiveness**.

Some things need a [Role](roles.html) to even attempt. A Loremaster might recall obscure historical details; others are limited to common knowledge. *Only* a Spellweaver can learn and cast spells.

When your [Role](roles.html) applies to a situation, add its rank to your EL. This makes you significantly better at the things your character does. Anyone can swing a chair, but a Warrior adds all their [Role](roles.html) ranks, and has a few other tricks as well.

Within a [Role](roles.html) you can learn relevant skills to improve your use of [Role](roles.html)-specific behaviors. Warriors can learn to use swords effectively; everyone else is just faking it.
