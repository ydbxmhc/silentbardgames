Running the Game

Hooks In Play

Hooks should be a large part of the game. They should develop over time, shrink as PCs overcome their fears and addictions, change as they move in new directions, grow as they get more invested in their commitments.

Character Hooks should change over time. One way this happens is when the basic behavior develops specific cases when it's stronger. That's not separate Hooks, just degrees of applicability.
