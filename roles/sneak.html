Social Roles

Sneak

— You never saw me, and you never will.

The sneak is a master of misdirection who understands that true invisibility isn't about becoming transparent — it's about becoming unremarkable. People see and hear what they expect to see and hear; they ignore the background noise. The Sneak weaponizes this tendency to overlook the mundane, blending into background noise until the moment they choose to act.

Sneak is a Role for infiltration, concealment, observation, and avoiding detection. They exploit blind spots — literal and psychological — and move through spaces and crowds without leaving a trace in people's memories. A Sneak might be a spy who maintains a cover identity, a servant ignored by the powerful, a thief who has never been caught, or a scout who vanishes into terrain so completely that enemies pass within arm's reach.

The Role applies to any situation where staying unnoticed matters: moving silently, hiding, tailing someone, passing unseen through spaces, avoiding surveillance, or manipulating attention away from yourself.

Common Sneak Maneuvers

Sneak Maneuvers are anything that makes a character less conspicuous somehow:

  • Urban Ghosts — City navigation, alley routes, architectural blind spots
  • Forest Camouflage — Natural concealment among trees and undergrowth
  • Social Camouflage — The art of being an unremarkable background presence in plain sight

Tactical Applications

  • Sleight of Hand — Palming, picking pockets, swapping objects at close range
  • Cat's Eye — Maintaining hidden status among quiet sounds, especially in poor light
  • Misdirection — Tossing distracting objects to divert attention elsewhere, timing a move to flash of a spinning shawl

Advanced Applications

  • Informational — Overhearing conversations not meant for outside ears or following targets
  • Psychological Operations — Creating paranoia through barely-glimpsed presence, using the fear of being watched
  • Support Operations — Discovering security measures before main infiltration, creating diversions from primary objectives
