Knowledge and Craft Roles

Healer

— Drink this. We'll see how you are in the morning.

Healers guide patients back toward health with careful intervention. They diagnose problems, treat injuries, manage diseases, and understand the human body thoroughly enough to know what it needs to repair itself. Every Healer understands that sometimes the best medical care is getting out of the body's way, and sometimes it's aggressive intervention — and the skill is knowing which is needed.

Every Healer understands the fundamentals of treating trauma — the basic cuts and fractures that require immediate attention. They aren't limited to bandages and bedside manner — they could be military medics performing battlefield surgery under fire, plague doctors investigating disease outbreaks, poison experts, or assassins who've learned anatomy for less charitable reasons.

Recovery and Treatment

Healer ranks add to recovery rolls for all patients under their care. Once per patient per roll, a Healer Boost can speed recovery significantly — but those do not stack. Additional Healers may try to Boost their senior member so that their roll is better, but only one bonus may be applied for medical care (though other roles might usefully reduce relevant situational Hindrances.)

Common Healer Maneuvers

Treatment Specializations

  • Trauma Medicine — Emergency procedures, wound treatment, triage
  • Apothecary — Preparation of medicines and understanding their application
  • Diagnosis — Reading symptoms and identifying less obvious problems
  • Surgery — Specialized care for patients requiring extreme and dangerous procedures

Specialized Applications

  • Plague Medicine — Specialized disease treatment and quarantine procedures
  • Burns — Specialized care for people subjected to extreme heat
  • Social Care — Bedside manner, managing patient psychology, emotional trauma
