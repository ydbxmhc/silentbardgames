Combat Roles

Warrior

— I am brave, strong, and ready to fight.

The warrior role is about physical conflict, dealing out harmful force (or the threat of it) with precision and using their training to survive the ire of others. They can effectively use any common or reasonable improvised weapon effectively; a warrior can make a broken stem hurt you. The Role also applies to feats of intimidation, strategy, and throwing anything handy at short range.

This doesn't mean they instinctively know the specialized techniques for exotic weapons like a khopesh or kusarigama — that requires specific techniques such as Maneuvers. The Warrior Role is a brawler; it represents someone who understands the fundamentals of violence: timing, distance, leverage, and the will to act decisively when it matters.

Common Warrior Maneuvers

Warriors will specialize their combat expertise in different ways. Remember, these are examples — each represents a specific expertise that transfers between related situations.

Weapon Categories

  • Brawling — special training for unarmed fights, binding, measure, and tempo with any bladed weapon
  • Swords — Edge work, binding, measure, and tempo with any bladed weapon
  • Shields — Group tactics, bash techniques, and weapon integration with any shield
  • Polearms — Extended reach, bash techniques, and weapon integration with any shield

Combat Techniques

  • Battlefield Awareness — Read combat flow, assess threats and opportunities
  • Combat Reflexes — Improved reaction to sudden attacks and ambushes
  • Dirty Fighting — Exploit any openings: eye gouges, groin shots, thrown sand
  • Armed Combat — Ways to leverage advantages against more experienced combatants
