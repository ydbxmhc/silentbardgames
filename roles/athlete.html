Outdoorsman Roles

Athlete

— Faster, stronger, higher… better.

The Athlete transforms their body into a precision instrument, pushing physical capability to amazing heights through discipline and relentless practice. They move with the mastery of a virtuoso and master feats of physical excellence: running, swimming, climbing, lifting, throwing, and maintaining peak performance under challenge.

They aren't combatants — boxing and wrestling are Warrior territory, as are pitching and hitting. Athletes focus on doing it better — running faster or further, jumping higher or farther, swimming treacherous waters, scaling challenging terrain, and maintaining performance under conditions that would crush others. They train to maximize their physical limits, not to inflict harm.

Need to fold a gate? Bend those bars? Scale a cliff in record time? Trust an Athlete.

Common Athlete Maneuvers

The best Athletes make physical excellence a way of life. They find ways to achieve more through intelligent application of their training.

Movement Specializations

  • Sprinting — Explosive speed, maximum velocity, efficient running form
  • Endurance Running — Energy conservation, maintaining pace, tireless endurance
  • Swimming — Stroke efficiency, breathing control, aquatic movement in all conditions
  • Climbing — Technical ascent, reading rock faces, body positioning on vertical terrain

Strength and Agility

  • Powerlifting — Maximum strength application for lifting, breaking, and pushing
  • Mounted Sports — Riding excellence specific for sport competition
  • Competitive Strategy — Reading opponents, adapting tactics in real time
