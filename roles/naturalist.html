Outdoorsman Roles

Naturalist

— You say "Wilderness" like it's a bad thing.

The naturalist understands that "civilization" is just a thin veneer painted over the fundamental forces that have always governed survival: weather, seasons, predator and prey, growth and decay. They read the stories written in animal tracks, growth patterns, and cloud formations.

Naturalists are good at wilderness survival: finding food and shelter, predicting weather, tracking creatures, and generally living off the terrain. They might be urban scavengers who know where day-old bread ends up, a ship's navigator reading weather and stars, or a scout tracking enemy troop movements.

Survival Mechanics

Add Naturalist ranks to daily rolls for providing food and shelter. Success provides for people equal to the effect generated. Without proper food and shelter, characters cannot make recovery rolls and must resist privation daily or suffer escalating fatigue as Harm.

Common Naturalist Maneuvers

Terrain Specialization

  • Forest Dweller — Woodland navigation, tree identification, forest creature behavior
  • Mountain Guide — Altitude adaptation, avalanche prediction, cliff navigation techniques
  • Desert Walker — Water conservation, heat management, reading sand and stone
  • Urban Scavenger — Food sources, sleeping spots, avoiding watch patrols and street predators

Specialized Knowledge

  • Weather Sense — Predicting wind, rain, snow, and storms
  • Tracker — Reading and identifying signs of passage
  • Herbalist — Plant identification for edible, medicinal, and poisonous properties
  • Beast Lore — Animal behavior, territorial patterns, predator-prey relationships

Production Skills

  • Agriculture — Managing crops and orchards, composting, saving seed
  • Food Preservation — Smoking, salting, drying techniques for long-term storage
  • Shelter Construction — Building techniques using available materials and tools
  • Fire Mastery — Starting fires even in wet conditions, producing smokeless flames
