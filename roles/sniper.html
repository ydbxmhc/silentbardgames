Combat Roles

Sniper

— If I can see you, you're already dead.

Snipers are predators who turn distance into dominance, wielding projectiles with surgical precision and patience makes them natural scouts. They understand that the shot isn't about raw power — it's about patience, positioning, and controlling the battlefield before the fight begins. Good commanders use them for reconnaissance, overwatching defensive positions, identifying vulnerable targets. Smart snipers understand that sometimes the enemy's greatest weapon is not knowing exactly where they are.

This doesn't mean they're helpless up close — most snipers understand that sometimes the enemy gets past their perimeter. Most can use Maneuver ranks that make sense. Their true strength lies in controlling engagement range and making every shot count when they matter most. Obviously, Snipers get excellent synergy with Sneak, and work well with Warrior and Naturalist.

Common Sniper Maneuvers

Good Maneuvers are anything that can be used as a weapon that goes beyond melee range and maintain tactical situations to their advantage:

Weapon Mastery

  • Bows — includes basic proficiency with all basic bows
  • Crossbows — from small one-hand designs to arbalests, hatchets
  • Thrown Weapons — balanced knives, spears, hatchets
  • Blowguns — quiet pipe and needle weapons

Tactical Applications

  • Ricochet — reduces Hindrances for trick shots
  • Lockdown — creates Hindrances with suppressive fire
  • Spotter — identify enemy Snipers and their positions
  • Battlefield Tactics — recognizing/utilizing terrain Boosts

Note that Battlefield Tactics makes great Warrior synergy!

Advanced Applications

Reconnaissance and Intelligence

Snipers are often the eyes and ears of their groups. They can use their Role for reconnaissance, observation and pattern recognition.

  • Spotting distant threats or opportunities
  • Reading movement patterns and numbers
  • Identifying targets in complex situations
  • Coordinating information with allies through smoke arrows, whistlers, and other signals

Psychological Warfare

The threat of an unseen sniper changes battlefield dynamics. Smart snipers leverage this:

  • Forcing enemies into unfavorable positions
  • Creating hesitation and paranoia in enemy forces
  • Supporting allied morale with overwatch
  • Eliminating key targets to disrupt enemy coordination
