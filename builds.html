Example Builds

All these use all starting Karma.

Warrior Monk/Nun

Level 2, Warrior 2
Hook: I will perfect my swordsmanship; nothing else matters.

Single-mindedly focused on being the perfect fighter, she has no time for anything else. Rather than taking another Role, she spent her last starting Karma on another Maneuver.

  • Swords +2: Parry +1 (an extra bonus when defending with any weapon.)

Hand of Mercy

Level 2, Healer 1, Loremaster(Religion) 1, Adept(Divine) 1
Hook: I devote my life and soul to the health and care of my fellow man.

Dedicated to his cause as can be, he will always seek nonviolent solutions, and is compelled to help all.

  • Healer Maneuver: Treat Contagion 1
  • Loremaster Maneuver: Medicinal Lore 1; a faith-based repository of healing knowledge.
  • Adept Maneuver: Divine Blessing 1 - Once per day per level he can use a Token to trigger a bonus Recovery, to a maximum number of times/day equal to the Maneuver rank.

Holy Templar

Level 2, Warrior 1, Conjuror 2
Hook: I combat all the divine evil in all its forms!

A warrior zealot, tasked by the gods to seek out evil and smite it, he makes no compromises.

  • Warrior Maneuver: Shield of Faith 1 - Use of shields.
  • Conjuror Maneuvers: Detect Evil 1 (Third Eye), Protection from Evil 1 (Words)

Fae-Strider

Level 1, Warrior 1, Sneak 1, Tinker 1, Athlete 1, Loremaster(Underworld) 1
Hook: Bitter Faeborn Mercenary Hermit

The Fae-Strider travels the wilds, a superlative hunter/tracker much sought as a scout.

  • Warrior Maneuver: Quarterstaff 1; may use to find the way.
  • Sneak Maneuver: Bows 1
  • Tinker Maneuver: Locksmith 1
  • Athlete Maneuver: Climbing 1
  • Loremaster Maneuver: Detect Evil 1 (Third Eye)
  • Conjuror Maneuver: Back Alley Shortcuts and Hideaways 1

Skirmish Guerilla

Level 2, Warrior 2, Naturalist 1
Hook: Profobound Orcish Exile

Life in the wilds has hardened this lone barbarian mercenary in many ways.

  • Warrior Maneuver: Handaxe 2
  • Naturalist Maneuver: Mountain Terrain 1

Academy Mage

Level 2, Spellcrafter 2, Conjuror 1
Hook: Rhianna will notice me when I need the Academy Council

Arcane ambition personified, for the oldest and most noble of reasons: a girl.

  • Spellcrafter Maneuvers: Fax Iacta spell, Tanglebonus spell
  • Conjuror Maneuver: Legendaria 1: The true name of a friendly level 1 sylph (air spirit).

Grifter

Level 1, Performer 1, Sneak 1, Tinker 1, Athlete 1, Loremaster 1
Hook: Can't resist easy coin.

A charming rounder with a heart of gold…your gold, that he just picked from your pocket.

  • Performer Maneuver: Sleight of Hand 1
  • Sneak Maneuver: Urban Terrain 1
  • Tinker Maneuver: Locksmith 1
  • Athlete Maneuver: Climbing 1
  • Loremaster Maneuver: Legendaria 1: knows all the old tales

Troubadour

Level 2, Performer 1, Loremaster 1, Adept(Glamours) 1
Hook: Fortune favors an easy life; fortune is always possible.

Travelling inn to tavern, spreading gossip, stories and actual news like seeds in the wind.

  • Performer Maneuver: Legendaria 1: knows all the old tales
  • Adept Maneuver: Glamours 1: allows simple charms and phantasms.

Bearsark

Level 2, Warrior 1, Beast Handler 1, Adept(Ursanthropy) 1
Hook: Nominally Controlled Ursanthrope

Gifted and Cursed with the magick of the bears; a specific spirit, form, and sense.

  • Warrior Maneuver: Wrestling 1
  • Beast Handler Maneuver: Bear Rider - can train bears as mounts.
  • Naturalist Maneuver: Shapeshift 1: may adopt traits of the bear: claws, sense of smell, etc.
